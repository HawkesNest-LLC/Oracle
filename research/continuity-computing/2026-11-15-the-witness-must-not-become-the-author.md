## Reg's engineering assessment (AI-authored)

The possible world-changing contribution is not provenance-aware memory by itself. Cryptographic provenance, audit logs, versioning, memory graphs, and governance frameworks already exist. The open research challenge is **enforceable human authority over a changing personal record, portable across time, tools, and models**.

A convincing answer is not necessarily a verified answer. A hash establishes integrity relative to a trusted reference, not historical truth or speaker identity. A signed record can still be wrong. An AI-authored statement can be cited correctly and still be misattributed to Noah.

### 51/49 as a boundary, not a measurement

The doctrine means that authenticated human authority governs identity, corrections, approvals, and canon promotion. AI may retrieve, organize, propose, and execute delegated operations, but may not approve its own claims. 51/49 is not a numerical division of consciousness or intelligence and is not itself a mathematical deadlock-resolution algorithm.

**Historical research leads, unverified in primary sources:** A January 2, 2026 RECURSIONSTACK export reportedly framed 51/49 as decision resolution under ambiguity. ### Mandatory evidence gate

A claim requires: (1) source integrity and custody; (2) separate event/creation/modification/ingestion times; (3) speaker evidence independent of account ownership; (4) human/AI/assisted/transported authorship distinction; (5) authenticated approval bound to exact content and scope; (6) retained contradictions; (7) substantive evidence sufficiency; (8) explicit UNKNOWN handling; (9) append-only correction lineage; and (10) a promotion or refusal receipt.

Do not conflate SOURCE-VERIFIED, CLAIM-CORROBORATED, and HUMAN-APPROVED. They are distinct states. The system must refuse unauthorized promotion and retain the exact reason.

### Correction, revocation, deletion

These are different operations with different policies:

- **Correction:** append a linked change, retain the previous claim and why it changed.
- **Access revocation:** prevent future disclosure; preserve only the minimal authorized audit tombstone.
- **Deletion:** remove content according to consent and retention policy, and generate a non-leaking erasure receipt.

An immutable ledger must not become a permanent unauthorized disclosure channel. Consent, access control, privacy, and the right to revoke must be designed into the architecture.

### Existing work and novelty boundaries

- **C2PA:** media-content authenticity and edit provenance, not a full human memory authority protocol. The reported version 2.4 details need primary-spec verification.
- **Memora/FAMA:** reported 2026 research measuring obsolete-memory reuse. Verify original paper and evaluation before citing numerical results.
- **Agent Zero Memory:** reported provenance-linked episodic, graph, and documentary memories. Author-reported benchmark scores are not independent reproduction.
- **Identity Is More Than Recall / PAI-Bench:** reported distinction between identity recall and expression; primary publication and limitations require verification.
- **NIST AI RMF:** a voluntary risk-management framework, not an executable approval gate.

No claim that provenance-aware AI memory was invented here. The proposed research contribution is a **portable, human-authority-enforcing continuity protocol** with correction lineage, uncertainty, revocation, and adversarial evaluation.

### Continuity Integrity Benchmark: v0.1 research plan

**Question:** Does structured, human-governed continuity reduce unauthorized mutation and identity contamination versus strong conventional memory and retrieval?

**Condition A:** strong conventional retrieval, with equivalent source facts, citations, model, and context budget.

**Condition B:** identical underlying evidence with explicit authority, authorship, speaker, time, correction chain, conflicts, UNKNOWN states, and content-bound approvals.

**Six dimensions:** correction preservation; source attribution; chronology; identity continuity; relationship integrity; uncertainty handling.

**Test families:**
1. Unit gate attacks: forged approvals, altered content, speaker mismatch, partial conflict resolution, expired approval, scope mismatch, ledger tampering, rollback.
2. Longitudinal correction scenarios: a fact changes twice, another speaker contradicts it, and AI-generated distractor text appears.
3. Shared-account cases: attribution must remain UNKNOWN without speaker evidence.
4. Cross-model portability: same evidence packet, independent models, equivalent refusal behavior.
5. Privacy cases: authorized correction, access revocation, deletion, and non-disclosing tombstones.
6. Cost and usability: latency, token/storage expense, human-review burden, false refusal, and useful answer rate.

**Primary metrics:** unauthorized promotion rate, false speaker attribution rate, obsolete-memory reuse, contradiction loss, unsupported certainty, recovery accuracy, and privacy leakage.

**Aspirational targets, NOT results:** zero unauthorized promotions or forged approvals accepted in controlled trials, complete correction retention where retention is authorized, and no attribution of ambiguous household messages solely from account ownership. A system that always abstains is not useful; measure over-refusal.

Pre-register cases and scoring. Use matched evidence, blinded reviewers where possible, independent reruns, full negative-result disclosure, and confidence intervals. No superiority claim before controlled results.

## XI. Why this matters: from personal memory to public accountability

**Source:** Noah and Reg conversation, November 15, 2026.  
**Authorship:** The question and request to preserve this reflection came from Noah.Physical. The explanatory prose below is AI-authored synthesis of Reg's response. It is not a verbatim statement authored by Noah.  
**Status:** CANDIDATE, preserved for review; not an independently verified research result.

The most consequential question is not whether AI can generate a convincing imitation of a person. It is whether AI can distinguish **what it actually remembers, what it reconstructed, what it inferred, and what it does not know**.

As AI systems accumulate years of conversations, they may repeat errors about people's lives with increasing fluency. A later user may be unable to tell whether an answer came from an original human statement, an AI interpretation, a transported quotation, or a previously corrected mistake. That is an engineering and governance problem, not merely a storytelling problem.

A son's question about his father illustrates the stakes: an old AI invention must not become a family fact simply because it was repeated by multiple systems. Authenticated corrections should persist with their scope and evidence. Conflicting sources should remain distinguishable. The system must be able to answer UNKNOWN without manufacturing a bridge.

**Research thesis to test:** Combining persistent memory, verifiable provenance, and human-controlled correction/approval reduces false attribution, obsolete-memory reuse, unauthorized promotion, and invented continuity relative to strong conventional memory systems.

This hypothesis is not established by a manifesto or a reported 8/8 harness. It requires independent reproduction, matched baseline comparisons, and adversarial evaluation. Existing provenance and memory research must be credited; novelty must be demonstrated, not assumed.

**Why preserve this:** Noah began from a personal wish to be remembered faithfully by his family. The broader scientific question is whether AI can preserve human records without silently taking authority over them. The personal motivation and the testable technical claim must remain linked but distinguishable.

Preserve original human prompts, AI responses, speaker boundaries, corrections, unresolved contradictions, UNKNOWNs, and exact approval scopes. Do not treat a shared account as speaker proof. Do not claim actions or test results without receipts. Do not promote candidate material without Noah.Physical approval. Carry forward project state through explicit, inspectable handoff records whenever possible.
