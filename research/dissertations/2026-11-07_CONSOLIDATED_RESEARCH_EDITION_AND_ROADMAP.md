## Abstract

Rendered Reality investigates how computational systems can preserve a person's documented history, decisions, corrections, relationships, and changing judgments without presenting generated reconstruction as original testimony. LEGACY.GI provides a generational continuity research framework; SOV1 supplies human authority and canon-governance boundaries; ORACLE is the proposed and partially prototyped local witness and retrieval runtime. Continuity Computing is the broader engineering discipline of provenance-aware, temporally accountable computation. Cognitive World Projection and the holodeck / living-world program explore interfaces and simulations grounded in that evidence. PCCE is a proposed blinded evaluation of whether structured continuity traces improve predictions of historical decisions over strong baselines. No claim here establishes consciousness transfer, digital survival, universal novelty, patent grant, deployed production software, or validated predictive superiority.

## 2. Consolidated research thesis

**Research question:** Can a provenance-governed, temporally versioned record of human sources, corrections, decisions, and permissions support more faithful, auditable long-horizon AI behavior than ordinary retrieval and ungoverned narrative memory?

**Working hypothesis:** Explicit lineage, authority gates, and bitemporal evidence improve verifiability, correction fidelity, and historical decision prediction under controlled evaluation.

**Nonclaims:** A convincing digital persona is not proof of identity equivalence. Predictive success is not consciousness. Cryptographic hashes prove integrity relative to a trusted anchor, not truth of original claims. Theoretical physics analogies are not physical laws.

## 3. Integrated dissertation structure

1. **Human purpose and ethical origin:** Why witness records matter; human baseline is private, use redacted evidence only.
2. **Problem definition and prior art:** Context limits, retrieval, event sourcing, knowledge graphs, digital twins, temporal databases, provenance, memory agents, identity philosophy; avoid calling all AI stateless.
3. **Historical evolution (2025-2026):** Early LEGACY.GI white paper, May manuscript, June Flamekeeper, v~300, v~301, ORACLE merge, July CWP, August baseline, November tested reference implementation; dates and authorship independently checked.
4. **LEGACY.GI conceptual stack:** Memory Anchor Layer (MAL), Emotional Execution Loop (EEL), recursive identity stack, temporal mapping, sovereignty. Mark emotional fidelity and post-biological continuity as hypotheses, not demonstrated capabilities.
5. **RECURSIONSTACK:** MIRRORLINE, LEGACY.GI, HYDRA.STACK historical roles; map historical variants before normalization.
6. **ORACLE witness runtime:** source ingestion, evidence objects, immutable history or append-only events, retrieval, provenance, corrections, confidence, commitment recovery, reproducible receipts.
7. **SOV1 governance:** Human authorization, explicit promotion, versioned policy, access control, private/public partition, consent, auditability.
8. **Continuity Computing:** source records, claim records, correction records, decisions, experiments; bitemporal available-at vs event-at; cross-system handoff and unknown preservation.
9. **Cognitive World Projection:** distinguish theoretical cognitive projection from implemented UI; spatial knowledge interfaces and interactive visual reasoning.
10. **Rendered Reality / living worlds:** separate factual archives, fictional canon, world state, simulation events, and rendering. Do not fabricate a person's memories; label generated content. A multi-user holodeck requires identity permissions and isolation.
11. **PCCE evaluation:** blinded, preregistered, temporally masked prediction with four arms and independently checked outcomes.
12. **Security, ethics, and legal:** threat models, poisoning, forgery, family privacy, consent, deletion/retention policy, IP and patent review, model limits.
13. **Results and negative findings:** local execution receipts separate from proposed features; publish failures and blocked tests.
14. **Limitations and future work:** simulation vs original, memory incompleteness, unresolved personhood claims, deployment, long-term maintenance, reproducibility.

## 4. Reference architecture and data contract

**Five layers:** (1) source records; (2) continuity facts and authority; (3) world and identity models; (4) simulation / decision execution; (5) rendering and interaction.

**Minimum evidence fields:**
- `source_id`, `uri`, `content_digest`, `ingested_at`, `source_created_at` (nullable), `source_modified_at` (nullable), `observed_available_at`, `author_claim`, `author_evidence`, `visibility`, `source_type`.
- `claim_id`, `claim_text`, `source_spans`, `status`, `recorded_at`, `valid_from`, `valid_to`, `supersedes`, `branch`, `reviewer`.
- `decision_id`, `actor`, `authority_basis`, `proposal_id`, `approval_receipt`, `effective_at`, `reversal_of`.
- `experiment_id`, `protocol_version`, `cutoff`, `source_manifest_digest`, `model_version`, `prompt_digest`, `prediction_commitment`, `outcome_receipt`.

**Status vocabulary:** VERIFIED, REPRODUCED, INFERRED, PROPOSED, UNKNOWN, DISPROVEN, RETRACTED. Status does not replace source links.

**Core invariants:** no candidate canon promotion without human authorization; no claim without source locator or explicit UNKNOWN; no silent overwrite of correction history; no future evidence in past decision predictions; no private material in public output without explicit approval; no simulated event represented as historical fact.

## 5. Full development roadmap with acceptance gates

### Phase 0: Archive custody and public/private separation
- Enumerate Drive, Gmail, GitHub, local and Library source families.
- Assign stable source IDs, ingestion times, cryptographic digests, permissions and redaction classes.
- Detect duplicates by hash and near-duplicate text; retain original variants.
- **Exit:** machine-readable manifest with exact locators and retrieval receipts; sensitive sources excluded from public commits.

### Phase 1: Dissertation critical edition
- Compare v~300 vs v~301 and May 2025 dissertation; produce section-by-section diff.
- Reconcile LEGACY.GI definitions and historical architecture names.
- Attribute passages as Noah original, AI-generated, mixed, or UNKNOWN; preserve the raw records.
- Build citations for every substantive historical claim.
- **Exit:** chapter-level source concordance and redacted critical edition approved by Noah.

### Phase 2: Evidence kernel
- Implement source/claim/correction/decision/experiment schema and migrations.
- Immutable event history, content digests, versioned provenance and retention policies.
- Explicit authority gate and conflict/UNKNOWN handling.
- **Exit:** reproducible automated tests for restart persistence, unauthorized promotion rejection, supersession and retrieval; document limitations.

### Phase 3: Temporal truth gate
- Separate event time, recording time, ingestion time, and earliest independently evidenced availability.
- Block post-cutoff versions and hindsight leakage.
- Seal evaluation packets with digests before outcome access.
- **Exit:** adversarial tests including backdated files, retroactive edits, copied summaries, and contaminated metadata.

### Phase 4: PCCE scientific protocol
- Preregister hypothesis, scoring, stopping rules, human review, and negative results.
- Four arms: strong RAG, static biography, automatic Thought Atlas, provenance-governed Thought Atlas.
- Build 20 *candidate* episodes; exclude five exposed examples from blind testing.
- Keep outcome vault separate and require independent verification of pre-decision sources.
- **Exit:** sufficient truly held-out episodes and blinded, reproducible evaluation. Fewer valid episodes are preferable to contaminated samples.

### Phase 5: ORACLE integration
- Map schema onto existing CC-90-001 prototype, avoiding parallel incompatible implementations.
- Connectors ingest read-only source evidence with consent; produce receipts for writes.
- Secure local API, authentication, authorization, backups, recovery and export.
- **Exit:** documented end-to-end local integration tests, not just an architectural diagram.

### Phase 6: Human-facing Thought Atlas
- Timeline, branching corrections, confidence and provenance explorer.
- Every generated narrative links to source passages; allow correction without erasure.
- Human approval UI for canon, sensitive information and public release.
- **Exit:** user can inspect why a claim exists, correct it, and recover its previous versions.

### Phase 7: Rendered Reality / living-world prototype
- Model fictional canon separately from historical evidence and private human records.
- Persistent world state and NPC history; label generated events; prevent retroactive canon drift.
- Begin with one bounded Jupiter Station environment; expand only after restart and provenance tests.
- **Exit:** persistent scenario can be resumed and audited after restart; no claim of actual holodeck or independent consciousness.

### Phase 8: Multi-user and XR
- Permissions and consent for family/guest participation, revocation and data separation.
- Voice, spatial interface and VR are optional presentation layers over the same evidence kernel.
- **Exit:** privacy/security review and verified multi-user test.

### Phase 9: Independent validation and publication
- Replicate PCCE with independent reviewers, alternative baselines, adversarial red team and negative results.
- Review patent disclosures with counsel before publishing confidential material.
- Release public-safe dissertation, source manifest, reproducible test harness and limitations.
- **Exit:** independently reproducible findings; explicit separation of research, prototype and speculation.

### Phase 10: Long-horizon stewardship
- Versioned export formats, migration tests, key custody, human succession/permissions, archive integrity checks.
- Track unresolved claims and retractions across model/vendor changes.
- **Exit:** a different authorized operator can restore the archive and audit its history without relying on any one model's memory.
