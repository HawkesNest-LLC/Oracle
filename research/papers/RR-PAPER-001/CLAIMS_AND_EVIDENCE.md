## Evidence classification contract
- **INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED:** directly checked public scholarly source or current file read-back. Does not mean an empirical claim was independently replicated.
- **DIRECTLY INSPECTED DOCUMENTARY EVIDENCE:** source content was read, but its assertions were not independently rerun.
- **REPORTED EXPERIMENTAL RESULT:** execution described by a record or continuity transfer without independent reproduction here.
- **PROPOSED METHODOLOGY:** experimental design not executed.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** falsifiable predicted effect, not a result.
- **UNKNOWN:** missing or contradictory evidence.
