## Classification contract

- **INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED:** a public document or artifact was fetched and inspected in this research session. This class establishes the observed contents, not independent replication of a claim inside them.

- **PROPOSED METHODOLOGY:** a future test is specified but not executed.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** a falsifiable prediction requiring evaluation.
- **UNKNOWN:** presently inaccessible, contradictory, or unestablished.
