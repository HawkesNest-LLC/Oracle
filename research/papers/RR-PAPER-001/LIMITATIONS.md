# RR-PAPER-001: Limitations, nonclaims, and open questions

**Date:** 2026-11-25  
**Status:** Public-safe scientific working paper, not peer reviewed.

## Source coverage and provenance

The inspected corpus includes selected connected Drive documents, historical research manuscripts and public repository files. It is **not** an exhaustive export of every historical ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Codex, Copilot, local ORACLE or other session. Some documents contain AI-generated summaries, composite authorship, or imported files with dates that do not authenticate original authorship. An identical quotation repeated by several agents is still one original evidentiary lineage until genuinely independent sources are found. A file's checksum establishes integrity relative to observed bytes, not who first wrote them or whether the claim is true.

The [November 7 consolidated research edition](https://github.com/HawkesNest-LLC/Oracle/blob/5d73e901a061945f35a0a72e636c649d4dcd95df/research/dissertations/2026-11-07_CONSOLIDATED_RESEARCH_EDITION_AND_ROADMAP.md) is explicitly a candidate partial recovery, not a finished dissertation. Its distinctions remain in force for this paper.

## Experimental strength

Historical CC-90-001 receipts document bounded local tests, but the current publication did not reexecute those tests. RR-LIVING-001 and other living-world reports describe promising small demonstrations while leaving key independent checks unresolved. Some reported experiments do not expose executable code, raw logs, sealed answer keys, calibration details or model versions through the available sources. Their findings cannot support statistical superiority claims. Ties remain ties. Small denominators cannot establish robust absence of rare privacy leaks.

For the H7 execution failure, the causal mechanism of earlier premature termination is **UNKNOWN**. The observed contrast between a prior incomplete thread and a later successful fresh-thread task does not determine whether context length, resource conditions, model variation, tool ordering, time budgets or another cause was responsible. No internal telemetry was available.

## Engineering readiness

The historical ORACLE runtime inspection recorded a particular state of a Windows local system at a past date. It is not verification of a live November 2026 endpoint, present authorization policy, current SQLite schema, device parity, active scheduler or background execution. An independently demonstrated local SQLite transaction does not establish cross-service atomicity. WAL does not remove all lock contention. A code listing in a Google Doc does not establish that code is deployed or secure.

The architecture has costs: extra storage for source custody, index maintenance, access-control operations, tool calls, human review and repair. A simpler retrieval system may perform equally well on some tasks. The full system should be deployed only when measured benefits justify its overhead.

## Human and fictional identity

Fictional autonomous initiative is a narrative and computational property, **not** proof of awareness. A character checkpoint, memory record or convincing digital person is not demonstrably identical to an originating human. No experiment in this manuscript proves consciousness transfer, personal survival after death, complete neural capture, or numerical identity across substrates. Treat speculation about the Continuity Horizon as a research ambition.

An approved fictional event can be canon within a story without being a real-world event. Scientific evaluation must not use an invented sensor reading, fictional character statement or story scene as evidence of physical discovery. Private creative manuscripts remain outside the publication package.

## Privacy, rights and long-term stewardship

Original family testimony, sensitive journals, private communications, credentials, work records, and unauthorized copyrighted text are excluded. Some connected document links may be restricted. A public repository commit is permanent in practical ways: deleting a later version cannot guarantee erasure from forks, mirrors or previously cloned histories. Therefore future public releases should be reviewed for consent and source exposure before publication.

Hash chains require trustworthy acquisition, signers and key custody before they can support an origin claim. Retention and deletion mechanisms must be tested rather than assumed from doctrine. Human oversight, independent review and corrections are necessary even when a model appears to remember instructions.

## What would change the assessment

Independent replication with frozen synthetic datasets, clean cross-model restoration, controlled H1-H7 baseline comparisons, tested security boundaries, direct current-host inspection, and publicly inspectable execution receipts could upgrade individual claims. Such upgrades should appear as dated revisions with links to primary outputs, not silent changes to earlier historical descriptions.
