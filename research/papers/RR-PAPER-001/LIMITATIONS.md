# RR-PAPER-001: Limitations and nonclaims

This is a scientific working paper and a **research-program synthesis**, not peer-reviewed evidence of a deployed technology.

## Empirical
The corpus is incomplete; archives may be missing or access-controlled. Some reported experiments lack raw logs in this environment. A documentation receipt is not independent replication. Cross-model portability, large-world scheduling and multi-week drift performance remain open.

## Causal
The earlier ChatGPT premature-termination observation lacks internal execution telemetry. A later completed assignment does not isolate cause. H7 is a candidate hypothesis, not a result. Observational case reports are not randomized trials.

## Epistemic
Raw source bytes and modification metadata have different evidentiary powers. Hashes detect changes to specific bytes, not truth, authorship, consent or completeness. AI-generated prose in a compiled dissertation must not silently become the original author's verbatim words.

## Engineering
The 2026-07-30 ORACLE runtime inspection is historical. A 2026-11-14 SQLite bridge is code described in a document. Neither proves the current local host state, production security or active scheduler. WAL and local ACID properties do not make cross-service writes atomically consistent.

## Philosophical
Fictional initiative is simulation, not subjective awareness. Behavioral resemblance, coherent autobiographical narratives and portable checkpoint reproduction do not demonstrate numerical personal identity, consciousness transfer or post-biological survival. No novel physics is demonstrated.

## Privacy and intellectual property
Private autobiographical journals, family records, employer material, credentials, copyrighted creative manuscripts, or third-party transcripts are not reproduced. Some links are access-controlled. This paper makes no patent-status claim. Publishing a source link does not license its contents.

## Future correction
Scientific findings may change; all updates should retain Git history, dated revision notes and exact evidence links. Historical descriptions should remain historically faithful even when superseded.
