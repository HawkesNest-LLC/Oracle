## 2. Actual scientific conclusions, by evidence strength
### P1. Established physics
Special relativity predicts different proper times along different worldlines: `dτ=dt sqrt(1-v²/c²)` in an inertial frame. Clock comparisons are experimentally supported. The local Temporal Lab 001 model implements this *constant relative speed* relationship only. It is NOT an accelerated round-trip spacecraft simulation.
Constant proper acceleration a along one inertial-frame direction has `t(τ)=(c/a)sinh(aτ/c)`, `x(τ)=(c²/a)(cosh(aτ/c)-1)`, assuming initially at rest, flat spacetime. Require SI unit consistency and numerical verification for all reported examples.
Exact solutions of Einstein's equations can contain closed timelike curves under particular global conditions. Their mathematical existence does not establish that such conditions can be built or reached.
For the standard Morris-Thorne traversable wormhole throat, the flare-out condition implies null energy condition violation in classical GR under the model's assumptions. It is too strong to claim all conceivable traversable wormholes are categorically disproven. Chronology protection is a conjecture, not a general proof.

### P2. What is NOT established
No observed physical past-directed time travel, controllable macroscopic traversable wormhole, engineered CTC, or demonstration that LEGACY.GI/SOV1 information metrics source gravitational curvature.
Quantum information, ER=EPR and entanglement do not provide an established controllable faster-than-light communication channel.
The phrase 'mathematically possible' must always name the metric, boundary conditions, and assumptions.
