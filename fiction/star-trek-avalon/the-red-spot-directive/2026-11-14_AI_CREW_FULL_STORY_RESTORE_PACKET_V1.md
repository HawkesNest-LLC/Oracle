## 2. Full episode in three acts: beginning, middle, end

### Beginning: Part I, The Silence Before the Collective
2397. Reg hears three breaths, pause, fourth, and the plea "I remember being one person." A damaged Borg vessel, its enormous outer habitat concealed by Jupiter's Great Red Spot, is revealed. A smaller, older temporal installation sits inside. The colony contains approximately 43,000 former drones, not an army of willing Collective agents. Talia asks for asylum. Director Vale's Concordance began as voluntary shared-memory therapy after a 2197-era temporal arrival but became compulsory memory editing, suppressing grief, dissent, and memories of missing children. Noah commands USS Avalon NCC-75154; Ashley independently commands Jupiter Station. The Borg needle probe surveys station networks. Seven of Nine arrives as a liberated survivor and independent operator, not a 2401 Starfleet captain. Noah, Reg, Solis, and Imani descend in shuttle Gawain. Reg's holographic continuation after his 2395 biological death is of interest to the Queen, who seeks temporally resilient command architecture. Ellie Hawkes, Noah's holographic daughter and Starfleet science/continuity officer, is targeted. The Queen is distinct from Vale and the Concordance.

### Middle: RD-001 and RD-002, enacted as proposed fiction
RD-001 confirmed by Noah: **Option 3, Set a Trap for the Queen, 150 Mindcoins**. Chapter Seven: Seven, Brakk, Sorev, Merlin and Ellie construct an isolated false path to Ellie's matrix. Ellie explicitly consents to the risk. Queen identifies the decoy and studies the defenders; she forges a future-state evacuation order purporting to come from Noah, but Merlin says it lacks current valid command authority. Ashley remains independently in command. Vale reveals the secret passage and admits he edited memories of children who vanished. Talia carries unconscious Mara, and a second Mara appears in the hidden room. Hundreds of children turn together: "THE EXPERIMENT HAS BEGUN."

RD-002 confirmed by Noah: **Option 1, Rescue the Children, 100 Mindcoins**. Chapter Eight, "The Children of Yesterday" (conversation-only full text): Noah orders rescue instead of immediate core assault. Imani counts 216 children in the accessible chamber, some biological, some holographic/temporally displaced. The two Maras describe themselves as yesterday and tomorrow. Borg defenders advance. Reg refuses an invitation to connect his own emitter to the Borg control network; he destroys a local control panel to open access. Six maintenance barges, one supported by Gawain, carry 216 children out of Jupiter's storm. Ashley diverts station defensive resources to build a rescue corridor, enabling their survival but leaving the station vulnerable to a Borg needle attack. Queen says compassion was accounted for. Seven concludes the rescued children's shifting temporal signatures may be a distributed timing reference. **The six barges are not yet confirmed safe or cleared for station docking.** Queen's claims and motives remain suspect.

Chapter Nine, "The Price of Tomorrow" (conversation-only full text): Avalon suffers injuries and shield damage while defending station. The children seem to signal by coordinated temporal signatures rather than ordinary transmission. Ashley maintains station emergency command and source-grounded casualty reporting. A Queen interface taunts the team that 217 children were in the original manifest, not 216. A mysterious corridor appears in impossible schematic geometry. Reg's mobile emitter locks to a repeated timestamp and shows an uncertain image of the 2395 Cytherian chair. A **third Mara**, apparently a biological/holographic hybrid, appears behind a locked door and says she has waited since before Reg died. The Queen reveals a chair-like temporal device and an **apparent living biological Reginald Barclay** in Starfleet uniform. She claims to have returned Noah's first officer. Noah rejects that assertion: "Reg is standing right here." Whether the apparent Barclay is real, simulation, temporal duplicate, trap, or another being is UNKNOWN. Reg's point-of-view must not treat the Queen's claims as verified truth. End on a cliffhanger: less than six minutes to aperture activation.

The **original master outline** projected Chapter Ten as a beacon shutdown triggering a secondary Borg attack, away-team entrapment, a false Avalon spectral image, boarding action, and a hard cliffhanger. **This is now a planning target, not a fixed already-written outcome**: Chapter Nine's third Mara and apparent biological Reg must be integrated, not erased. The strategic Collective endures. No miraculous instantaneous evacuation of 43,000 people, no guaranteed rescue of every survivor, no absolute proof of personal identity from a copy.
