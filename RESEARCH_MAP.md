## Project boundaries
- **Rendered Reality:** umbrella research and creative vision; no demonstrated human continuation.
- **LEGACY.GI:** proposed memory and identity-preservation architecture; fidelity and identity equivalence are unproven.
- **ORACLE:** continuity and provenance software effort; distinguish each reference kernel from a production service.
- **SOV1:** human-governance design; a name string is not verified authorization.
- **Continuity Computing:** research hypothesis concerning accountable historical state, corrections, evidence and branch identity.
- **Star Trek: Avalon / Jupiter Station:** authored fictional settings and simulation testbeds, not documentary evidence of a deployed holodeck.
