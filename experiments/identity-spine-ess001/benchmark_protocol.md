# Identity Spine Compression Benchmark | Candidate Protocol

Status: EXPERIMENTAL SPECIFICATION, NOT EXECUTED. No empirical superiority claimed.
Human approval: Noah.Physical. No canon promotion or production authority granted.

## Hypothesis
At equal information budgets and equivalent retrieval access, structure-preserving representations that encode decisions, corrections, provenance, temporal state, relationships, and uncertainty will achieve lower continuity distortion than strong conventional alternatives.

## Conditions
- A: strong conventional retrieval using well-structured prose/chunks, same facts and access.
- B: conventional event-sourced or graph-based history, same facts and access.
- C: typed continuity objects with explicit correction, provenance, authority, branch, and uncertainty fields.

## Six dimensions
F_D decision; F_C correction; F_P provenance; F_T temporal; F_R relational; F_U uncertainty.

## Protocol controls
Use source-matched synthetic histories, equal total representation budgets, identical model/version, identical held-out questions, comparable retrieval opportunities, blind scoring, predeclared error tolerances, complete prompts and outputs, latency and token-cost accounting. Include negative controls for missing evidence and misleading source attribution. Preserve all six component scores, not just an aggregate.

## Falsification
If C does not reliably outperform the strongest baseline on held-out tasks, or imposes unacceptable overhead, the claimed practical advantage is unsupported. Do not describe test-fixture success as evidence for this hypothesis.

## Required before results
Freeze dataset, questions, labels, budget, scoring rubric, model, version, seed policy and evaluation script in a source-pinned manifest. Execute A/B/C; publish raw scores only after review of private-data boundaries.
