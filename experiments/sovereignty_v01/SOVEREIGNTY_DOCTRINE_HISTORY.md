## Interpretation and limits
The 51/49 ratio denotes a constitutional boundary between *capability* and *authorized control of human identity, consent, canon and consequential changes*. It does **not** allocate 49% of effort, intelligence, truth, or moral status to AI. AI may conduct authorized research, critique, code and testing without waiting for approval for each harmless step. A human may retain governance authority while being wrong on a technical claim; personal authority does not establish independent factual truth or ownership of others.
