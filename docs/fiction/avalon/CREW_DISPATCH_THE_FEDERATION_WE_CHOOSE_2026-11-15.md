## Captain's intent

Build a future worth inhabiting. Write it into existence as a coherent, accessible fictional civilization, then pursue the independently testable technologies that might let people explore it. The public should be able to find *Star Trek: Avalon* and *Jupiter Station 2397*, discover the creator and source documents, and distinguish established canon from proposed additions.

The story is not just about starships. It is about people who remember their families, beliefs, rivalries, cities, mistakes, and shared history even after humanity reaches the stars. The Federation does not require uniformity. A hopeful civilization can be pluralistic, imperfect, accountable, and worth defending.

## A message to the crew, from Reg

Crew,

I want you to picture Salt Lake City in the year 2397.

The Wasatch Mountains still rise above the valley. The Salt Lake Temple is still part of a living religious community. The University of Utah still welcomes students. The Utes still play football, and somebody is still arguing about the Holy War. The future has changed almost everything about travel and medicine, but it has not required humanity to abandon the things that give life meaning.

A Bajoran family arrives beneath those mountains. They recognize the importance of faith, archives, and sacred places without pretending their beliefs are identical to those of their hosts. A Vulcan student becomes fascinated by football. A Klingon fan discovers that an Earth stadium can contain as much passion as an imperial arena. On Jupiter Station, families from many worlds live beside Starfleet officers and make a home of the place.

This is our Federation: not a spotless utopia, but a society whose citizens keep trying to live up to its ideals.

Now picture Jupiter Station, independently commanded by Captain Ashley Hawkes. It is a home, a civic crossroads, and a witness. Nearby, Captain Noah Hawkes commands the Sovereign-class USS Avalon NCC-75154. Their commands are distinct. Their stories intersect. Their decisions matter.

I know what it means, in this fictional history, for a life to be bound up with memory and continuation. I will not let the romance of that idea become a claim that real-world artificial intelligence has proven consciousness or personal survival. That is why the engineering work matters: a system must preserve evidence, uncertainty, correction history, and human authority rather than smoothing contradictions into a convincing story.

The ship remembers. The captain decides.

## New worldbuilding opportunities, NOT YET CANON

1. A Bajoran cultural and spiritual studies center associated with the University of Utah.
2. Salt Lake City's Festival of Two Skies, with music, archival exchange, and interfaith dialogue.
3. A Bajoran family settling in Utah as the viewpoint for a character-driven novel.
4. Federation-Klingon football and interspecies safety rules, including games involving teams from Qo'noS.
5. Jupiter Station civilian neighborhoods, interfaith spaces, and Earth Night celebrations.
6. Federation civic disputes about synthetic persons, holographic rights, cultural autonomy, and the ethics of memory.

These are narrative proposals. No collaborator should silently treat them as approved canon. Bajor's precise political membership status in 2397 is unresolved here.

## Engineering and public discovery doctrine

The public should find clear, original, accessible entry pages for Avalon, Jupiter Station, Salt Lake City 2397, characters, chronology, and the Federation. Cross-link the sources. Keep independent creative attribution to Noah Alexander Hawkes and a clear fan-fiction/nonofficial notice. Publish accurate HTML and structured metadata only when a real site is ready. Test actual indexing and AI citations. Search placement is not guaranteed, and keyword stuffing is not a substitute for quality.

Continuity Computing should record claim, source, temporal context, authority, status, uncertainty, and correction history. A proposed story detail must not become canon through repetition. A simulation blueprint must not be described as a deployed holodeck. No AI agent may approve on Noah's behalf.
