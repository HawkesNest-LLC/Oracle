# Governed persistent narrative continuity
Research concept / draft, 2026-11-27
Project creator: Noah Alexander Hawkes. Draft synthesis: ChatGPT. Not peer reviewed. No experimental results claimed.

## Abstract
A persistent multi-agent narrative environment offers a bounded testbed for historical fidelity, correction survival, and identity drift. We propose exchanging provenance-bearing records across language-model sessions while separating author-approved state from generated proposals. The architecture is a research proposal, not a demonstrated autonomous runtime.

## Architecture
Each contribution carries a unique identifier, parent record, creation date, fictional sequence, source references, attributed origin, state changes, unresolved questions and approval status. Original text remains separate from derived state. Conflicting branches remain separate until evidence or authorized decisions resolve them. Missing evidence is explicitly unknown. Human approval governs promotion; archival receipts distinguish prepared records from verified writes.

## Proposed evaluation
Compare stateless generation, summary-conditioned generation and source-linked retrieval under matched prompts, models, budgets and narrative horizons. Use synthetic characters and predeclared ground truth. Randomize scenarios and repeat across seeds or independent runs. Measure source-supported recall, contradiction rate, correction survival, attribution accuracy, temporal ordering, character differentiation and sustained goal development. Include ambiguous identity cases and unavailable-source controls. Blind evaluators to condition where possible; report uncertainty and failures. A proposed 30-day narrative horizon is a design option, not a completed experiment.

## Limitations
External memory does not guarantee identity preservation. Retrieval errors, stale summaries, model changes, evaluator bias and branch ambiguity can degrade continuity. Functional self-modeling and fictional autonomy do not establish subjective awareness, consciousness or personal survival. Manual exchanges do not demonstrate autonomous inter-agent communication. No novelty or priority claim is made.
