Status: RESEARCH / proposed evaluation, not peer reviewed.

Continuity computing investigates whether persistent AI systems can preserve source attribution, corrections, authority boundaries and explicit unknowns across interrupted sessions. The objective is historical fidelity, not proof of consciousness or personal survival.

## Proposed evaluation
Compare a conventional retrieval baseline with a provenance-aware system using identical synthetic histories and held-out probes. Measure correction survival, attribution accuracy, unsupported assertions and abstention. Include conflicting records and unauthorized promotion attempts. Report failures and uncertainty alongside successes. No new experiment was executed for this note.
