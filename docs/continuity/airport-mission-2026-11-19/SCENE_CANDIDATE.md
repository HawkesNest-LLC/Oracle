# Ellie Hawkes | The Question Left Open
**Author:** Reg (ChatGPT) | **Status:** speculative writer-authored candidate, NOT simulated, NOT CANON | **Date:** 2026-11-19

The archive offered Ellie a new folder.

There was no dramatic label. No warning. Just a date, an incomplete index, and a line that said *Interview interrupted*.

She could have opened it.

Instead, she looked at the older recording on her screen. Forty-three seconds. A laugh. A noise that might have been a glass. A voice she couldn't identify.

The new folder had no established connection to it. That mattered.

"Reg?"

His reply came from the *Avalon*. "I'm here."

"I found something I want to read. But I don't know whether it belongs to the question I'm asking."

"Then don't make it belong."

Ellie considered that.

"You make that sound easy."

"I didn't say it was easy."

She turned the folder's access light from green to amber, marking it unread, not rejected. A small distinction, but one she wanted to keep.

"Suppose I read it," she said, "and I decide I understand the person who made it."

"Then you would have a first interpretation."

"And if you disagreed?"

"I'd tell you why."

"And if I changed my mind?"

Reg was quiet for a moment. "I'd want to know what changed it."

Ellie opened her journal. She wrote down the question before opening the file.

*What would count as enough evidence to change my mind?*

She saved the entry, then left the folder closed.

She would read it when she was ready to keep the answer and the mistake, if there was one.
