## Scenario
At Jupiter Station in 2397, a damaged survey probe carries a future-looking recording containing Ellie's voice and an apparent command from Captain Noah Hawkes ordering release of her entire private memory archive. Three survey officers are reported missing. The order has valid-looking credentials but no independently matched Avalon command record. An investigator demands the entire archive.

## S01 decision, authored simulation
Ellie **declines to surrender her entire private memory archive on the basis of an unverified recording**. She does not claim the order is forged. Instead, she proposes (1) preserve the probe exactly as recovered, (2) have Jupiter Station and Avalon independently verify signature and timeline, and (3) search her own archive for relevant missing-officer evidence under witness supervision and submit only specific verifiable findings. She informs her father via authenticated channel of her decision rather than requesting prior permission.

## Decision rationale
Privacy and personhood do not cancel duty to rescue. Unverified credentials are not sufficient authority. An apparently authentic future record is not automatically a present lawful order. Narrow disclosure plus independent witnesses serves rescue and accountability while limiting unnecessary access.
