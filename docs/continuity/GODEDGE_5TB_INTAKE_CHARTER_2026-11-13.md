## Architecture boundary
1. Source vault: preserve raw originals without rewriting; do not upload private family photos or sensitive documents to public repositories by default.
2. Inventory: stable source ID, source system, path, media type, byte size, capture time, extraction status, cryptographic digest, access policy.
3. Provenance graph: creator, speaker, transport, derivative relationship, revision, correction, adoption and revocation.
4. Continuity state: decisions, commitments, unresolved questions, relationships, fictional branches and real-world chronology kept distinct.
5. Retrieval: source-linked answers with calibrated UNKNOWN, conflict preservation, and no fabricated memories.
6. Agent execution: least-privilege tool access, explicit action boundaries, immutable receipts, human approval for protected actions.
7. Rendering: text and interactive worlds derived from grounded sources; generated reconstructions labeled as such.
