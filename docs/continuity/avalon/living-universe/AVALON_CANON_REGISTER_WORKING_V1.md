## Status vocabulary

- **ESTABLISHED:** explicitly controlled by inspected creator correction or adoption record; inspect original story for scene details.
- **CANDIDATE:** proposed fiction or commissioned scenario without adoption receipt.
- **CONTRADICTED:** competing claims exist and have not been adjudicated.
- **SUPERSEDED:** a later controlling correction replaces an older account; retain the old account as history.
- **OPEN:** no supported resolution. An open item must not be silently completed.
