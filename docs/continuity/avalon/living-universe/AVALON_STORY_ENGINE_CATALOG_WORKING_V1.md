**Status:** PROPOSED RESEARCH TOOL, NOT CANON. **Authority:** Noah.Physical. **Source IDs:** [M1 manifest](source-manifest-v1.json). **Scope:** Issue #27, PR #28. This register distinguishes retrieved claims from extrapolations; its publication does not approve fiction.

## The four dramatic layers

**Adventure:** discovery, rescue, diplomacy, scientific mystery. **Institutions:** command, government, rights and accountability. **Continuity:** history, contested evidence, remembered consequences. **Home:** Ashley, family, civilians, relationships and return journeys. These are proposed editorial framing, not a recovered creator quotation.

## Story template

1. **Opening ordinary reality:** show work or home before the disruption.
2. **Incident:** define the problem in one concrete sentence.
3. **Evidence ledger:** observed / reported / inferred / unknown.
4. **Actors:** each faction's objective, capability, intelligence gap, red line and internal disagreement.
5. **Choices:** at least three plausible options, each with a cost.
6. **Authority:** who may act, who must consent, who can object.
7. **Decision:** a character acts before all uncertainty disappears.
8. **Consequence:** a relationship, record, institution or resource changes.
9. **Next-episode obligation:** what future writers must not reset.
10. **Canon status:** candidate until Noah.Physical approves.

## Writer's room anti-patterns

Do not use the Temporal Core to explain every anomaly, make Romulans universally deceitful, reduce Klingons to honor slogans, subordinate Ashley to Avalon, treat Ellie as a switchable device, or declare archival possession equivalent to legal jurisdiction. Do not use a Q appearance as an easy answer to the 2395 gap. Do not make new episodes retroactively resolve existing mysteries without author approval.

## Two-Witness pilot gate

The November 13 directive commissions a Romulan report, a Klingon envoy, conflicting Avalon/Jupiter evidence and independent commands [S01]. Before drafting the pilot, recover the adopted signal sequence [S03], confirm branch authentication [S05], and compare any Meridian/Venn references. A compelling episode can end with an accountable protective action and a still-open historical question.
