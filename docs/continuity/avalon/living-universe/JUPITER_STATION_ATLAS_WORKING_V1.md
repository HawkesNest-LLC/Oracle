## Established orientation

Jupiter Station is in the Sol system, a large inhabited Federation setting, independently commanded by Captain Ashley Hawkes. It is the Hawkes family home and an operational, civilian, scientific and archival environment [S01, S07, S08]. Its exact population, deck count, dimensions, endurance and module schematic are not verified in this source set.
