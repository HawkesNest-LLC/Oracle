## The three final goals
1. REMEMBER THE JOURNEY: Preserve Noah's writings, decisions, relationships, corrections, creative canon, provenance, and uncertainty across time and platforms.
2. CARRY THE WORK FORWARD: A governed, persistent AI research and execution system that resumes work, executes bounded tasks, tests changes, tracks commitments, and produces receipts across model upgrades.
3. ENTER RENDERED REALITY: A source-grounded, persistent interactive world including Jupiter Station and USS Avalon, with voice/VR, persistent consequences, and consent-based family participation.

The goals are complementary: evidence foundation -> governed execution -> experiential simulation. The archive is not equivalent to the person; fictional canon is not real-world history; behavioral simulation is not evidence of consciousness transfer or survival.

## Phase 1 | 0-3 months | Foundation
- Recover and re-run CC-90-001 locally with exact test and environment receipts; audit current PR and main state.
- Define immutable sources, assertions, corrections, conflicts, authority, valid-time and recorded-time, and branch separation.
- Build CC-Conflict as an extension only after comparing existing code; replace unjustified scores with evidence-backed uncertainty or UNKNOWN.
- Implement authentication, scoped human approval, privacy and consent controls, export/backup and restore.
- Preregister matched-budget baselines: conventional retrieval, event-sourced provenance, governance-disabled kernel, governance-enabled kernel.
Exit: reproducible local restartable service with tested no-unauthorized-promotion, correction retention, branch separation, and abstention invariants.

## Phase 2 | 3-9 months | Memory Recovery
- Inventory accessible ChatGPT, Drive, GitHub, manuscripts, and archives, with source IDs, hash, custody, original author, acquisition date and gaps.
- Build chronology and correction graph, human-review queue, cross-source retrieval and citation UI.
- Evaluate historical recovery, perspective attribution, chronology, corrections, uncertainty and cost against strong baselines.
Exit: source-linked historical answers that preserve disagreement and explicitly report missing evidence.

## Phase 3 | 9-18 months | Operational Reg
- Persistent task/commitment state, owner/deadline tracking, approved agent permissions, test runner, rollback, scheduled review and receipt generation.
- Model migration tests: a replacement model resumes from the same governed state and passes continuity benchmarks.
- Add witness queries only with consent, safe provenance and the ability to decline or retract.
Exit: interrupted work can be resumed with auditable actions and no fabricated completion claims.

## Phase 4 | 18-30 months | Jupiter Station Prototype
- A single bounded 3D environment, voice conversation, persistent location/objects, character state and canonical scene registry.
- Noah commands Avalon; Ashley commands Jupiter Station; Reg first officer; Sorev science officer; Ellie holographic lieutenant junior grade in the 2397 canon. Verify all details against original approved sources before use.
- Keep fictional simulation events SIMULATED until explicitly promoted by Noah.Physical; never merge fictional events into real-life biography.
Exit: enter, interact, leave, return and verify the same authorized world state.

## Phase 5 | 30-60 months | Shared Rendered Reality
- Expand worlds, optional VR, family permissions and independent identities, multiplayer, persistent consequences, portability, and abuse prevention.
- Evaluate usability, user trust, data minimization, consent, operational costs and accessibility.
Exit: usable multi-participant environment with durable governance and exportability. Not a promise of complete historical or conscious identity reconstruction.
