## 4. What the event establishes about home

The 2395 catastrophe is a historical anchor of this continuity. A candidate home history that lacks or contradicts that lineage cannot be accepted merely because its clocks say 2397 or its inhabitants have familiar faces.

**The anchor is necessary to the described lineage, but it is not a sufficient unique branch identifier.** Two histories can share the 2395 event and diverge afterward. A copied ledger can repeat the correct story without proving the endpoint's continuity from departure.

The adopted framework requires independent physical references and a documented history of those references. Documentary agreement helps interpret measurements; it cannot replace physical branch discrimination. Matching dates, matching memories, and matching records alone do not settle the return.

Avalon cannot certify its own return exclusively from its onboard logs. Jupiter Station holds independently governed evidence. Nor may the station be the sole witness of itself. Conflicting accounts remain attributed and preserved while the crew investigates, rather than being rewritten to force agreement.
