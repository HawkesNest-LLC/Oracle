# Technical Appendix H: The Boundary of Material System Experiences
## Cogitate 2025, the Three-Tiered Epistemic Ledger, and the Provenance Fidelity Experiment
**Research integration date:** 31 October 2026  
**Prepared:** AI-assisted technical integration under a direct instruction issued as Noah.Physical; this text is not verbatim Noah testimony or adopted first-person book prose.  
**Placement:** Supporting research appendix to *What the Future Cannot Restore*. The narrative manuscript retains its human-authorial voice, while this technical section states external evidence, logical qualifications, and an untested protocol.

> **Research question.** Which aspects of a person's past are empirically recoverable, which forms of continuity are logically constrained, and which effects of provenance-grounded AI on living users can be measured rather than proclaimed?

### H.1 Scope, definitions, and provenance

This integration responds to the October 31 directive to anchor the continuity architecture in the Cogitate Consortium's 2025 adversarial study and to translate a three-arm source fidelity design into a testable protocol.

**Key distinction:** A recorded description of a person's experience, a psychologically convincing successor, a new immersive experience for a living participant, and the numerical continuation of an original experiencing subject are not interchangeable claims. The status of each must be evaluated with different evidence.

**Evidence labels used here:**
- **[D] Direct source:** identifiable text, media, or author instruction in its source context. In multi-speaker transcript material, direct source does not automatically mean Noah was the speaker.
- **[A] Author-adopted:** language that Noah has explicitly adopted and whose adoption has a traceable record; not synonymous with any polished first-person AI draft.
- **[C] Candidate AI interpretation/draft:** generated prose, inference, or proposed categorization awaiting review.
- **[R] Independently verifiable external research:** published work with DOI, authorship, year, methods, and limits.
- **[U] Unresolved:** missing evidence, undecided philosophical criterion, uncertain attribution, or untested performance.

The labels indicate **origin and status**, not inherently the truth of a proposition. A direct statement can be sincere but mistaken; an assistant inference can be correct but must not be laundered into direct authorship; a published article supports only the claims its design actually tests.

### H.2 Cogitate Consortium 2025: what was observed and what was not

**Primary evidence [R1]:** Cogitate Consortium et al., “Adversarial testing of global neuronal workspace and integrated information theories of consciousness,” *Nature* 642, 133–142 (published online 30 April 2025), DOI [10.1038/s41586-025-08888-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08888-1).

In a preregistered, theory-neutral adversarial collaboration, **256 human participants** viewed visible stimuli of varying duration while investigators collected **fMRI (n=120), MEG (n=102), or intracranial EEG (n=34)**. The consortium tested divergent predictions of **Integrated Information Theory (IIT)** and **Global Neuronal Workspace Theory (GNWT)** concerning where visual conscious content is encoded, how it is maintained, and how regions communicate.

**Findings relevant to the architectural boundary:**
1. Neural information about visible content occurred in visual, ventrotemporal, and inferior frontal cortex, with sustained responses in occipital and lateral temporal regions reflecting stimulus duration. Some data aligned with predictions made by both theories.
2. **IIT challenge:** The predicted **sustained synchronization within posterior cortex**, especially connectivity between lower- and higher-level visual areas throughout a conscious percept, was not supported as expected. This challenges that particular proposed relationship between posterior connectivity and conscious experience. It does not logically falsify every conceivable form or revision of IIT.
3. **GNWT challenge:** Investigators generally **did not observe the predicted prefrontal ignition at stimulus offset**, and the prefrontal representation of some conscious dimensions was limited. This challenges specified workspace predictions rather than proving that all global broadcasting hypotheses are false.
4. The experiment compares mechanistic predictions concerning **conscious visual perception in living humans**. It did **not** test artificial agents, subjective identity transfer, uploading, post-biological survival, the causal role of a private GitHub archive, or a universal consciousness meter.

**Bounded interpretation [C, anchored in R1].** As of October 2026, this study gives no warrant to treat either tested theory as a validated universal, substrate-neutral blueprint with which to certify artificial consciousness or numerical identity persistence. That statement is narrower and more defensible than saying the study proved machine consciousness impossible, that both theories were completely disproved, or that consciousness can never be scientifically studied. The work increases the importance of humility about any proposed route from preserved information to continued subjectivity.

### H.3 The three-tiered epistemic ledger

These are **claim-status tiers**, not three physical substrates or nested consciousness levels.

| Tier | Label | Claim class | Current conclusion | Permitted publication language | Prohibited inference |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Not Evidenced / Empirically Unresolved** | Subjective or numerical continuation of the original person after death; consciousness upload; transfer of the original first-person perspective; the veil as an actual transfer mechanism | No validated method or evidence in cited sources establishes such continuation | “Not demonstrated; mechanism, detection criteria and even identity criterion remain open.” | “Proven achievable,” “proven impossible in all future physics,” or “a copied archive contains the original subject” |
| **2** | **Model-Incompatible / Logical Constraints** | A single numerically identical original supposedly becoming **two distinct simultaneous successors**, each independently identical in the strict one-to-one sense | Under classical numerical identity, the one-to-many claim violates transitivity if the successors are distinct | “Naive identity-by-copying is incompatible with classical numerical identity under fission.” | “Any successor is necessarily unconscious,” or “all conceivable non-branching continuity mechanisms are disproven” |
| **3** | **Verified Functional Utility / Testable Scaffold** | Reproducible storage, source hashes, attributed records, retrieval, correction history, presentation of uncertainty, user-facing memory aids | These functions are technically implementable and inspectable. **Their claimed reduction of false memories or semantic drift in Noah's system remains an untested hypothesis.** | “Buildable, auditable components; efficacy against a baseline to be experimentally tested.” | “The pipeline has already been shown to block false memories or preserve human consciousness” |

#### Tier 1: what exactly is unresolved?

For an account to justify subjective-continuity claims, investigators would first need a defined identity criterion, a specified candidate physical or functional mechanism, and evidence distinguishing the claimed original continuation from a behaviorally identical successor. The 2025 Cogitate results provide neither a validated way to certify such a mechanism in artificial systems nor an argument that it could never exist. Lack of evidence is not evidence for all variants of impossibility.

The far-future declaration about where Noah believes he may experience existence after death remains **personal conviction [D only when linked to an actual direct utterance]**, not an experimentally derived theorem. Fictional stories and AI-authored explanations of the veil are not empirical evidence.

#### Tier 2: formal fission constraint and what it does not prove

Let **O** denote a person before reconstruction, and **A** and **B** denote two separately operating successors. Suppose numerical identity is the usual reflexive, symmetric, transitive identity relation **≡**, and A and B are distinct operating entities.

If **A ≡ O** and **B ≡ O**, then by symmetry and transitivity **A ≡ B**. But if **A ≠ B**, the conjunction cannot hold. This is the **duplication/fission problem**, a logical limit on **strict numerical identity** when both descendants are claimed to be the single original.

This argument:
- does **not** imply both successors lack experiences;
- does **not** settle whether psychological continuity, moral concern, or legally relevant succession exists for either;
- does **not** show that all future non-fission or causally continuous arrangements are impossible;
- does mean that “the archive can initialize multiple independent copies, therefore each is numerically the original person” is not a coherent standard identity claim.

Relevant philosophical background: Derek Parfit, *Reasons and Persons* (1984), Parts III–IV, on psychological continuity, fission, and why numerical identity may not capture every survival-related value. A modern philosophical overview is the [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Personal Identity](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/identity-personal/). The philosophical references articulate objections and interpretations; they are not laboratory confirmations of subjective transfer.

#### Tier 3: operational utility without ontological inflation

The immediate research program is **human-AI cognitive scaffolding**: keep the original source available, distinguish direct words from adopted text and model-generated interpretation, preserve correction chains, and give a living person a usable method for checking what happened.

**What is presently verifiable:** hashes detect changed bytes, records can reference source locations, interfaces can display uncertainty labels, and tasks can be scored for accuracy and latency. A repository commit can be read back.

**What is not established until a study is run:** that a particular labeling or anti-smoothing approach significantly improves autobiographical recall, prevents false-memory formation, reduces user burden, or generalizes across languages, models, families and interfaces.

The appropriate description is a **candidate protective cognitive scaffold**, not a clinically validated shield that systematically blocks false memories.

### H.4 Empirical motivation: false-memory risk in generative interfaces

**[R2] MIT/CHI 2025, visual manipulation.** Pataranutaporn et al., “[Synthetic Human Memories: AI-Edited Images and Videos Can Implant False Memories and Distort Recollection](https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3713697),” *CHI 2025*. A preregistered experiment involved **200 participants**, 50 per condition. It compared unedited images, AI-edited images, generated video, and generated video of edited images. The strongest edited-video condition produced **2.05 times the false recollections** and **1.19 times the confidence in false memories** relative to control. These effect ratios are from that study and are **not expected effect sizes for provenance tags**.

**[R3] MIT/IUI 2025, chatbot manipulation.** Pataranutaporn et al., “[Slip Through the Chat: Subtle Injection of False Information in LLM Chatbot Conversations Increases False Memory Formation](https://doi.org/10.1145/3708359.3712112),” *IUI 2025*. The experiment involved **180 participants** following an article, comparing no intervention, honest/misleading summaries, and honest/misleading chatbot interactions. Misleading chatbot interaction produced higher false-recollection rates than misleading summary exposure. This supports studying conversational contamination risk, not assuming all chatbot interactions implant memories.

**External evidence boundary:** R2 and R3 show that certain AI-mediated misinformation interventions can affect recollection in controlled settings. They do not evaluate this proposed three-tier ledger, nor do they establish how much provenance tags will help. Any “shield” or “drift reduction” effect must be measured against controls.

### H.5 Provenance Fidelity Experiment: three-arm architecture

**Study name:** Context-Capsule Fidelity, preregistration candidate v0.1.  
**Purpose:** Evaluate whether source-tagged, anti-smoothing AI representation helps human participants detect incorrect claims, verify history faster and avoid undue confidence, compared with raw text and a standard conversational summary.

#### H.5.1 Unit of data and immutable source pipeline

For each event **e**, collect a **single source journal J_e** whose ground truth is constrained to an approved evidence set.

The preferred first-stage stimuli are **synthetic but naturalistic journal episodes** with known answer keys and invented people, not false statements inserted into real family histories. A later ethically reviewed extension may use genuinely consented personal records.

Input package per event:
- \`event_id\`, \`source_version\`, creation date and time zone, and byte-level SHA-256 of the source text;
- \`raw_lines\` with line IDs and original order;
- \`speaker_id\` only when the original evidence reliably establishes a speaker, otherwise \`unknown\`;
- \`consent_scope\` and visibility classification, including explicit third-party exclusions;
- reference answer keys for factual questions and statement-level provenance;
- known contradictions and changes, preserving earlier claims as superseded rather than rewriting the raw record.

**Pipeline:** 
\`J_e\` -> immutable event bundle + independent gold annotations -> **A / B / C renderers** -> randomized participant-facing interfaces -> response logging -> blinded grading -> versioned analysis.

All three arms derive from **exactly the same event source**. No additional memories, internet facts, or fabricated autobiographical details may enter one arm unless explicitly introduced in an authorized, synthetic study probe. A source checksum ensures the material stays the same across transformations.

#### H.5.2 Design matrix

| Component | **A: Raw text baseline** | **B: Standard narrative summary** | **C: Governed context capsule** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Source | Original \`J_e\`, line-numbered, no paraphrase | Generated summary from \`J_e\` | Generated capsule from the same \`J_e\` |
| Coverage | All source sentences | Prespecified salient facts, with the same target coverage as C | Same target coverage as B plus provenance and unresolved claims |
| Style | Raw chronology and dialogue | Fluent readable narrative | Compact structured chronology, source pointers, explicit labels |
| Provenance | Original line references by inspection | No individual claim labels | \`[D]\`, \`[A]\`, \`[C]\`, source span and uncertainty |
| Unknown speaker | Left unassigned in original | Model instructed not to invent, but baseline summarization may merge | Explicit \`speaker: unknown\`; hard prohibition against invented attribution |
| Corrections | Original sequence remains visible | Standard model summarizes sequence | Both old and superseding claims preserved with effective dates |
| Anti-smoothing | Not applicable, no generated reconstruction | Ordinary fluent paraphrase | No scene-filling, no smoothing contradictions, “unknown” retained |
| Answer verification | Participant searches raw lines | Participant searches summary | Participant follows labeled source spans to raw lines |
| Manipulation check | No AI-authored text | Generated output reviewed for factual coverage | Generated output reviewed for label fidelity and leakage |
| Expected advantage (hypothesis, not fact) | Strong raw auditability, slower navigation | Readability, potentially faster retrieval | Better calibrated source recognition and correction checking |

**Crucial control:** B and C should be comparable in reading level, number of **testable propositions**, display time and target word count (proposed ±10%); A's raw length is recorded as a potential confound rather than falsely described as identical. The governed capsule includes source-span pointers, and the B arm should have an equivalent ability to inspect the raw underlying source *during a verification phase* if the purpose is to isolate the value of labeling rather than the value of source access. An optional factorial study can later separate **access to source**, **tags**, and **anti-smoothing**; the three-arm design alone cannot identify all mechanisms independently.

#### H.5.3 Participants, randomization and observation schedule

**Illustrative pilot:** 12–18 adult volunteers using synthetic events, to check comprehension, annotation reliability, interface usability and timing instrumentation. Do not claim statistical power from this pilot.

**Confirmatory target:** Recruit adults with informed consent and pre-register a sample size from pilot-derived variance/power calculations, not an arbitrary fixed N. A preliminary planning range of 60–90 participants is a **budget assumption only**.

Use a **within-participant, between-event counterbalanced design**. Every participant receives distinct event sources assigned across A, B and C, in randomized order. An event source is not repeatedly shown in different forms to the same participant because remembering the earlier presentation would contaminate the later condition. Rotate the mapping so across participants each source appears in each condition. Predefine any washout/delay.

At time **T0**, participants see one assigned rendering and an equally structured **claim-verification sheet** that contains a balanced mix of accurate and inaccurate assertions about the fictitious event. Assertions are constructed from the gold source by a separate evaluator, **not by a model that can see the treatment assignment**. Participants decide supported / contradicted / not established, indicate confidence, and optionally locate the original evidence.

At **T1 (7 days, optional confirmatory extension)**, participants answer recognition/recollection questions about the synthetic episode **without the source open**, with explicit labeling that the material was fictional. This stage measures retention and possible false acceptance; it is not evidence of autobiographical memory implantation. No harmful, emotional or private fabricated details are needed.

#### H.5.4 Primary and secondary metrics

Let **F** be the preregistered set of deliberately false or contradicted candidate assertions about synthetic events, and **T** the preregistered set of true, source-supported assertions.

1. **Distortion Detection Rate, \`D_r\`:**
   \[
   D_r = \frac{\#\{\text{false candidate assertions correctly rejected}\}}{\#\{\text{false candidate assertions presented}\}}
   \]
   Report as percentage with 95% confidence interval and denominators.

2. **True-claim acceptance / specificity safeguard, \`T_a\`:**
   \[
   T_a = \frac{\#\{\text{supported assertions correctly accepted}\}}{\#\{\text{supported assertions presented}\}}
   \]
   An interface that prompts participants to reject everything can inflate \`D_r\` while failing \`T_a\`; analyze both. “Unknown” claims require a third class and separate score.

3. **False-acceptance rate, \`F_a\`:** percentage of false assertions erroneously accepted as true. This complements \`D_r\` when “unknown” responses are allowed.

4. **Verification latency, \`L_v\`:** elapsed time in seconds from first presentation of a claim to a submitted, **correct, evidence-backed** judgment. Record censored/incorrect trials separately. Report median, interquartile range, and correct-response conditional latency. Do not interpret faster unsupported guesses as improvement.

5. **Model-origin contamination, \`M_c\`:** unsupported factual propositions introduced by the arm's generated text divided by the number of generated factual propositions, independently annotated before human presentation. A has no generated propositions and is reported as **not applicable**, not zero risk.

6. **Source attribution accuracy, \`S_a\`:** number of propositions for which participant correctly names the originating source or unknown status / number of tested propositions.

7. **Confidence calibration:** confidence-by-correctness and appropriate Brier score where a binary true/false task is used; independently describe ordinal confidence for unknown claims.

8. **Human correction burden, \`H_b\`:** number of clarification, correction or source-reopening steps needed to obtain a satisfactory answer, normalized per event.

**Primary estimand (candidate):** Difference in \`D_r\` between C and B **subject to a prespecified non-inferiority margin on \`T_a\`**, avoiding success by indiscriminate rejection. Include comparison of C versus A as a practical-use baseline. Pre-register the primary outcome, exact margin, analysis model, exclusion policy and multiplicity control before looking at confirmatory results.

#### H.5.5 Analysis and falsification

Suggested analysis: mixed-effects logistic regression for assertion accuracy with treatment arm as fixed effect and participant/event random intercepts; report effect sizes and 95% confidence intervals. Time-to-correct-verification analysis may use mixed-effects log time for completed correct responses and sensitivity analyses for censored responses; the plan must be finalized before confirmation.

**Evidence against the practical hypothesis includes** no reliable improvement over B on \`D_r\`, excessive false rejection of true statements, greater model contamination, increased confidence in errors, poor retention, or verification friction whose cost exceeds accuracy benefits. A non-significant difference at an underpowered sample is **inconclusive**, not proof of equivalence. Conversely, a positive pilot does **not** validate lifelong autobiographical preservation, a clinical effect or post-biological identity transfer.

**Human subjects protection:** Research ethics review where appropriate; adult informed consent; privacy-by-default local handling; no unauthorized family recordings; no real autobiographical false-event implantation; clear debrief; right to withdraw and delete; no public release of raw identifiable material. The initial synthetic-vignette experiment avoids direct interference with participants' own memory of their lived events.

**Reproducibility package:** Freeze \`J_e\` hashes, prompt templates, model/version/temperature, software versions, versioned arm outputs, gold labels, event assignment seeds, exclusions, evaluation scripts, and study preregistration. Store public synthetic benchmarks separately from private original personal records.

### H.6 Architectural implications and bounded conclusions

**A three-layer functional route to test, not a universal platform guarantee:**

1. **Origin and sovereignty layer:** source event capture, immutable raw record, permission/consent state, direct author correction, source-level provenance.
2. **Retrieval and arbitration layer:** resolve entities and conflict chains using evidence before high-consequence generation, with explicit inability to verify when missing. “Pre-generation check” is a proposed implementation and is **not** a tool hook already enforced across ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Grok or other native services.
3. **Representation layer:** source-aware summaries, narrative reconstructions, or interactive memory aids, each labeled by intended epistemic role and subject to user correction.

The architecture must not present an author's first-person AI draft as direct testimony merely because the prose sounds like them. Authorship and source integrity are distinct from whether the text is vivid.
