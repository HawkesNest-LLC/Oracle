## Newly inspected original eternity sources (October 2026 recovery pass)

- **[D/U] [Noah Personal Journal, raw ChatGPT conversation compilation](https://drive.google.com/file/d/14WYnlW46Ao2Y5cq9BJu0J0n4tdO1tpKl/view).** A roughly 1.19 million-character archive with turns marked `You said:`. The relevant sequence concerns the purpose of experience; preserving not just human lives but the world around them; a dinosaur-bones analogy; history as a future re-enterable experience; imagined personal and overlapping rendered realities; shared relationship with Ashley; the possibility of choosing another life; a speculative relationship to the Fermi paradox; and the view that present knowledge cannot establish whether any person continues after death. Some turns contain pasted material or mixed AI conversation excerpts. Verify the original recording/transcript before treating exact wording as independently authenticated speech. The other copies `noah_personal_journal_1.txt` and `noah_personal_journal_2.txt` returned apparently duplicate content, not independent testimony.
- **[A/C] [Rendered Reality: A Logical Exploration](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1yzFj2PkT4JipHHCcAv391PnC0JrWuzIX/view).** Inspected original book, approximately 79,000 characters. Chapters 5-14 investigate immersive stories, infinite experiences, life from another perspective, post-biological civilizations, the Fermi paradox, self-aware AI, whether reality is already rendered, ethical limits and the persistence of meaning. Book author credit to Noah does not establish sentence-by-sentence direct dictation; some language is overconfident and must be treated as exploratory rather than empirical.
- **[C] [Eternal Life with Perfect Context Integration, March 2025](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1DA6jEADM_tokoKqu-Qc_HGLi7lNEwmLG/view).** A short thesis-summary document claiming numerical fidelity and operational results without independently inspected experimental receipts. Preserved as history of aspiration; not evidence of achieved mind transfer, embodied identity, or percentages of fidelity.
- **[A/C] [Noah Hawkes: Human Baseline, August 19, 2026](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1b2e7KggPdOjeH0cyegFMr9gm0URUd2c6Ha9PNe4LyEQ/edit).** First-person human-centered document reasserting that the person must not disappear behind the architecture. Attribution and adoption should be audited, even where the voice is close.

**Current book addition:** [Eternity Is the Experience](18A_ETERNITY_IS_THE_EXPERIENCE.md), drafted from the evidence above in new first-person prose. It does not claim reality is simulated, consciousness has been transferred, that AI necessarily seeks experience, or that any future civilization has already achieved rendered existence. Original research statements are not automatically promoted to present-day beliefs, and private family history is not reproduced.

## Open corrections

1. Father's recordings: existence and survival of any recordings require reconciliation across sources.
2. Specific rebirth quotations: original speaker and exact wording not yet verified.
3. Cosmology: distinguish Noah's hope, philosophical exploration and any settled view.
4. Family privacy: previously public references do not authorize new private disclosures.
5. Technical status: manuscripts and proposals are not proof of deployed systems, filings or validated results.
6. Editorial voice: first-person drafts must remain open to Noah's rewrites, even where they sound close.
7. Completion: the 25-chapter / ~100,000-word goal remains open. These are early chapters, not the whole book.

**Publication boundary:** This is a public repository, including branches. Do not merge or treat candidate book prose as approved without Noah's review.
