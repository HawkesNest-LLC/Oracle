## Canon specification
Jupiter Station operates a Temporal Core that deliberately observes resolvable temporal and quantum branches without transporting the station through time. The Core is not a Temporal Drive and is not omniscient. To acquire information, REG must resolve a causal address, establish a controlled observational lock, distinguish measured evidence from reconstruction, capture read-only records, validate their origin, and preserve all conflicting evidence.

Functional schematic:
CAUSAL ADDRESS -> OBSERVATIONAL LOCK -> ISOLATED CAPTURE -> REG PROVENANCE REVIEW -> MEMORY ALPHA / MEMORY BETA -> COLD LEDGER -> RIGHTS AND COMMAND AUTHORIZATION

Memory Alpha protects the active Federation historical record. Memory Beta preserves adequately attributed records from other branches. The Cold Ledger preserves originals, conflicts, and decision receipts. Alternate histories are not false simply because they are not ours. The fictional source describes an order of magnitude near a million Federation-origin repository domains, not a million complete universes.

Proposed Mark I hardware: independent chronometric reference array, causal-address resolver, subspace phase interface, isolated observation buffer, origin validator, continuity firewall, separate Alpha/Beta gateways, witness and command arbitration console. These are story-engineering functions, not real-world specifications.

REG may compare solutions from different Federations and propose Synthetic Temporal Technology, but extraction and deployment require rigorous review. Identity reconciliation requires consent and rights review. History must not be overwritten. The Elias Venn case remains the cautionary event in the source.

Hawkes Doctrine: We are allowed to know more than we are allowed to do.

Command: Captain Hawkes commands USS Avalon; Captain Ashley commands Jupiter Station; Jake Sisko witnesses independently; REG reports evidence and uncertainties. REG renders. Hawkes arbitrates. Jake witnesses. Avalon carries the receipt.

Source protection: Memory Alpha, Memory Beta, screen history, Star Trek Online, and alternate histories remain available for examination. Source preservation is not automatic adoption of contradictory events. Picard 2399-2401 is protected future for story planning, without granting 2397 personnel unauthorized foreknowledge. The Eye of the Planet and Borg connection remains unresolved.

## Proposed mission slate (not canonized events)
1. The Other Avalon: a verified branch describes a ship whose crew never joined ours; a distress call raises the question whether answering constitutes interference.
2. The Missing Second: the discrepancy from the 2395 refit appears in a foreign archive; Engineering seeks an explanation without inventing a cause.
3. A Million Federations, One Dinner: cultural exchange from consented alternate Federation recipes becomes a surprisingly dangerous diplomatic banquet.
4. The Borg Who Asked to Leave: a former drone in another history demands the right not to be studied.
5. The Iconian's Receipt: two genuine alternate archives dispute who owned an ancient gateway tied to the Progenitors.
6. Tomorrow That Never Came: REG sees a future where Jupiter Station is lost, and Captain Ashley refuses to treat a prediction as guilt.
7. Ghosts of the Enterprise: an uncommissioned Enterprise's log holds a discovery and a casualty list with familiar names.
8. Q and the Index: a record purportedly signed by Q plays tricks on REG's definition of chronological order.
9. The Venn Appeal: a rights-bearing holographic person challenges unauthorized identity integration.
10. Jake's Impossible Headline: Jake must explain a million repository domains without claiming a million complete universes.
11. The War We Didn't Fight: a Star Trek Online branch offers a solution to an Iconian conflict at a steep diplomatic cost.
12. The Last Light on Deck Four: a station power isolation drill becomes a domestic comedy while raising serious questions about recording private lives.

Missions are candidates requiring Noah.Physical's later selection. Preserve the earlier source as originally labeled candidate; this new document records the explicit 2026-10-11 promotion of core capability, principles, and governance.
