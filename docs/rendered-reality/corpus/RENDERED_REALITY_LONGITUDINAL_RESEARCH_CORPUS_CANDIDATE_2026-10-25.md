## Abstract

This paper reconstructs the documented development of Rendered Reality and associated systems from surviving historical archives, 2025 LEGACY.GI material, correspondence, research manuscripts, 2026 corrections, and selected GitHub evidence. It separates the original broad ambition to preserve identity and meaning from a later measurable program for preserving decisions, corrections, time, relationships, provenance, and uncertainty. The central hypothesis is that trustworthy longitudinal AI requires inspectable, reversible, human-governed continuity, not merely fluent memory-like outputs. This is an intellectual history and proposed evaluation framework, not proof of consciousness transfer or validated digital immortality.

## Contradiction and correction register

1. **LEGACY.GI versus the six scores:** The five-layer 2025 architecture predates the later six-dimensional measurement ruler. The corrected October master record explicitly warns against equating them. Historical state retained; current distinction recorded.
2. **Single account versus single speaker:** The corrected master record establishes household sharing. Earlier user turns must be attributed individually, not assumed to belong to Noah.
3. **Digital immortality versus evidence preservation:** Early post-biological aspirations and later empirical framing coexist in the documentary history. Their precise relationship is not fully resolved by an AI-authored retrospective synthesis.
4. **Theory versus working code:** Architecture diagrams, case studies, and autonomy claims require repository commits, execution logs, and reproducible tests. The ORACLE self-patch discrepancy remains a specific audit target.
5. **AI-written first person versus original testimony:** A reconstructed first-person book can be adopted as authored work without becoming evidence of the exact words spoken years earlier.
6. **Publication approval versus blanket authorization:** The October 23 Noah.Public approval covers a redacted manuscript, not all underlying private archives.
7. **Patent correspondence versus patent grant:** A prior-art discussion and stated disclosure date do not prove issuance, filing completion, or enforceable claims.
8. **Speculative physics versus experimental science:** Quantum proposals, Original Entropy Shape, and Light Compression Law require operational definitions and falsifiable tests before strong scientific claims.

## Empirical evaluation program

**Dataset design.** Assemble timestamped, speaker-attributed records with consent and redaction. Preserve original text and metadata separately from summaries. Use independent reviewers to label decisions, corrections, uncertainty, relationships, and source dependencies. Deduplicate copied lineages before splitting datasets.

**Baselines.** Compare chronological retrieval, generic summarization, semantic vector retrieval, and structure-preserving compression at matched storage or context budgets. Record compute and annotation cost. Never compare a model with access to later corrections against a baseline that was denied them.

**Metrics.** Score correct attribution, decision state, correction survival, temporal ordering, relationship preservation, provenance precision, uncertainty calibration, unsupported-memory rate, and contradiction detection. Report inter-rater reliability and confidence intervals. Treat any composite score as a constructed metric, not a measurement of personhood.

**Adversarial tests.** Insert persuasive but false biographical claims; copied AI interpretations; contradictory versions; family-account speaker ambiguity; retrospective date confusion; and simulated consent revocation. A trustworthy system should refuse unsupported promotion even when a fabricated story is emotionally compelling.

**Implementation audit.** Trace named modules to source code, commits, test suites, runtime configuration, logs, and independently reproducible checks. Record what was planned, generated, committed, executed, and observed as separate states.

**Publication rule.** Negative findings, failed replications, missing sources, and uncertainty must be included in the results. No benchmark claims are made by this manuscript because the proposed evaluation has not been run here.
