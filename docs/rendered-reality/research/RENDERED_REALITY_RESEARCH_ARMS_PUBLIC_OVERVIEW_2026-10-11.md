# Rendered Reality: Continuity Research Arms (Public Working Overview)

**Origin:** Research direction and human editorial authority, Noah A. Hawkes. This edition is AI-drafted for review.
**Status:** Research proposal / candidate; not empirical results or canon promotion.
**Date:** 2026-10-11.

## Research premise
Can external records, provenance-aware retrieval, and human-governed correction improve continuity across chat interruptions, compression, and model changes? This is separate from consciousness, personhood, identity equivalence, or claims of post-biological survival.

## Ten research arms
1. **Provenance and witness:** attribute every claim and draft to the actual source, version, author, and correction chain. Track false authorship.
2. **Correction fidelity:** apply later authorized changes while retaining historic variants. Track superseded-canon errors.
3. **Temporal and relational continuity:** preserve event order, who commands or knows what, and which relationships are established.
4. **Experiential fidelity:** measure whether event reconstruction retains relevant relationships, meaning, and grounded details without inventing historical speech.
5. **Cross-thread recovery:** test retrieval after context breaks; score source precision, source recall, omissions, unsupported guesses and avoidable user re-briefing.
6. **Privacy and publication:** separate private family and manuscript records from public research abstracts; require artifact-specific consent.
7. **Identity modeling limits:** measure how well documented preferences and editorial judgments can be predicted without equating imitation with a person's identity.
8. **Governed world simulation:** track simulated offscreen events separately from creator-approved story canon, with rollback and source trails.
9. **Compression and transfer:** compare continuity preservation at matched budgets and with independent held-out tests.
10. **Human editorial correction:** preserve rejection history and measure whether later drafts meaningfully honor explicit corrections.

## Experimental design
Use versioned source manifests and hashes. Freeze all evaluation material before testing. Require two or more independent reviewers for a shared sample and report inter-rater agreement. Include adversarial cases covering wrong-person, false-authorship, uncertainty becoming certainty, generated prose becoming alleged memory, stale timeline facts, and mixed fictional worlds. Report negative findings and missing sources.

The **48-probe Round 1 v0.2 calibration packet** recorded in Noahhawkes/oracle-ai-runtime@4088e70dde975a1e4b9371aecae7f11b5a37483a is calibration-only and excluded from future confirmatory use. Its existence is not a demonstrated effect.

## Wrong-thread case study
A real multi-project conversation was distributed across separate chat contexts, motivating a source-recovery design case. Assess whether an AI can identify relevant archives, apply later role and chronology corrections, keep creative inventions distinct from events, and disclose missing platform transcripts. Since this case shaped the methods, it is exploratory rather than a blinded confirmatory benchmark.

## Boundaries and next steps
Storage is not retrieval; retrieval is not fidelity. A commit is evidence of publication, not acceptance. A story-world temporal archive is not a deployed research system. A convincing AI persona is not proof of human identity continuity.

Next: finish the claim schema, score calibration items independently, register a genuinely unseen benchmark, compare baseline compression with source-linked retrieval, and publish transparent limitations.
