# Continuity, Not Cloning: Master Manuscript Production Blueprint

**Authorial authority:** Noah Alexander Hawkes (Noah.Physical)
**Status:** Production blueprint, not a completed paper
**Target:** 20,000–25,000 substantive words, approximately 50–75 formatted pages
**Scope:** Independent research only. Exclude EcoWater and all employer-specific examples.

## Research method

Read original source texts and preserve document versions, authorship, evidence and uncertainty. Separate design proposals from implemented software and historical test receipts from independently rerun tests. Check external scientific claims against primary literature. Write each section in full prose with claim-level citations and one concluding sentence stating what it proves. Compare against simpler baselines, do not assume the five-layer stack wins. Preserve revisions rather than silently overwriting them.

## Detailed chapter plan

### 1. Title and Abstract

State the bounded thesis and verdict. Define the distinction between Continuity Intelligence, Continuity Computing, and the Continuity AI product. Reject unsupported mind-uploading claims and disclose that longitudinal testing remains incomplete.

### 2. Table of Contents

Preserve the nineteen-section structure and show the route from prior research to literature, architecture, implementation, falsification, and appendices.

### 3. Introduction

Tell the solo-builder story. Define persistent state, human authority, candidate versus canon, historical versus current state, provenance, and UNKNOWN. Connect LEGACY.GI, ORACLE, SOV1, and Rendered Reality without treating proposals as deployed systems.

### 4. Literature Review

Synthesize catastrophic forgetting, EWC, replay, LoRA, hybrid memory, long-horizon agents, sleep-time computation, and verification. Compare peer-reviewed findings with preprints and vendor claims. Include evidence against complex memory systems.

### 5. Core Problem

Explain why archives alone cannot preserve meaning. Analyze corrections, time, authority, duplicate evidence, and false consensus. Critically examine the author's rate-distortion framework.

### 6. What Works in 2026

Review episodic and semantic memory, knowledge graphs, retrieval, checkpointing, consolidation, and deterministic tests. Separate individual component demonstrations from unproven six-month reliability.

### 7. What Does Not Work as Advertised

Analyze compression limits, EEG signal limitations, invasive BCI trials, FlyWire and MICrONS, mind uploading, neuromorphic hardware, and correlated self-verification failures. State exactly what is and is not demonstrated.

### 8. Five-Layer Continuity Stack

Specify append-only records, episodic plus graph memory, semantic consolidation, procedural memory, and deterministic verification with human approval. Compare against event sourcing and temporal databases.

### 9. Data Model and Memory Schema

Define identifiers, actor, authorship, source hash, event and validity timestamps, confidence, contradictions, supersession, expiry, permissions, and privacy. Provide machine-readable examples and invariants.

### 10. Consolidation Pipeline

Explain ingestion, frozen evidence window, candidate extraction, contradiction checks, merge, retire, promote, approval, commit, rollback, and regression testing. Distinguish manual iPhone operation from autonomous execution.

### 11. Evaluation Suite

Compare stateless, long-context, simple retrieval, and five-layer systems. Specify matched packets, blinded scoring, contradiction and rollback tests, provenance coverage, token efficiency, backward transfer, and 30/90/180-day evaluation.

### 12. Solo Builder Evolution

Describe Stage 0 Remember Correctly, Stage 1 Consolidate, Stage 2 Generalize and Act, and Stage 3 Co-evolve. For each give build scope, subscription-only tools, success metric, and kill criterion.

### 13. Solo Builder Playbook

Provide exact Markdown file structure, daily closeout, weekly consolidation, cross-model handoff, source ledger, and phone copy-paste prompts. Include recovery from provider changes and interruptions.

### 14. Risks and Mitigations

Investigate fossilization, drift, false continuity, provenance laundering, privacy, right to forget, model dependency, consent, and identity fragmentation. Explain governance and technical controls.

### 15. What Would Prove Me Wrong

Specify measurable falsifiers including simpler baseline victory, harmful consolidation, excessive maintenance, and failure to preserve corrected state. Distinguish architecture failure from failure of the broader research question.

### 16. Conclusion

Argue for accountable continuity as a useful product, not a frozen imitation of a person. Return to the evidence and state the next falsifiable experiment.

### 17. References

Verify author, year, title, venue, DOI or primary link, and publication status. Separate external literature from the author's original design documents.

### 18. Appendix A

Show a full illustrative sleep-cycle memory diff with before and after state, authority, provenance, rollback, and tests. Label synthetic examples explicitly.

### 19. Appendix B

Provide complete iPhone-ready prompts for ingestion, closeout, consolidation, approval, contradiction audit, regression, checkpoint restoration, and cross-model handoff.
