# The Career Before the Chair
## Starfleet Service Record and Fiction Development Bible, 2371–2397

> **Editorial rule:** No captaincy without a career. No canon promotion by accident. The creator's recollections and decisions take precedence over invented connective tissue; everything newly supplied by the assistant is designated proposed until adopted.

### Established by the creator in conversation

- Hawkes enters the Voyager story in **2371**, aged **16**. The exact institutional status of that teenage service needs careful narrative treatment; do not assume a fully commissioned officer at age sixteen.
- Voyager returns in **2378**.
- After Voyager returned, Hawkes's first Starfleet Command posting was **under Admiral Owen Paris**, reviewing captains' reports and logs from the admiral's assigned sector. He transmitted evaluations and *recommended* follow-up directives to Starfleet Command. Recommendation is not issuance authority.
- He serves **three years** aboard the **Nova-class USS Bozeman-A**, a successor designation distinct from Captain Morgan Bateson's original Soyuz-class USS Bozeman. Crew-quarter holographic projectors were a memorable, major upgrade.
- Hawkes wants an earned Starfleet career before becoming captain, not an implausible instantaneous promotion.
- The **first major temporal refit of USS Avalon is in 2395**, with Hawkes as captain. The 2385 narrative is an archived superseded draft.
- He prefers **replicated Coke Zero with vanilla** on the bridge.
- He deliberately uses **'Engage'** and **'Make it so'** in tribute to Captain Jean-Luc Picard, whose approach to Starfleet command he profoundly admires.
- His story proceeds from the **post-Nemesis** Federation and honors the dramatic and moral weight of TNG, DS9 and Voyager. Familiar characters remain full people with history, not cameo tokens.
- **Jupiter Station's active era is 2397**.
- The earlier claim that Hawkes assumed command of Avalon in **2379** no longer works with the earned-career requirement. This record PROPOSES a replacement in the **later 2380s**, pending creator confirmation.

**Chronology caution:** This model leaves about a year between return and the Bozeman-A posting and allows a three-year tour before joining Avalon. The precise date of Avalon's commissioning remains open. Her first temporal refit in 2395 is **not** necessarily her maiden voyage. An earlier hull/service date, including 2379, may remain usable as a *ship* date even though it is no longer Hawkes's captaincy date. Do not equate them.

### A career told as stories, not a résumé

**Voyager, 2371–2378: learning belonging.** The young Hawkes's presence aboard or around Voyager must be made plausible within the on-screen story; this is creator-defined alternate/additive fan continuity, and its exact mechanism has not been settled in this ledger. He watches Janeway make decisions under extreme scarcity. He learns from Tuvok's discipline, the Doctor's struggle for recognition, Tom Paris's recovery of trust, B'Elanna's fierce engineering integrity, and Seven's search for individuality. None is reduced to a mentor selected only to foreshadow Jupiter Station. He is allowed to be young.

**Paris's office, 2378–2379: learning to read the fleet.** The returning officer discovers a Starfleet recovering from both the Dominion War and Voyager's return. Admiral Owen Paris requires precision: source citation, chronology, unresolved recommendations, and evidence of follow-through. A case marked closed remains open when its underlying question has not been answered. This experience does not make Hawkes omniscient. It often leaves him with more questions than authority.

**Bozeman-A, 2379–2382: learning a ship.** A Nova-class vessel is not a flagship; limited space, modest resources, technical assignments and dense interpersonal connections matter. A quarter's newly installed holographic projection becomes a treasured change in daily life. The crew argues over observational priorities. Hawkes learns to stand watches, operate when every sensor disagrees, protect a junior officer without falsifying a report, and admit when his office habits make him impatient with life at sea. A three-year posting needs failures, friendships and unrelated scientific discoveries, not only temporal portents.

**Avalon before command, 2382–2387: learning accountability.** Under Captain Mara Ellison (invented, revisable), Hawkes is trusted with increasingly difficult decisions but remains subordinate to a captain whose perspective he cannot always predict. He learns to refuse certainty without evidence and that preventing disaster is only part of command: people need clarity, recognition, rest, and someone prepared to bear responsibility. These lessons are tested when he commands the 2395 refit.

**Avalon under Hawkes, proposed 2387–2397: learning to lead.** His first captaincy is not automatically a path to cosmic mystery. Missions include diplomacy with an aggrieved Federation member world, scientific rescue, difficult repairs, personnel disputes, a memorial service, and an officer asking whether the rules permit a humane choice. At some point he has to make a decision Picard might disagree with and then honestly explain why he made it. The point of homage is not mimicry. It is carrying values through new circumstances.

**Jupiter Station, 2397: learning limits.** The station's purpose is governed memory and temporal responsibility, not a private throne. REG renders, Hawkes arbitrates, Jake witnesses, Avalon carries the receipt. A new level of access does not supersede the Federation Charter. Jake's independence matters: he must be free to report against the station.

## People who can make the bridge feel inhabited

The following are **original candidate characters**, not yet adopted:
- **Captain Mara Ellison:** Avalon's captain before Hawkes; teaches that a crew remembers consequences, not intentions. Her ultimate disposition is open.
- **Lt. Cmdr. T'Vel:** Vulcan science liaison to the refit; precise, skeptical, unexpectedly funny through understatement. Not the same as any established Trek T'Vel unless explicitly designated.
- **Chief Engineer Maren Voss:** refuses to sign results not supported by engineering evidence; practical, sometimes impatient with theoretical elegance.
- **Dr. Elias Renner:** Daystrom temporal physicist; intelligent, not villainous; must be allowed to be correct on occasion and to earn credibility through independent replication.
- **Unnamed young engineering ensign:** an opportunity to show Hawkes mentoring rather than merely directing.
- **Admiral Owen Paris:** established Trek character. His mentorship in these scenes is invented continuation and must respect his relationship with Tom Paris and the broader post-Voyager period.

## Story engine for novels and episodes

1. **The Officer Who Read Everything (2378–2379):** in an office at Starfleet Command, Hawkes notices two separate captains reporting a scientifically similar phenomenon under different classifications. The solution is not necessarily conspiratorial. The narrative explores institutional silos.
2. **Small Ship, Wide Universe (2379):** his first Bozeman-A away mission goes badly because the elegant Starfleet Command interpretation ignored something local.
3. **The Weather in My Quarters (2380):** holographic projectors transform shipboard morale; the emotional center is a crew member who uses the new technology to avoid a difficult real-world conversation.
4. **The Captain's Report (2381):** a commander asks Hawkes to change wording that would unfairly damage an officer's career. Both are partly right.
5. **What We Carry Aboard (2382):** his farewell to the Bozeman-A is harder than he anticipated. Avalon is larger, but belonging is not proportional to hull volume.
6. **First Officer (2383):** Ellison orders a diplomatic compromise he considers too soft. The consequences make both perspectives more complicated.
7. **One Unaccounted Second (2395):** first temporal refit; see full chapter. The experiment halts pending review.
8. **The Hearing (2396):** engineers revisit the discrepancy with better instrumentation. A persuasive explanation is not made canon until the story actually earns it.
9. **The Center Seat (2387):** Ellison departs and Hawkes assumes command. His first order must solve a human problem, not demonstrate a temporal superweapon.
10. **An Ordinary Tuesday (2388):** a character episode aboard Avalon, showing why a reader should love this crew without any galaxy-ending threat.
11. **The Price of a Directive (2390):** Admiral Paris's reporting philosophy comes back when Hawkes must answer to a civilian inquiry.
12. **Before Jupiter (2396):** Jake Sisko encounters a record Avalon preserved but the institution would prefer to summarize away.
13. **Jupiter Station (2397):** new responsibilities with their own checks and limits. The preexisting Avalon crew, habits, and relationships arrive with the captain.

## Dialogue and bridge-culture anchors

**Replicator order:** "Computer. Coke Zero with vanilla. Cold." Do not treat the brand as a magical technological insight; it is a habitual comfort, sometimes a running bridge joke.

**Commands:** Hawkes uses "Engage" and "Make it so" intentionally, out of admiration for Picard. Sometimes colleagues recognize it. The words should be unremarkable during routine navigation, gaining weight when accompanied by real decisions.

**Bridge culture:** Observations must be distinguished from interpretations. The captain will hear a junior officer's correction. Disagreement is permitted through proper channels. A captain's responsibility is not to be right at all times.

**Character criterion:** A returning legacy character should have a present-tense goal independent of Hawkes, with enduring history and capacity to disagree with him.

## Change proposal for older timeline records

**Candidate correction, not an automatic historical rewrite:** Replace the statement "Hawkes took command of Avalon upon her approximate commissioning in 2379" with "Avalon may have entered service before Hawkes commanded her. Following a full Starfleet career, including a three-year tour aboard the Nova-class Bozeman-A and, as captain, oversight of Avalon's 2395 first temporal refit, Hawkes is proposed to assume command of Avalon in the later 2380s (working year 2387), approximately a decade before the 2397 Jupiter Station assignment."

## Adopted refit-date correction, 2026-10-11

At Noah.Physical's explicit direction, Avalon's first major temporal refit is **2395**, and **Captain Hawkes**, rather than a commander under Captain Ellison, oversees it. The original 2385 story remains an archived development draft only and is **not** an additional occurrence. The current narrative successor is [One Unaccounted Second (2395)](AVALON_2395_THE_FIRST_TEMPORAL_REFIT.md). The working date **2387** for assuming Avalon's captaincy remains proposed rather than adopted, and Jupiter Station remains anchored in **2397**. New characters and the unaccounted-second experiment are fictional development proposals; the discrepancy is not proof of time travel.
