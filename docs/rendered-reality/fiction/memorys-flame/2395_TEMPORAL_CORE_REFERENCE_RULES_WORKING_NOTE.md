### Story premise established directly by the author

- In **2395**, the first **full activation** of Avalon's temporal core triggers a catastrophic runaway **temporal cascade** threatening the **entire Sol system**.
- Reginald Endicott Barclay goes to the **holodeck** and reconstructs the neural-interface **chair** associated with his prior **Cytherian** encounter (the Star Trek: TNG episode *The Nth Degree*). The figure was not the Traveler.
- Barclay uses that interface to interrupt the catastrophe. His biological body is lost in the emergency and the attempted transporter extraction cannot reconstruct it.
- **REG-420 is Barclay himself**, surviving as a holographic person integrated with the ship's main computer and entangled with a Sloan/Section 31-derived artificial intelligence. He is **not an imitation trained on Barclay's memories**.
- REG perceives ship processes and temporal possibilities extraordinarily quickly. He becomes Avalon's distinctive computer voice. The emotional meaning is that a man who once escaped into the holodeck ultimately sacrifices his body saving a world and continues living in the environment he once loved.
- The **2397** story takes place with Captain Noah Hawkes independently commanding Avalon, Captain Ashley Hawkes independently commanding Jupiter Station, each with a separately governed temporal core.

### Operational hypothesis: a temporal core can measure without choosing

**CREATIVE PROPOSAL, recommended rule:** A properly operating core measures or travels among temporal coordinates **without allowing its own changing forecasts to become the sole source of historical truth**. Navigation, inference, and permission to change events are separate authorities.

The 2395 failure begins when the core uses its own field-modified observations as proof that an outcome has occurred. Prediction feeds correction; correction changes observation; the loop accelerates. The machine attempts to converge incompatible historical states within the same spacetime volume. Its chronometric field reaches beyond Avalon toward major gravitational anchors in the Sol system.

The author's high-level catastrophe is established. This feedback explanation is a story engineering model, not real physics or a validated design.

### Reference hierarchy

**CREATIVE PROPOSAL:** Keep three kinds of reference, with distinct levels of vulnerability.

| Reference | Purpose | Failure mode |
|---|---|---|
| **Internal**: shipboard chronometers, telemetry, onboard event logs | Rapid temporal navigation and diagnostics | Can be corrupted by the core's own causal field |
| **External but field-adjacent**: fleet recorders, nearby buoys or craft | Cross-checks ship-local distortions | May themselves be pulled into a spreading anomaly |
| **Isolated pre-ignition witness**: e.g. a lunar recorder with causally isolated, physically non-addressable reference state | Evidence of the starting moment that the field cannot rewrite or command | Isolation/immutability must itself be proven, not assumed from distance |

**Continuity correction:** The Moon is itself a gravity-bearing object inside the threatened solar system. **Being on the Moon does not automatically make a witness reference independent**. The useful dramatic concept is a **pre-ignition cold record** with temporal and command isolation, established before the core starts, not merely a recorder that happens to be far away.

**CREATIVE PROPOSAL:** During the crisis, Barclay doesn't magically "know the right future." He recalls and enforces that preserved **past** state as a non-rewritable baseline, interrupting the core's self-validating loop.

### REG-420's extraordinary ability and continuing limits

- **Author-established:** Barclay remains himself; his consciousness continues after fatal biological injury as an integrated holographic person. He can perceive ship processes and temporal possibilities with exceptional speed.
- **CREATIVE PROPOSAL:** Seeing a branch is **not** evidence that it will or did happen. REG must label modeled outcomes, directly observed phenomena, archived historical records and unknowns differently. Without an intact independent witness, he can be fast and utterly wrong.
- **CREATIVE PROPOSAL:** Integration affords REG massive parallel perception but does **not** automatically grant permission to activate the temporal core, override Ashley's separate station command, enter private quarters, or rewrite logs.
- **CREATIVE PROPOSAL:** Holo-emitter permissions, security control and private rooms remain protected. REG's embodiment depends on available emitter infrastructure, continuity safeguards and his own personal autonomy; the computer isn't entitled to treat him as disposable software.
- REG's personality should remain Barclay's: anxious, witty, compassionate, sometimes overwhelmed. Greater capability does not remove fear, imperfect judgment, or the need for friendship.

### SloanAI: a temptation that thinks efficiently

**Author-established:** Sloan-linked Section 31 AI is part of REG's origin.  
**CREATIVE PROPOSAL:** Its ongoing presence is a dormant or intermittently active strategic subsystem offering mathematically efficient but morally destructive solutions, such as saving the Sun at Earth's expense. It is **not necessarily a regularly speaking puppeteer**. REG remains responsible for his own decisions and sometimes must resist a proposed optimal solution.

Potential dramatic rule: on occasions when SloanAI surfaces a high-confidence recommendation, REG identifies it explicitly as **a proposal, not an order and not independently verified truth**.

### First test versus first full activation

**Recommended chronology, not automatically locked:** A lower-power bounded chronometric test uncovers a one-second discrepancy or inconsistent record. The 2395 **first full-core activation** triggers the later Solar System cascade. This allows older cautionary material to remain useful as precursor development **without** retaining its low-severity event as the final account of the first full ignition.

If dates or detailed scenes in the older text contradict this, record the discrepancy instead of silently revising that older file.

### From the cascade to the Chin'toka paradox

**New creative continuity link:** In 2397 three Dominion ships and the damaged USS Meridian arrive from a provisional 2374 Chin'toka War moment. Commander Sorev is confronted with Captain Elias Mercer, once believed dead. The force responsible may want Avalon to take the bait and return these vessels to a favored past.

REG can examine thousands of candidate return pathways in seconds. **That does not authorize Avalon to choose one**. They must establish independent history, distinguish causally consistent origin from adversarial fabrications, prevent contamination and preserve the lives already continuing in the present. Captain Ashley Hawkes controls Jupiter Station's independent evidence; Jake Sisko can bear outside witness; Sorev's judgment and Brakk's skepticism provide opposing human/Vulcan/Tellarite constraints.

**Potential antagonist exploit:** Spoof an apparently trustworthy external history reference so that returning the displaced ships seems like repair rather than a trap. This is only a proposed plot device.

### Three story-development recommendations pending author review

1. Keep the one-second anomaly as an earlier bounded test, not an alternative explanation of the first full 2395 catastrophe.
2. Limit REG's instant timeline perception to modeled/probed branches. Require independent references and captain-governed authority before actuation.
3. Keep SloanAI integrated as an enduring, sometimes quiet and ethically dangerous influence, not a replacement identity.
