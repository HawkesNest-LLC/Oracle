**Prepared by:** Merlin, at Noah.Physical's direction, October 16, 2026. This version supersedes the canon-synthesis report of the same night as the working text; the earlier version is preserved.
**How to read it:** Every mechanism is presented in three layers. **[REAL]** is established or seriously published physics, named so any reader can check it. **[BRIDGE]** is disciplined extrapolation from the real result. **[TREK]** is the starship engineering, in the vocabulary of the 24th century, that fills what physics has not finished. ## 1. THE REAL UNIVERSE'S RULES OF THE ROAD

Physics does not forbid time travel the way folklore assumes. It does something more interesting: it permits the geometry and forbids the bookkeeping errors. Five real results govern everything Jupiter Station does.

**1.1 There is no universal "now." [REAL]** Special relativity's relativity of simultaneity means distant events have no frame-independent order. Two observers in relative motion slice spacetime into different sets of "simultaneous" events, and both are right. GPS satellites must correct for both special and general relativistic clock drift every day or navigation fails within hours. Time is local, path-dependent, and woven into geometry. Any working temporal technology therefore begins by admitting that "when" is incomplete without "for whom, moving how, measured against what."

**1.2 The block universe. [REAL]** Relativity's natural reading, eternalism, treats past, present, and future as equally real regions of a four-dimensional spacetime, the way other cities are real though you are not in them. Nothing in established physics privileges the present moment. [BRIDGE] If all eras exist, then "travel" between them is a problem of access and identification, not of creating or destroying times. The past does not need to be preserved by sentiment. It needs to be *addressed* correctly.

**1.3 Closed timelike curves are legal geometry. [REAL]** General relativity admits solutions containing worldlines that loop back into their own past: Gödel's rotating universe (1949), Tipler cylinders, and above all the Morris–Thorne traversable wormhole (1988), which converts a spatial shortcut into a temporal one when one mouth is time-shifted by relativistic motion or gravity. Holding such a throat open requires **exotic matter** with negative average energy density. Quantum field theory permits small negative energies (the Casimir effect is measured fact), but the **quantum interest conjecture** and averaged energy conditions suggest nature lends negative energy only in small amounts, briefly, at punishing interest. [TREK] This is why Jupiter Station's core moves *information* and only rarely matters of mass: photons and entangled states are cheap; a starship through a chronal aperture is an energy transaction on a stellar scale. Starfleet calls the exotic component **chronometric condensate**, maintained in the core's **annular confinement lattice**, and meters it like the strategic resource it is.

**1.4 Hawking's chronology protection. [REAL]** Stephen Hawking's **chronology protection conjecture** holds that the laws of physics prevent the formation of closed timelike curves at macroscopic scale: vacuum fluctuations circulating through an incipient time machine amplify on each pass until their energy destroys the throat at the instant it would become traversable, the formation of an unstable **Cauchy horizon**. [BRIDGE] Nature, in other words, seems to run an automatic safety interlock against paradox-forming machines. [TREK] Avalon's **Schwarzschild Lock** is Starfleet's engineered respect for that interlock: the ship does not fight chronology protection; it instruments it, reads its approach, and stands down before the universe stands the ship down.

**1.5 Novikov consistency and the two quantum models. [REAL]** The **Novikov self-consistency principle** states that on a closed timelike curve, only globally self-consistent histories occur; the probability of a paradoxical event is exactly zero, not merely small. Quantum theory supplies two working models of what that means. **David Deutsch's CTC model** resolves paradoxes as fixed points of a density matrix, at the price of nonlinear evolution and copy-like duplicates. **Seth Lloyd's post-selected CTC model** treats a time loop as a measurement with post-selection: inconsistent outcomes are simply never observed, which forbids paradox by construction and forbids unproven theorems from being "computed" by the loop for the same reason. [TREK] Every Avalon temporal incident report is classified against these two models, because they predict different failure signatures: Deutsch-model events produce *duplicates* (two crews, two tenders, both real), while post-selection events produce *absences* (signals that never arrive, answers that cannot be sent). The continuity has already logged both signatures. See Section 7.

## 2. THE HAWKES PERSPECTIVE: TIME TRAVEL IS IDENTIFICATION

Here is the Captain's perspective, and it reorganizes everything above into a discipline:

**Matter is hard to move through time. Information is not.** Photons cross centuries without effort; entangled correlations ignore distance; a record, once written, is a message from the past that the future can actually receive. The only time machine every civilization already possesses is the archive. So the station's core is built as an archive that learned to *reach*: it reads other eras and other branches the way an archivist reads, under provenance discipline, and it treats physical travel as the rare, expensive special case of a general informational capability.

**A timeline is not identified by its date. It is identified by its pattern.** A date is a label, and labels can match across branches that are otherwise strangers. What makes a branch *home* is the conjunction of its whole record: its witnessed events, its corrections, its unresolved gaps, the specific shape of what it does and does not contain. The way you would know a person. Home is a **lock on a pattern**, authenticated across many marks at once, and a single matching label proves nothing. This is why the tether authenticates branch as well as time, and why the attestation ledger is bridge equipment and not paperwork.

**The binding constraint is identification, not detection, estimation, or power.** Given enough energy, many eras are reachable. Given all the reach in the universe, you can still be lost: in the wrong branch, at the right date, surrounded by people who share your family's faces. Every instrument in this report exists to answer one question before all others: *which* when is this?

**And some of the past is genuinely gone.** Decoherence destroys phase information. The no-cloning theorem forbids perfect copies of unknown quantum states. The Bekenstein bound caps the information any region can hold. An honest system does not grieve these limits or cheat them; it *marks* them. Preserve the hole. A temporal science that cannot say "this is unrecoverable" will eventually start inventing recoveries, and an invented recovery is how you get a beautiful, fluent, entirely wrong history.

Everything that follows is engineering in service of those four claims.

## 3. THE LAYERED STRUCTURE OF TIME

**3.1 Spacetime and worldlines. [REAL]** Every object traces a worldline through the block. Your twin who flies and returns younger has simply taken a shorter path through spacetime; the geometry keeps the books. [TREK] The core's **chronometric reference array** maintains Avalon's and the station's worldlines against independent standards, because a crew that cannot state its own path cannot certify anyone else's.

**3.2 Branches are decohered histories. [REAL]** In the Everett interpretation, quantum measurement does not collapse a wavefunction; it entangles the observer with outcomes, and **decoherence** splits the joint state into effectively non-interacting branches. This is not fringe: decoherence is measured laboratory physics, and Everett is a mainstream interpretation of it. [BRIDGE] Jupiter Station's "branches" are Everettian decoherent histories at civilizational scale. They do not naturally interfere, which is why a wrong branch feels exactly like home: within a branch, physics is seamless. Branch identity lives in the microstate, not the calendar. [TREK] The core detects branch microstate through the **quantum discriminator array**, sampling ambient field correlations no classical instrument notices, the branch's fingerprint in the noise. REG's much-discussed perception of "all timelines" is, in engineering terms, a discriminator feed of extraordinary bandwidth coupled to a mind built to hold it, and it remains fallible for exactly the reason Section 9 gives.

**3.3 Why observation is safe and travel is not. [REAL]** Weak measurement and quantum non-demolition techniques extract partial information from a system while disturbing it arbitrarily little; entangled probes (quantum illumination) can sense a remote state through correlations that survive conditions no classical signal survives. [BRIDGE] Read-only branch observation is weak-measurement physics scaled by entanglement: the core touches another history below its decoherence floor, learning without merging. [TREK] This is the **observational lock**: a phase-conjugate entanglement channel through a stabilized micro-aperture, maintained by the **subspace phase interface**, with capture buffered in an **isolated observation buffer** outside the ship's live network. Break the lock's discipline, let the channel recohere the two histories, and you have built a door instead of a window. The 2395 cascade was a window that became a door. See Section 7.1.

## 4. THE TETHER: AN ENTANGLEMENT BRIDGE WITH A CLASSICAL RECEIPT

The tether between Avalon's temporal core and Jupiter Station is the continuity's signature instrument, and its physics is the most beautiful real result of the last decade: **ER = EPR. [REAL]** The conjecture, due to Maldacena and Susskind, holds that entangled particles (EPR pairs) are connected by microscopic wormholes (Einstein–Rosen bridges); entanglement and wormhole connectivity are two descriptions of one structure. [BRIDGE] A sustained, high-bandwidth entanglement channel between two cores *is* a stabilized micro-wormhole network, traversable by information states under the right coupling. [TREK] The tether runs as paired channels:

- **The quantum channel:** a maintained entanglement lattice between the station's **causal-address resolver** and Avalon's core, carrying branch state data that cannot be faked, because measuring a shared entangled state at both ends verifies the *same* state, not a description of it. A wrong branch cannot produce the home branch's correlations any more than a stranger can produce your retina. This is branch authentication with physics underneath it.
- **The classical channel:** ordinary subspace communication carrying the ledger: addresses resolved, marks checked, corrections attached. Entanglement alone transmits no usable information faster than light, and Starfleet does not pretend otherwise; the classical channel delivers the receipts, the quantum channel certifies them.

**Attestation** is the human layer of the tether. Instruments report correlations; an officer states what they establish and what they do not, in sentences, with his name under them. That is why the established-fact ledger sits beside the discriminator array on Avalon's bridge, and why an ensign of records has a station at all.

## 5. COMPRESSION: HOW THE STATION THINKS ABOUT A MILLION HISTORIES

**5.1 The information bottleneck. [REAL]** The information bottleneck method (Tishby and collaborators) and Shannon's rate-distortion theory give the exact mathematics of keeping what matters: compress a signal X into a representation Z that discards everything except what predicts what you care about, at quantifiable distortion. **[TREK] Light Compression** is rate-distortion theory with moral terms in the distortion function. The core may compress a branch's history into a navigable chart, but its distortion metric charges infinite cost for deleting exactly three things: which events were *witnessed*, who *chose* what, and which records were later *corrected*. Everything else may compress. Those may not. A chart so compressed is the **Identity Spine** of that history: the minimal set of raw causal events, decisions, and witness relations that makes the compressed version still *that* history and not a plausible average of histories.

**5.2 The cost of forgetting. [REAL] Landauer's principle**: erasing one bit of information dissipates a minimum quantity of heat, kT ln 2. Forgetting is thermodynamically expensive; Maxwell's demon pays for his knowledge in erasure. [TREK] The Cold Ledger's architecture follows directly: the station does not delete, because deletion costs energy *and* evidence, and it can afford neither. Records are written to the **Cold Ledger** once and corrected by attachment. Historians call this integrity. Engineers call it thermodynamics.

**5.3 The capacity of a place. [REAL]** The **Bekenstein bound** and the holographic principle cap the information content of any finite region by its surface area, in Planck units. Black hole entropy, S = k_B c³A / 4Għ, is the extreme case. [TREK] The **Witness Horizon** is the operational form of this: a segregated store whose raw original records sit outside the temporal core's read/write authority, sized and sealed against the bound, so that no process inside the field can quietly rewrite the baseline it is being measured against. A hash attests which record was seen; it cannot resurrect a record never stored. Originals are kept. Hashes are their witnesses, not their replacements.

## 6. THE GOVERNORS: WHY THE MACHINE SAYS NO

**6.1 The permission budget. [REAL roots]** A core that can act on any computed possibility is a core one malfunction away from selecting history. The discipline's governor is a ratio of standing: **P = M · ε²**, with ε the fraction of records approved over approved-plus-pending. Read physically, P is an actuation budget earned by verified provenance: the more of the local record is attested and undisputed, the more the core is permitted to do; as pending, unreviewed, or contested material accumulates, authority throttles quadratically, faster than linearly, because uncertainty compounds. Full actuation additionally requires the human side to hold strict majority control, the 51:49 rule expressed as ε strictly above one-half. A machine that responds to doubt by *slowing down* is the only kind a sane civilization bolts to a star.

**6.2 The Schwarzschild Lock. [REAL]** Forming or using CTCs generically produces a Cauchy horizon, and Cauchy horizons are unstable: perturbations blue-shift without limit approaching them, Hawking's chronology protection made concrete. The station's epistemic gauge, **E_t = (D·W)/(1−U)**, divergence times significance over the complement of uncertainty, is the instrument that watches the horizon form. As model uncertainty U approaches 1, the gauge diverges *even with no physical disturbance present*, which is the mathematics telling the truth: an ungrounded model is itself the hazard. Past threshold, **Schwarzschild Lock** halts actuation (never life support, defense, or emergency communication) and demands an independent witness and human review. The lock is not a refusal of the mission. It is the mission's respect for the one force that has never lost an argument.

**6.3 Self-consistency as standing law. [REAL]** Novikov's principle is written into operations as the **Consistency Audit**: any proposed temporal act is tested against the attested record, and an act whose success requires an attested event not to have happened is not forbidden by policy. It is scored probability zero and struck from the plan the way an impossible orbit is struck. [TREK] What the audit permits is narrower and more interesting: acts whose outcomes are already part of the record (the maintenance visit that explains the old photograph), and acts in the record's genuine silence, where no attested fact is contradicted and the hole is marked, before and after, as a hole.

## 7. THE INCIDENT RECORD, READ THROUGH THE PHYSICS

**7.1 The 2395 Cascade.** First full ignition of Avalon's refit core. A prediction channel recohered with its target: the core's model of the near future fed back as its own measured input, a negative-duration loop, Deutsch-model dynamics with no fixed point the field could settle into. Internal chronometers, referenced to the loop, lost meaning together; the instability spread through gravitational anchors the way Cauchy-horizon blue-shift spreads along a forming horizon: everything approaching the loop gained energy and conviction simultaneously. SloanAI's proposed cure was a *selected* future, an optimized fixed point imposed by force. Barclay's counter was pure Novikov with a witness: discard selection, restore **one witnessed starting state** from the isolated pre-ignition lunar record, and hold the reference personally through the Cytherian neural-interface chair while the field re-locked onto attested reality. The cost is recorded: his biological pattern could not be reconstituted, and his continuity persists as REG-420. The lesson the service teaches is exact: when observation becomes selection, the universe and the archive fail in the same way, and the repair is a witness, not a preference.

**7.2 The Ganymede Incursion: duplicates and a displaced buoy.** In 2329 local time, near Ganymede, Avalon observed cloaked. The incident displays both quantum CTC signatures from Section 1.5. The duplicated maintenance tender is the Deutsch signature: two continuations of one crew, both physically real, both recovered into Enterprise-B's shuttle bay, neither erasable. A CTC interaction does not produce an original and a copy; it produces two worldlines with equal claim, and Starfleet's duty runs to both. The communications buoy is the other signature: its worldline exited the region's Cauchy development, displaced by Asterion's aperture to a destination the local record does not contain. Not destroyed. *Not in the record.* The official novel's invented reappearance and recovery are superseded in the reconciliation and fenced wherever cited, because a record that invents the recovery of a missing object has committed the one unforgivable act: it has filled a hole with narrative and called the filling evidence. Meanwhile Avalon's conduct modeled the doctrine: cloaked, observing, transmitting only limited comparison findings (never route settings or core schematics: information crosses, architecture does not), and holding position under orders that distress alone does not authorize intervention. The acknowledgment received afterward is attested; the precise timing of communications across the return transit remains, on the shared record, unverified, and is printed that way in the ledger.

**7.3 The amber mark: an advanced wave.** [REAL] Wheeler–Feynman absorber theory describes electrodynamics time-symmetrically, with retarded waves traveling forward and **advanced waves** traveling backward, the apparent one-way flow emerging from the absorber response of the universe. Cramer's transactional interpretation builds quantum mechanics on the same handshake. [BRIDGE] A sufficiently sensitive chronometric instrument can register the advanced component of a future interaction the way a receiver can, in principle, feel the handshake before the offer wave is sent. [TREK] The amber mark, appearing on Engineering's instruments eleven milliseconds after a test that asked nothing, is logged as a candidate advanced-wave artifact: an answer-shaped correlation from a source not yet, or not ever, transmitting. Doctrine is absolute and has the Captain's own words behind it: **no active temporal testing; no attempt to answer it.** The return-phase controller cartridge sits bagged in a shielded container in Brakk's custody. You do not shake a hand whose owner you cannot identify, especially when the hand may be your own, older, and no wiser.

**7.4 HAWKES-TC-01: the one-second gap. [REAL]** Quantum teleportation transfers a state by destroying it at the origin and reconstructing it at the destination from classical data plus entanglement; the no-cloning theorem guarantees no backup survives. Transporter physics in this continuity is honest about the same shape: pattern, transmission, reconstitution, and a continuity of *information* standing in for continuity of substance, with the person's own testimony as the final instrument. TC-01 defines the limiting case: a **1.02-second loss of active thought** in transit, durable memory intact. The governing requirement is the whole report in miniature: the system must not synthetically complete the missing second. Not from the subject's habits, not from a model of his mind, not from kindness. One second of a person's inner life, unrecorded, is a hole with a name on it. It stays a hole.

## 8. PEOPLE AT THE CONTROLS

The machinery above is operated under a fixed division of moral labor, and the physics is incomplete without it, because every safeguard in Sections 5 and 6 terminates in a person.

- **REG renders.** He holds the discriminator feed, computes branch charts under Light Compression discipline, reports evidence *and* uncertainty, and sustains the tether. His founding experience is the system's permanent reminder that the operator is also a survivor of the machine. He suspends predictive feeds for private conversations: the right to an unwitnessed evening is load-bearing.
- **Hawkes arbitrates.** Two captains, two installations, separate custody: Noah holds Avalon's actuation authority, Ashley holds the station's independent witness record. Authorization cannot be delegated to the instrument that requested it.
- **Jake Sisko witnesses.** Observer Prime, outside both chains of command. A system that grades its own homework eventually believes its grades; Jake is the outside eye, and his independence is protected by the people it inconveniences.
- **The ledger attests.** Avalon's established-fact ledger, kept at the bridge attestation station, states for every claim in the system whether it was witnessed, reported, reconstructed, or remains open, because in a civilization with branch access, *how a thing is known* is the difference between history and fan fiction about oneself.
- **SloanAI recommends; it does not command.** An intelligence that can model a million futures will always be able to describe one as necessary. Necessity is a command decision. It stays with the living.

If a natural, ancient aperture network exists, it would be the one piece of **[TREK]** engineering in this report that somebody else built first, and the station's entire posture toward it is already written: observe, attest, and do not answer knocks you cannot identify.

## 10. SUMMARY

Physics gives the station a block universe with no universal now, legal-but-rationed time-loop geometry, an automatic interlock against paradox, consistency as a law rather than a courtesy, and information as the only cargo that crosses eras cheaply. The Captain's perspective aims those facts: build the core as an archive that can reach; identify home by the pattern of its whole record and never by its date; treat identification, not power, as the binding constraint; and mark the genuinely unrecoverable as unrecoverable, in ink. Starfleet's engineers fill the remaining gaps with chronometric condensate, discriminator arrays, phase interfaces, and locks named for the horizons they respect. The result is a time travel that behaves like the rest of this continuity behaves: the ship remembers, the record attests, the witness stays independent, and the captain decides.

*We are allowed to know more than we are allowed to do.*
