## Major new narrative developments in this edition

- Asterion's recovery search advances its receiving endpoint through the retained departure history each time its integrator rejects changed conditions. The cumulative endpoint advance accounts for the thirty-three-year mismatch. This replaces multiple competing explanations with one inspectable fictional mechanism.
- Avalon's arrival is deliberately earlier than the call's embedded local timestamp, allowing observation of the routing failure that has already delivered a call to 2397. This clarifies event order without implying ordinary subspace communication moves backward in time.
- Leda's local service call is accidentally carried by the recovery relay. Avalon's automatic carrier receipt, sent before temporal classification, supplies an ordinary reply address, not a home-tether credential. Both ends' records and Jupiter's independent precursor capture explain the crossing.
- Mara Rusk is newly named. A fresh challenge and reference-succession review support a live exchange with the station. Her adult life is neither erased nor treated as disposable to restore the father's expected year.
- Asterion's recovery controller uses measured signatures to attempt an acquisition of Avalon's external reference. It cannot authenticate as Jupiter. Its interference threatens receiver margin, creating a direct danger to Avalon's safe withdrawal.
- Rusk physically severs the recovery lattice to release Avalon. He is injured, permanently loses that array, and authorizes a conventional last transit using his own ship and Meridian's authenticated beacon. He does not become harmless, forgiven, or acquitted.
- Enterprise and the other local ships contribute medical transport, traffic clearance, evidence custody, and independent command judgment. Their older weapons do not conveniently defeat superior future machinery.
- A fresh authenticated Meridian arrival acknowledgment is received through Asterion's closing carrier. The edition therefore establishes an observed successful arrival but does not invent the family's reunion, legal outcome, or the complete history after it.
- Ensign Davi and her emitter-repair scene are new supporting fiction. The captain allows additional repair time instead of exposing an engineer beneath a live unstable assembly.
- REG deliberately closes the temporal observation bus while keeping his active matrix powered. He later agrees to discuss the experience with the counselor. There is no fork, memory reset, new biological death, or claim that he loses identity.
