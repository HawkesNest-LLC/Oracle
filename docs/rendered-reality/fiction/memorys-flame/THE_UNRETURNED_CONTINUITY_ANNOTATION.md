## Deliberate changes from the outline

- Post-repair operational clearance occurs in the new opening. It is not backdated into the novel, whose epilogue still awaits final cloak clearance. Approximate stardate 74012 is a proposed episode placement, not a calculated date.
- The danger is coupling a foreign navigation reference to home, not admitting conflicting evidence into the archive. Foreign claims may be preserved without authenticating the requested succession.
- Rescue alternatives are tested before withdrawal. Transport, shuttle/field extension, and an independent anchor fail for explicit **new proposed fictional constraints**. These are not previously established universal limits, and independent review remains possible.
- A private recording is not played. The foreign captain voluntarily summarizes it. This retains the domestic stakes while avoiding unexamined playback or redistribution of private correspondence.
- Shared Ganymede evidence is a scoped, sealed copy with explicit omissions of routing credentials. Raw included blocks remain unmodified; source originals stay in custody. The evidence transfer receives separate station authorization and is recorded as an intervention.
- Other REG's record smoothing does not invalidate the rights or existence of his crew. It affects the reliability of particular claims. A lost address prevents current contact; it does not prove death or erase personhood.
- The closing answer states last confirmed survival, not present knowledge after contact loss. The crew does not declare rescue permanently forbidden.
- Three next-chapter paths are options only.
