1. **Relational checks supplement instruments.** "Trust that test above all of ours" conflicts with Promotion Order §3. Shared prior branches can share private memories. Proposed replacement: "Private relational checks may support the instrumental assessment. They do not uniquely identify a branch, and nobody is required to disclose their contents."
2. **Historical marks are scoped evidence.** A recovered-buoy claim might be an error, a later discovery, or evidence of a different history. It is not by itself proof that a station is foreign. Use: "Our last authenticated record leaves the buoy's destination and recovery unresolved. A different report requires origin and sequence review." Use "both tender continuations and crews were recovered aboard Enterprise-B; subsequent lives unprovided," not an unlimited present-tense survival claim.
3. **Authentication is more than lore matching.** A conjunction of known facts, even a long one, is corroboration, not a unique credential. Provide a scoped identity/evidence packet and an independent current reference exchange. Do not send raw private keys, tether control credentials, or assume whole-record comparison is complete.
4. **Transit and colony reception require checks.** Communication does not prove safe transit. Confirm a fresh colony beacon, observed access conditions, available reception capacity, destination consent, and local authority. Noah can offer to request a berth; he cannot promise another jurisdiction's berth before its acknowledgment. The receiving colony may grant refuge without proof of an exact original branch.
5. **Vargas III remains a proposed setting.** Its charter, attestation office, legal arrangements, and beacon are scene developments. New arrivals retain rights and may preserve names, culture, and records; their command structure operates within a consented host agreement, not automatic sovereignty over colony institutions. Inability to prove origin must not bar emergency assistance.
6. **The refuge beacon is not a substitute home.** It can offer its own stable destination reference and a public comparison record of a consented Avalon encounter. It does not lend Jupiter's authenticated home tether or turn every ship's branch separation into a measurable scalar "distance."
7. **Triage before reassurance.** "Nobody here is in a hurry" should become "Tell us first if anyone needs immediate assistance. Then take the time you need." Displacement does not establish absence of damage; phrase REG's initial observation as evidence available so far.
8. **Real transmission errors remain real errors.** A wrong command assignment from another AI can inspire an alternate-history fiction. It does not prove that the real system reached an actual quantum branch. Keep this transformation explicit in development notes.
9. **Historical attestation date requires a source.** The April 2025 triad claim remains source-attributed pending its primary record; do not make it an independently verified date merely because the fictional ship transmits it.

A revised candidate can keep the three-packet structure: a concise statement of welcome and values; scoped evidence plus an authenticated communication procedure; and a consented refuge referral after colony confirmation. Keep more dialogue on the bridge so the values emerge through choices rather than turning the entire hail into an archive recital.
