## Episode-resolution facts now part of this commissioned fictional edition

- The first damaged alternate Avalon disappears into the doorway after the five remaining people are rescued. **Thirty-seven survivors** from the damaged ship are recovered in total. **Dr. Adrian Hale**, medical officer aboard that damaged vessel, is the **one confirmed fatality aboard that ship**, not the Voyager Doctor. The name is newly supplied by the completed edition.
- The next, apparently crewless copied Avalon is destroyed or displaced after attacking Jupiter Station. Station-controlled Tug Six and the abandoned berth seventeen assembly are sacrificed; one station defense platform is destroyed and another disabled, with further repairs required.
- The original Cytherian interface headrest contains an unidentified seventh fitting. An independent station record from 2395 captured a three-and-four pulse before the temporal-core activation and a further response eleven seconds later. The literal cause of Reg's missing or unreconciled integration interval remains unresolved.
- Merlin identifies a secondary fluctuation in the third pulse of the 2395 station record and the present probe record, absent from the berth seventeen copy. Ellie detects a command-like attempt to substitute trusted command provenance. Both are independently meaningful findings, not proof of an adversary's inner motives.
- Captain Ashley protects Jupiter Station throughout the emergency and retains independent authority. The old sensor mast is removed and quarantined. The temporal tether remains disconnected at episode close pending renewed, independent authorization. Forty-one civilians in the threatened promenade are successfully evacuated, and a transport with twenty-nine civilians is protected.
- Ghost One and the later crewed shuttle Lark locate an unknown ringlike structure in Jupiter's upper atmosphere. An inner command-generating assembly is physically separated from an outer signaling structure. The command-like transmissions cease during the measured interval, the exterior threat ends, and the outer original three-and-four signal persists.
- Reg deliberately disconnects his holographic projection for forty-three seconds while Sorev and Solis complete the physical operation and escape. That span is deliberately **not narrated as directly witnessed by Reg**. Their observations arrive later through witness records.
- The biological Barclay's genetic and clinical resemblance does not resolve his provenance or identity. He and the injured second Captain receive individual protection, not automatic merging into existing identity records. The Doctor from Voyager remains Jupiter Station's chief medical officer.
- The episode ends with Avalon returning under a manually controlled, independently observed docking procedure; Jupiter Station remains a living family home; the main immediate threat is resolved, while deeper historical questions remain available for later stories.
