## What this episode adds to the adopted fictional continuity

1. The post-crisis cadence initially alternates **11.03 seconds** and **9.4 seconds**. Sparrow observes a repeatable pressure-wave return through Jupiter's atmosphere. The 9.4-second period is consistent with a physical resonant/ranging process of the outer ring. The longer period correlates with the earlier editor-linked fixed timing. No one equates this with a verified explanation of Reg's distinct eleven-second integration gap.
2. The previously removed sensor mast leaves a **quarantined module** retained on a service frame. It resonates physically, without a trusted digital or power connection. Captain Ashley orders the module's final detachment and remote towing. The module's observed resonance ceases, but the root response inside the central vault persists.
3. Jupiter Station's reference chamber is described as a historic **2350 reference-vault assembly** later incorporated into the present station's central spine. It contains **three independent atomic chronometers** within separate protected enclosures. The interior had not been physically entered since the 2395 crisis; the external observation and service passage remained accessible under manual controls.
4. The 2395 independent custody archive records the three-and-four pattern **17 minutes and 43 seconds** before Avalon's activation. This is prior to the catastrophic core ignition but does not on its own establish what initiated the field, whether a station material coupling then existed, or the builders of the Jovian ring.
5. A black material vein with ceramic three-and-four-grooved contacts is observed behind a long-sealed service panel and within the central chronometer housing. It forms an unwanted physical coupling between the Jovian field and the station's output/reference pathway. Its complete provenance and growth mechanism remain unknown.
6. The sealed cylinder recovered from the damaged Avalon is examined under inert read-only conditions. It contains the alternate Barclay's personal **approach log**, *not* the missing eleven seconds. His recorded testimony describes first receiving a 9.4-second navigation-like pattern and answering it with their trusted temporal reference as a courtesy. A longer fixed pattern and trusted-source overlay are recorded afterward. The cylinder's content has evidence value but its entire upstream truth is not retroactively certified.
7. The **thirty-seven survivors** are treated as people requiring housing, care, consent and dignity, not as automatic identities in a registry. **Mara Venn** speaks with both Barclays. The Doctor from Voyager serves as Jupiter Station's chief medical officer and attends the memorial for the *different* doctor, Hale.
8. **Merlin's second five questions** are retained as unresolved at their original asking: original ring audience, black-material resonance, chronological/physical arrival of coupling, contents of the cylinder (later opened), and risks of leaving the root undisturbed. The later discovery about the cylinder updates question four without implying all five were previously solved.
9. **Captain Ashley Hawkes** enters her own station's reference vault with Reg's limited projection, Sorev and Solis. Using manual shunts/dampers, Ashley physically cuts the root after Reg disconnects his optical projection. The gap is exactly **43 seconds**. Chapter Twelve is an explicitly attributed account from Ashley, not a memory Reg claims to have experienced.
10. The residual field collapses after a **0.8-degree station-attitude adjustment** under Ashley's command. The severed material is moved to quarantine. Station reference measurements resume as independent locally governed processes. The automatic Avalon tether remains disconnected.
11. Sparrow subsequently observes a dominant **9.4-second** outer-ring cadence without command-like overlay during the measured interval. The outer ring's behavior supports the functional nickname **listening archive**, retaining return timing. It is not proof of consciousness, an authenticated builder, or a recovered intended purpose. A weaker pre-Federation environmental observation is a historical lead, not a confirmed construction date.
12. The living biological Barclay keeps the name **Reginald Barclay** for medical records and asks friends to call him **Reggie**. That choice is his, without settling whether he and Commander Reg are numerically identical. The episode closes with a family dinner and a manual return to berth twelve under Captain Ashley's station authority.
