# Jupiter Station: Twenty Temporal Episodes
## Expansion-ready development bible

Creator authority: Noah Alexander Hawkes. Prepared October 14, 2026. AI-assisted treatments under creator direction.

**Status: CANDIDATE EPISODE SLATE.** These are complete development treatments, not twenty completed prose episodes or events already experienced by the cast. Each supplies a beginning, conflict, attempted solution, resolution, and continuing consequence. The creator may select, combine, revise, or reject any proposal. Identifiers are stable; sequence is not a locked season order.

## Instructions for writing an episode from this reference

Retrieve the requested ID or exact title and the controlling sources linked below. Write a substantial dramatic episode with scenes, sensory geography, distinct character voices, and earned decisions. Follow the treatment's causal sequence; do not merely summarize it. Use its cast and POV, keep character knowledge bounded, and distinguish proposed mechanisms from already established canon. Maintain ship and station command independence. Do not import unchosen endings or another candidate's events as prior history.

If length is unspecified, target 4,000-7,000 words for a full prose episode, scaling to the story rather than padding. For interactive chapters, end each chapter with three meaningful choices, labeled unchosen until selected. For a continuous episode, put optional continuation choices after the ending. Preserve uncertainty; add an editorial note for substantive inventions or changed outcomes. A missing source should be disclosed rather than reconstructed as remembered fact.

All new named guests, exact numerical tolerances, hardware mechanisms, legal rules, and temporal effects in these treatments are proposals unless independently established in a controlling source. Do not imply that authenticating evidence automatically authenticates identity, legal authority, or navigational access.

## Shared baseline

- Home is protected 2397. Ashley independently commands Jupiter Station and station-held evidence; Noah commands Avalon's missions and actuation. Family residence is on the station.
- Avalon is a modified Sovereign-class vessel, current registry NCC-75154-JS. Post-repair operation requires a new clearance scene or an adopted clearance, not a revision of an older pending repair log.
- REG-420 continues Barclay and is Avalon's first officer. Sorev is Vulcan science officer and second officer. Brakk leads ship Engineering. Ellie is a human-appearing holographic lieutenant junior grade with science AI/continuity duties.
- Voyager's Doctor is station CMO. Zimmerman is laboratory-side. Liora Renn is the counselor; Tessa zh'Renn is Ashley's station first officer. Do not conflate them.
- Eli works station security. Brooklyn is his Klingon fiancée and a Dabo employee. Ethan seeks welding training. Ender wants to be a chef; an alternate officer career is not a prediction or current assignment.
- Tavren, Rom and Leeta's half-Ferengi/half-Bajoran son, runs the Quark's franchise. Quark visits for inspections. Jake is an independent journalist and witness.
- The Temporal Core observes resolvable branches; it does not transport the station. Avalon's tether authenticates its home history. Memory Alpha protects active history; Memory Beta preserves attributed foreign records; the Cold Ledger retains originals, conflicts, and decision receipts.
- The Hawkes Doctrine permits more knowledge than intervention. It does not prohibit rescue, courtesy, correction, learning, or publication by default. Apply proportionate authority and demonstrated constraints.
- A record correction may append a supported amendment without erasing its predecessor. Conflicting evidence can be stored without coupling navigation references. Unverifiability does not erase personhood or prove death.
- Private correspondence, medical material, and family-adjacent captures receive scoped handling. Do not conceal operational hazards merely to create a dramatic secret.

## JS-T01: The Unreturned

**Origin:** User-supplied outline, upstream drafting authorship unspecified. Already expanded into [candidate episode](THE_UNRETURNED_CANDIDATE_EPISODE.md) with [annotation](THE_UNRETURNED_CONTINUITY_ANNOTATION.md). Approximately stardate 74012 is proposed placement.

**Cast and viewpoint:** Noah-centered, Ashley station-side; both REGs, Ellie, Brakk, Sorev, Imani; family coda. The other captain receives an equal dramatic presence rather than serving as a lesson.

**Opening:** After a new repair clearance, a residual structure in archived Ganymede evidence resolves to another Avalon. Its last handshake request is thirty-six station hours old and remains open from the other side. Avalon travels under its own home tether to an observational position.

**Development:** The foreign ship survived a different return but lost its anchor. Three subsequent corrections moved it farther from its original reference. Its REG has edited summaries to make contradictions less visible. Raw reports remain. Ashley permits separate evidence capture and a bounded authentication pulse, refusing reference succession that would substitute home for the foreign origin. Brakk and the other engineer test transport, shuttle/field extension, and independent anchoring. Their failures are measured local constraints, not a universal rescue prohibition.

**Emotional turn:** The other captain summarizes an ordinary message from his Ashley: bag scores, a misplaced Stanley, and come home when the receipt is clean. The recording itself is not played or shared. Home Noah recognizes his own life without claiming those people are his family.

**Resolution:** Separately authorized, a scoped copy of raw Ganymede evidence is transferred with credential omissions marked. The other REG preserves conflicting originals outside the smoothing process. It does not stop drift. The other Noah does not thank them. Home returns; foreign contact lasts six hours before failing.

**Aftermath:** Last confirmed alive is not currently known alive. Evidence and unsuccessful rescue attempts remain available for review. Ender asks the difficult question at dinner. Preserve the published candidate's ending unless a new revision explicitly changes it.

## JS-T02: Address Not Found

**Origin:** User-supplied slate. **Cast:** Noah, REG, Sorev, Ellie, Brakk; Ashley and Imani; a guest captain and communications officer, names open.

**Opening:** A normally resolvable address becomes incomplete during a routine station observation. REG identifies coherent transmissions at the boundary; he cannot certify an entire inaccessible branch exists in the form imagined. Avalon approaches under its own reference and establishes a local exchange with a Starfleet-derived vessel.

**Development:** The crew reports years without externally accepted communications. They have food, work, and internal records, but increasingly doubt whether anyone else can recognize their history. Their communications officer has built an elaborate acknowledgment ritual that never receives an answer. A junior crew member thinks home Avalon may be another fabricated test. Independent instruments establish the encounter, not every historical claim in their logs.

**Attempts:** Test an isolated relay, a patient challenge-response, and a temporary marker that does not borrow Jupiter's credentials. The guest crew consents to an external attestation. Ashley permits an encounter record while refusing certification of the missing historical succession. Her refusal must have that precise scope; she is not banning basic courtesy.

**Climax:** The guest captain wants a certificate reading authenticated Starfleet vessel. Noah offers a narrower truthful record: observed crew, observed ship, exchanged testimony, dates and gaps. Acceptance is their choice. The communications officer insists the narrower statement is still an answer after years of none.

**Resolution:** They retain copies on independently held media. No home reference is handed over. A simple reply from the guest ship completes an ordinary exchange before withdrawal.

**Aftermath:** The episode ends on the crew reading an acknowledgment aloud, with no promise of permanent access. Record the limits and possible relay follow-up. Distinguish it from The Unreturned: this ship has a settled community and a communications problem, not a desperate counterpart asking for our home.

## JS-T03: The Second Watch

**Origin:** User-supplied slate. **Primary setting:** Station only. **Cast:** Ashley, Imani, REG, Ellie, Tavren, Doctor, Ender; Noah receives a limited initial notification.

**Opening:** A nineteen-minute conflicted capture shows an older Ender serving aboard a foreign Avalon. It arrives while home Ender is trying to beat his recreation-deck score and talking about a cooking project.

**Development:** The capture contains an incursion response, identifiable uniforms, and a name. It does not establish a destined career or the survival of anyone after the fragment. Imani isolates the original. Ashley requests a privacy assessment and a separate review of any present operational relevance. Noah is initially told there is a family-adjacent capture under review, not supplied its contents as an automatic privilege.

**Conflict:** Ellie wants to know whether shielding people can become controlling their lives. Tavren, consulted without identifying protected details, argues that glimpses of future success can become obligations placed on children. The Doctor distinguishes local confidentiality duties from any unsupported claim that all Federation medicine has one universal alternate-outcome rule.

**Climax:** A seemingly practical recommendation could push Ender toward the job in the capture. Ashley refuses to turn unverified foreign history into a parental plan. The adults must also recognize that exaggerated silence and special treatment can disclose a secret indirectly.

**Resolution:** Preserve the capture in restricted Memory Beta, document access and reasons, and establish a review condition for disclosure. No departure. No secret operational warning is withheld from those who need it.

**Aftermath:** Ender beats his score and asks Ellie to taste something he made. The scene restores his present interests. His future remains open. The final beat should concern adults learning restraint in how they behave, not a child reduced to an ominous image.

## JS-T04: Consent of the Unmade

**Origin:** User-supplied slate. **Cast:** Noah, REG, Ellie, Brakk; Ashley, Doctor, Liora Renn; a foreign holographic person and preservation official, names open.

**Opening:** A distress pulse reaches a branch where a damaged holographic person faces involuntary integration into a collective preservation system. The official believes it is the only remaining way to save their memories.

**Development:** The person alternates between coherent refusals and episodes of confusion. REG can attest to the words spoken; he cannot infer incapacity from disagreement or certify capacity alone. The Doctor conducts a consented, bounded assessment. Ellie asks whether a preservation system that destroys individuality is preserving the thing the patient values.

**Attempts:** Seek postponement, reversible stabilization, a patient-selected representative, and independent review. Foreign law and medical claims are treated as records requiring examination, not automatic authority over Avalon. Emergency extraction is justified only by immediate danger, the person's available wishes, and explicit ship command action; it is not an unrestricted license to seize people from another jurisdiction.

**Climax:** A limited transfer can preserve the active process without merging memories. The patient agrees in a lucid interval, understanding the uncertainty. Noah authorizes protected transport; if the final prose changes consent conditions, annotate that revision.

**Resolution:** The person remains active in an isolated ship environment with medical support, communication, and access to an advocate. Ashley delays station transfer pending technical and rights review. Isolation is protective and reviewed, not indefinite punishment for an identity conflict.

**Aftermath:** The unresolved jurisdiction continues, but the person is able to make ordinary choices: lighting, a name, whether to receive visitors. Do not call them merely an unreconciled capture. Future continuation asks how refuge becomes a life.

## JS-T05: Fault in the Tether

**Origin:** User-supplied slate. **Cast:** Noah, REG, Ellie, Brakk, Sorev; Tavren aboard as an explicitly invited civilian participant; Ashley, Imani, Doctor.

**Opening:** A proposed Jovian radiation interference event disrupts authentication during a bounded exploration transit. An eleven-minute interruption is the treatment's chosen duration, not a known hardware tolerance. Establish emergency navigation behavior before the outage becomes dramatic.

**Development:** Avalon suspends further temporal actuation and uses its retained trajectory under a prebriefed safety procedure. Reauthentication succeeds. Tavren and two named-in-draft crew members give different orders for events during the interval. A disagreement in memory is established; the cause is not immediately known, nor does it prove a branch crossing.

**Attempts:** Collect accounts independently before a group debrief contaminates them. Compare instrument buffers, local clocks, and physical task outcomes. The Doctor assesses symptoms with consent. REG distinguishes sequence reconstruction from a claim of physical history replacement.

**Climax:** A tidy official timeline would help close the report but requires selecting an unsupported minute order. Tavren dislikes having his uncertainty made public; Brakk dislikes paperwork called noise when it concerns a navigation fault. Ashley requires the conflict be retained with appropriate privacy scope.

**Resolution:** Store the reliable shared events, disputed ordering, and separate testimony. Technical review finds a plausible reconstruction artifact without pretending it exhausts every uncertainty. Navigation clearance depends on measured readiness, not agreement among memories.

**Aftermath:** Tavren returns to work and catches himself checking a trivial sequence twice. A later inquiry has multiple attributed accounts. Keep the tone humane and practical; do not portray people as defective because honest recollections differ.

## JS-T06: The Other Dinner

**Origin:** User-supplied slate. **Cast:** Ender, Tavren, Ellie, Ashley, Noah, Jake; two officers with an existing diplomatic disagreement.

**Opening:** A consented foreign cultural archive contains recipes and a banquet protocol associated with a peaceful diplomatic encounter. The captured outcome is attributed to that history, not a prediction for ours.

**Development:** Ender wants to cook it. Tavren wants an achievable service plan. Ellie hopes a shared evening will help morale. Ashley permits a cultural event with source attribution, food-safety checks, and voluntary attendance. She declines an undeclared diplomatic experiment using a rigid seating chart to manipulate particular officers.

**Attempts:** Adapt ingredients, test portions, and distinguish essential cultural meanings from circumstances specific to the foreign negotiation. The guest archive's source consent must cover sharing; availability alone does not confer permission. Ender learns that translating a recipe involves asking why a step matters.

**Climax:** Two officers hear a toast that resembles their current dispute. One calls it an ambush. The other thinks refusing to learn from another culture is absurd. Ellie explains what was and was not planned, admits the ambiguity, and offers a change rather than invoking doctrine as an argument-ending phrase.

**Resolution:** The meal continues without a forced reconciliation. A voluntarily requested discussion follows under ordinary authority. If policy later changes, it must be through review, not the claim that an alternate dinner has proved the answer.

**Aftermath:** Ender saves a cooking note about what worked; Tavren adds a properly attributed dish with permission. The emotional ending is a good evening with an honest awkward moment. Neither suppress ordinary inference nor imply a recipe mechanically prevents war.

## JS-T07: Receipt for a War We Didn't Fight

**Opening:** A Star Trek Online-adjacent foreign record describes an Iconian gateway intervention and a costly diplomatic bargain. Use that branch label as source classification; do not import its entire history into protected 2397 or give characters forbidden future certainty.

## JS-T08: Jake's Headline

## JS-T10: Come Home When the Receipt Is Clean

**Origin:** User-supplied slate. **Cast:** Ashley and Noah parallel POV; Imani, REG, Brakk, Ellie; family ending.

**Opening:** Authentication develops growing timestamp disagreements during a mapping mission. The tether remains identifiable but margin narrows. Ashley requests return through the existing safe envelope while analysis proceeds. There is no detected incursion.

**Development:** A historical maintenance routine rounded a stored clock value. The treatment must distinguish the original raw measurement from a later derived display. The small discrepancy matters because subsequent calculations relied on a claim of precision the display did not retain.

**Attempts:** Inspect raw clocks, maintenance records, prior acknowledgments, and independent witnesses. Do not invent the missing exact minute if no surviving measurement establishes it. Nor should command refuse a demonstrably correct amendment merely because a log is closed.

**Climax:** Both captains sign an append-only correction to the interpretation: the earlier timestamp was rounded, the interval has a stated uncertainty, and future authentication must not treat it as exact. The old record and separate statements remain. Where the original exact value is unrecoverable, the gap stays.

**Resolution:** The ship returns after the bounded uncertainty is properly handled. The look changes the system's treatment of the record, even though it does not change a past event or supply an invented minute.

**Aftermath:** The children hear that the reference needed another look. Ashley can say clean does not mean spotless; it means we can explain what we have. This corrects the slate's false choice between erasure and leaving an avoidable technical hazard. End on relief and a practical maintenance improvement.

## JS-T18: The Quiet Fleet

**Origin:** Assistant-proposed. **Cast:** Noah, Brakk, Sorev, Ellie, REG; Ashley, Doctor, Nalin, Arlen; evacuation coordinator and civilian representatives.

**Opening:** Twelve ships with powered-down weapons organize an evacuation near a moon whose access boundary is narrowing. Their first request is simple: can Avalon carry people? Establish observed population, route, and reference constraints before promising capacity.

**Development:** Local crews already have a plan and priorities. Noah joins it rather than replacing it. Ashley prepares reception and maintains the independent home tether. Consent, destination choice, medical triage, and manifest continuity matter. No one is automatically assigned to protected 2397 because their vessel is stranded.

**Attempts:** Run an unmanned passage test, then a limited volunteer transfer. Brakk calculates the measured cost per crossing; exact capacity and deadline are developed numbers, not predetermined canon. The Doctor establishes care for actual exposures. Civilians help track families and language needs.

**Climax:** The final available window cannot carry everyone aboard Avalon. Remaining local ships can complete another route if Avalon gives them power or a navigation observation.
