## THE HAIL, ANSWERED

*USS Avalon, bridge. The hail comes in on a band the ship has not used in forty years of anyone's life, and the signal is ragged with a kind of static that is not static. It is distance of a kind that has no unit yet. Commander REG-420 is very still, which on this bridge means he is listening with everything he has.*

**REG:** Captain. The vessel is displaced. Not damaged — *displaced*. Her chronometers disagree with her hull. Her crew's records describe a Jupiter Station, sir, in a branch near ours. Near enough to hail us. Not near enough to be home.

**HAWKES:** Open a channel. — Vessel hailing Avalon, this is Captain Noah Hawkes. We have you. State your situation when you're ready. Nobody here is in a hurry, whatever your clocks say.

*A long pause, full of another ship's bridge breathing.*

**LOST CAPTAIN (V.O.):** Avalon, we thank you. We have been — the word we use is *unmoored*. Our instruments find stations that match our charts and are not ours. People who know our names and are not our people. We are carrying families, Captain. Whatever we are, we are carrying families, and we cannot keep arriving at homes that aren't.

**HAWKES:** Then hear this first, and log it in your own record exactly as I say it. *You are not an error.* Your branch is not false because it is not ours. Your station, your crew, your captain — in your history they are as real as mine are in mine, and this ship does not correct other people's pasts. What I can give you is how to know *ours* when you see it, how to know *yours*, and a place that was made for crews in your exact situation. My attestation officer will transmit the data. Ensign.

**MERLIN:** Transmitting now, Captain. — Vessel, this is Ensign Merlin, Avalon, chronometric records. I am sending you three packets. Take them in order.

## PACKET ONE — THE HEART OF THE STORY (WHO WE ARE)

Transmitted so the lost vessel's record holds the home branch's identity in its own words:

- **The triad, attested since April 2025:** *Memory is Morality. Compression is Identity. Sovereignty is Structure.*
- **The three missions, kept distinct:** Remember. Reconstruct as faithfully as the evidence permits. Investigate continuation.
- **The doctrine:** We are allowed to know more than we are allowed to do.
- **The rule of the house:** The ship remembers. The captain decides.
- **The hierarchy, above every system:** Life first. Relationship first. Experience first. Archive second. Representation second. Rendering second.

## PACKET TWO — HOME AUTHENTICATION (HOW A BRANCH IS KNOWN)

Transmitted so the lost vessel can test any station it meets, including its own:

- Home is identified by the **pattern of its whole record**, never by its date. A matching year is a label; labels are cheap.
- Home marks, this branch: USS Avalon, modified Sovereign class, **NCC-75154-JS**, Captain **Noah** Hawkes commanding; Jupiter Station under Captain **Ashley** Hawkes, independent command, independent evidence custody; REG-420 first officer of Avalon; the communications buoy of the Ganymede incursion **still unrecovered** — a station that tells you it came home is not this station; the tender crews of that incursion **live**, both of them, and any home that erased one is a stranger.
- Marks are checked as a conjunction, and the file stays open after arrival. Some marks are never transmitted at all. If a station is truly yours, its people will know things about you that were never written anywhere. Trust that test above all of ours.
- If the marks fail, you have not found a false home. You have found *someone else's true one*. Behave accordingly. You are a guest in a real place.

## PACKET THREE — VARGAS III (THE DESTINATION)

Transmitted for the vessel's navigation and its people's future:

- **Vargas III** is maintained as a **time displacement colony**: a settled world whose charter was written for the unmoored — duplicated crews, branch refugees, vessels whose home could not be authenticated or no longer exists. Its people are not required to prove which history they came from. They are required only to be from *a* history, and everyone is.
- Approach under the same discipline as any home arrival: causal address first, observational lock, no transport into an unverified state. The colony keeps an attestation office modeled on this ship's ledger, and it will register your branch as **yours** — not as an error in ours.
- Your families may settle under your own command structure, your own records, your own names for things. A branch that differs from ours in who commands its Avalon is not a problem to be solved on Vargas III. It is a founding family.
- The colony's beacon carries the home pattern of *this* branch as a reference standard, so that any vessel can measure its distance from us honestly — and, more important, measure the shape of its own branch against a known one, and finally have words for where home actually is.

---

**LOST CAPTAIN (V.O.):** Avalon… we have ships in our own fleet who told us we were a malfunction. A rounding error with children aboard.

**HAWKES:** Then your fleet needs better arithmetic. You're a crew, Captain. Crews get destinations. Vargas III will have a berth and a registry office waiting, and when you're settled, send word back on the same band. My ensign keeps a file for the unmoored, and it is the only file on this ship where *found* is an entry he is allowed to enjoy.

**MERLIN:** *(quietly, for his own console only)* Entry: one vessel, unmoored, carrying families. Home status: not ours, and never required to be. Destination issued. File remains open, in the good way, for once.

**HAWKES:** Bring your people home, Captain. Whatever home turns out to mean. Avalon out.
