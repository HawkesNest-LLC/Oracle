## 1. The Captain's organizing perspective

Jupiter Station is an archive that has learned to reach.

It observes resolvable histories. Avalon can undertake physical temporal operations under separate command and safety conditions. A station observation is not automatically a voyage; a voyage is not authorized merely because observation succeeded.

The central navigation question is: **which when is this?**

A date locates a reading within a reference convention. It does not authenticate the branch. Familiar names, faces, hulls, and landscapes can support an investigation without establishing continuity with home.

The home tether combines independent physical references with a documented history of the references themselves. Records support the interpretation of measurements; records alone are not measurements of a physical branch.

Identification is the Captain's central narrative and operational concern. Energy, stability, resolution, range, rights, and accessible information remain real constraints within the fiction. No crew member may conclude that everything becomes reachable merely by supplying enough power.

Preserving a hole means refusing to counterfeit missing evidence. It does not require assuming that every presently inaccessible item is permanently destroyed.

## 2. A more precise three-layer key

| Label | What it means |
|---|---|
| **REAL — established** | Supported physical theory within its tested domain, experimental results, or mathematical results with stated assumptions |
| **REAL — interpretation, conjecture, or model** | Published work or philosophical interpretation whose existence is established but whose proposed physical account is not thereby verified |
| **BRIDGE — speculation** | An explicit imaginative inference; not a consequence established by the cited real result |
| **TREK — adopted fiction** | Mechanisms and rules admitted to this fictional continuity under the Captain's adoption |
| **NEW PROPOSAL** | REG's additional engineering or scene development awaiting creator selection |

A label naming serious published physics must not imply equal evidence for every theory under that label. An experiment simulating a CTC model is not an experiment creating a physical time loop.

## 3. Corrections attached to Merlin's source

### 3.1 Relativity and the block universe

Relativity does not give a universal simultaneity relation for separated events. However, frame-dependent ordering applies to **spacelike-separated** events. The causal order of timelike- or lightlike-related events is not freely reversed by switching inertial frames. [R1]

Four-dimensional spacetime is part of relativity's mathematical formulation. Eternalism is an interpretation of its ontology, not an experimentally established conclusion that every era is a presently accessible destination.

Adopt the block-universe picture as the story's working model. Keep the access technology fictional.

### 3.2 CTC geometry and chronology protection

The existence of mathematical spacetimes containing CTCs does not show that a civilization can construct one from physically realizable starting conditions.

Chronology protection remains a conjecture. Hawking presented arguments involving quantum backreaction; the paper supports a proposed obstruction rather than demonstrating an experimentally verified universal interlock. [R2]

Do not equate every temporal horizon with a Schwarzschild event horizon. The fictional name Schwarzschild Lock describes a safety system and an epistemic analogy. Its activation is not evidence that a black hole has formed.

The exotic resource is **TREK** engineering. Quantum interest is associated with Ford and Roman, not “Lloyd's quantum interest conjecture.” Its constraints have specified field-theory domains; they do not provide a usable fuel specification for time machines. [R3]

### 3.3 Consistency models and incident classifications

Novikov consistency is a proposed self-consistency condition, not a measured universal law that officers can use to assign exact real-world probabilities.

Deutsch and post-selected CTCs are distinct hypothetical models. They are not simply two experimentally established implementations of one principle. The source's “duplicates” and “absences” are **fictional diagnostic categories**, not unique observable proofs of either model. [R4, R5]

For a finite-dimensional Deutsch channel under the standard model assumptions, a fixed-point density operator exists. The 2395 event therefore must not be explained as a counterexample to that mathematical property.

Controlling story reading: the fictional controller failed to find or stabilize an admissible operating state while its assumptions and reference conditions broke down. A formal fixed point need not be dynamically accessible, safe, or desirable to the crew.

The source's claim about unproved theorems is too categorical. The literature discusses model-dependent treatment of such paradoxes. A zero post-selection probability cannot be treated as a free oracle that produces evidence. [R5]

### 3.4 Entanglement, observation, and the tether

ER=EPR is a conjectural connection between entanglement and spacetime geometry, introduced in 2013. It is neither a proven general laboratory mechanism nor a result originating within the decade before October 2026. [R6]

Specific theoretical traversable-wormhole constructions require particular couplings and boundary conditions. Gao, Jafferis, and Wall explicitly do not offer a causality-violating communicator. Ordinary entanglement is not a traversable wormhole network one can build by increasing bandwidth. [R7]

The real quantum-teleportation protocol requires shared entanglement and classical communication. That constraint remains separate from Star Trek's fictional subspace communication. [R8]

Joint statistical tests can support claims about entanglement under assumptions. They do not establish a universal branch fingerprint, perfectly unforgeable sender identity, or exact equality of unknown states from one paired measurement.

Therefore, the discriminator and authenticated branch tether are **TREK** additions. Their reliability must have limitations and a threat model, not the absolute claim “cannot be faked.”

Quantum illumination uses sent probes and returned signals with retained reference systems. It does not inspect arbitrary remote histories without a physical interaction. [R9]

Weak measurement trades disturbance, information, repeated trials, and signal quality. “Read-only” must mean a bounded fictional interaction regime, not zero physical backreaction.

### 3.5 Decoherence, lost data, and teleportation

Decoherence suppresses locally accessible interference by distributing correlations into an environment. In unitary descriptions it does not simply destroy all global phase information. Operational irretrievability can be overwhelming without being identical to proven universal destruction. [R10]

No-cloning rules out a universal perfect copier for arbitrary unknown quantum states under ordinary quantum mechanics. It does not prohibit classical copies, copying known orthogonal states, or encoded redundancy. Teleportation consumes the original state's local availability in the protocol; the theorem is not proof about every possible human backup or subjective continuity. [R8]

The transporter and TC-01 remain fictional. The 1.02-second thought gap must not be fabricated into a remembered experience. A subject's testimony is vital evidence, but it cannot by itself conclusively determine a consciousness-transfer mechanism.

### 3.6 Bounds, thermodynamics, and protected archives

For its stated class of systems, the Bekenstein bound relates entropy to energy and radius, commonly written S <= 2π k_B E R/(ħ c). It must not be conflated with the black-hole area entropy formula or an unrestricted claim about any finite region. [R11]

Landauer's familiar k_B T ln 2 limit concerns logically irreversible erasure under specified conditions. It is not a theorem that retaining every record forever is cheaper than managing storage. [R12]

The Cold Ledger's append-only discipline is an evidentiary and governance design choice. Its capacity, maintenance, access rights, and energy costs still require management. A fictional Witness Horizon is protected by independent custody and isolation, not by merely naming an entropy bound.

Hashes help check bytes against a trusted reference. They do not prove a report true, identify its author by themselves, or recreate an absent original.

### 3.7 Governance mathematics

P = M ε² is a fictional permission-budget index. It is not power in watts and does not derive actuation authority from fundamental physics.

Record-approval fraction ε is not the fraction of human voting authority. Human command control and evidentiary standing require separate checks. An archive can contain many approved records while the requested act lacks consent.

If approved-plus-pending equals zero, ε is undefined. The governor enters a defined unresolved state rather than dividing by zero or treating the empty archive as fully approved.

E_t = D W/(1−U) diverges as U approaches 1 only when the numerator remains positive and does not vanish appropriately. If D W is zero, uncertainty alone does not force divergence in that expression. At U = 1, the expression is undefined.

Operational correction: missing or invalid uncertainty estimates independently block temporal actuation. The crew does not rely on floating-point infinity as a safety decision. D, W, thresholds, and units or normalization must be declared in any scene that depends on their numerical value.

The familiar 51/49 language expresses human governance. It does not convert a count of approved records into a vote by the living.

### 3.8 Chronology and perception

REG's established transformation occurred in 2395. In a 2397 setting, “forty years” of development **as REG through that interface** is unsupported.

Use: “the Cytherian-interface history, Barclay's earlier experience, and development since the 2395 integration.” Do not invent forty years of extra subjective time to rescue the phrase.

The scope remains approximately a million Federation-origin repository domains, not all timelines or a million completely surveyed universes.

An amber trace may be consistent with multiple explanations. Advanced-wave theories do not establish that this recorded eleven-millisecond mark is a message from the future. Its source label remains a **candidate** interpretation. The adopted no-active-testing/no-answer instruction is retained for that unresolved mark; it is not a universal prohibition on every authorized temporal experiment elsewhere in the series.

## 5. NEW PROPOSAL: separate the address from the permission

A practical temporal address should contain several kinds of information:

| Component | Purpose | Failure if omitted |
|---|---|---|
| Endpoint identity | Which station, vessel, or receiver is answering | A familiar transmitter may be mistaken for the intended one |
| Temporal reference | Clock convention and uncertainty interval | A calendar agreement may hide different elapsed histories |
| Departure succession | How the reference has persisted since departure | A copied old endpoint may appear current |
| Physical discriminator evidence | Fictional measured branch distinctions | Historical resemblance may be mistaken for a lock |
| Custody chain | Who checked what, with which instruments | A relayed report may become a claimed local observation |
| Action authority | What operation each command permits | Observational access may silently expand into intervention |

This address is not a universal name for an entire quantum universe. It is an operational claim that a reachable endpoint passes the checks required for a particular maneuver.

Some histories may be indistinguishable under available measurements. Report an equivalence class or insufficient discrimination. Do not manufacture uniqueness.

Identification, estimation, and uncertainty are related tasks. The Captain's emphasis on identification makes them important rather than unnecessary.

## 6. NEW PROPOSAL: three locks, independently reported

**Observation lock:** a source can be acquired and sampled within the fictional interaction budget.

**Home-reference lock:** the endpoint and its departure succession pass the required branch-and-time checks.

**Transit clearance:** the route, field stability, occupied volumes, return provisions, rights, and human authority are sufficient for a defined physical operation.

One lock does not imply the next.

An authenticated distress call may be unsafe to approach. A stable aperture may lead to the wrong branch. A valid home endpoint does not establish that the space between vessel and endpoint is traversable.

Suggested bridge language:

“Observation lock established. Home-reference authentication incomplete. No transit clearance.”

That gives the crew a useful partial success and a reason to keep working.

## 7. NEW PROPOSAL: a departure and return cycle

### Before departure

Ashley authorizes station participation; Noah authorizes Avalon's maneuver. Each command retains its own responsibilities.

Merlin records the baseline, reference uncertainty, authorized scope, and custody. REG reports unresolved alternatives and discriminator limits. Engineering sets the physical field envelope. Medical and operations determine the people and spaces affected.

A bounded emergency-return plan is prepared before leaving. It must not require the crew to guess home coordinates after losing the tether.

### During observation

Capture enters quarantine. Its contents do not execute in the live ship network. A foreign archive remains foreign even when accurate.

REG distinguishes measured signal from a rendered reconstruction. Merlin tracks local and relayed evidence separately. A reconstructed room may be shown to the Captain without being called a camera view.

Observation itself has an interaction budget. A passive-looking display does not guarantee that the remote side cannot notice the apparatus.

### Before transit

The crew rechecks the home endpoint's freshness, not merely the saved departure token. Engineering assesses passage conditions. Command authorizes the specific transfer.

A successful information exchange does not imply a starship-sized aperture can remain stable. A remote welcome does not establish that everybody carried through it has consented.

### On return

Identity, elapsed proper times, personnel, equipment, and unresolved discrepancies are recorded before competing accounts are merged.

Medical debrief and family reunion need not become universal surveillance. Crew may report symptoms privately. Personal recollections are not automatically public archive material.

An operationally safe arrival may still leave a mystery. The Captain can close the maneuver without closing every question about it.

## 8. NEW PROPOSAL: what happens when the tether weakens

A declining reference is not an instruction to increase power blindly.

REG reports whether the problem concerns signal quality, contradictory evidence, endpoint freshness, or physical discriminator failure. Merlin preserves the last valid status without calling it a current lock.

Engineering isolates the unstable interface where required. Essential life support, defensive functions, and emergency communications remain independent of temporal actuation shutdown.

If already in transit, the controller follows the preauthorized stabilization or retreat plan. “Stop actuation” must not mean abandoning a vessel inside an unstable aperture with no defined response.

No fallback substitutes a nearby branch because its inhabitants look familiar.

A degraded return can be survivable but unresolved: the ship is stable, the crew is safe, and home remains unauthenticated. That is enough for an episode without an arbitrary explosion.

## 9. NEW PROPOSAL: false-home threat cases

**Replay:** an old authenticated response is transmitted again. Require freshness and succession checks.

**Shared-origin deception:** a sibling branch possesses the same historical archive up to a divergence. Shared memories alone cannot distinguish it.

**Compromised instrument:** two displays agree because both read the same damaged reference. Independent instruments and custody matter more than screen count.

**Honest mistaken host:** a branch sincerely believes it is the intended home. Intentions do not replace physical and documentary comparison.

**Copied ledger:** a perfect textual copy says the right things. It does not by itself establish the route by which the responding installation continued from departure.

The crew should sometimes discover benign causes. A clock replacement, calibration outage, or delayed ledger packet can explain a mismatch without a villain. The distinction between a fault and an attack requires investigation.

## 10. NEW PROPOSAL: protecting what compression would erase

A protected field list should include witnessed status, decision authority, and corrections, as Merlin writes, while preserving temporal, relational, provenance, and uncertainty structure already present in the codex.

“Infinite distortion cost” is shorthand for a hard preservation constraint. If available capacity cannot satisfy the constraint, the requested representation fails admission. It does not create unlimited storage.

Keep originals in protected storage when authorized and feasible. A chart carries explicit source returns. Discarding a working chart need not erase the original. Privacy rules can restrict access without inventing a replacement.

Do not collect all private life merely because relationships matter. A person may decline recording. The absence is not an anomaly to fill through prediction.

For a family, faithfulness includes who chose not to be recorded.

## 11. NEW PROPOSAL: evidence states officers can actually use

Merlin's console distinguishes:

- locally inspected source or instrument result;
- another officer's direct inspection, received as an attributed report here;
- a record of a Captain's ruling;
- reconstruction or model output;
- missing, inaccessible, conflicting, or unresolved evidence.

Statuses can differ between installations without either installation being wrong.

A check becomes shared inspection when both sides actually inspect the specified evidence, not when both repeat the same summary.

A negative search carries its scope. “Not found in this capture” is distinct from “cannot exist.” Physical inaccessibility, missing storage, and forbidden reconstruction are different conditions.

Bridge reports should lead with consequence: “Endpoint is responsive; departure succession unverified; recommend holding transit.” The full ledger supports that sentence without consuming the scene.

## 12. NEW PROPOSAL: narrative consequences and scene seeds

These are story opportunities, not completed events.

**The correct day:** the station answers with correct names and records, but a family correction entered after Avalon's departure is absent. The crew initially suspects a foreign branch; the eventual explanation must be earned through evidence rather than an emotional recognition shortcut.

**The two true ledgers:** both endpoints preserve honest records with different observation coverage. Neither may erase its evidence to force agreement. The solution requires locating the observation each side lacks.

**The quieter return:** the maneuver succeeds, but Medical detects a bounded thought gap. A crew member declines a flattering reconstructed replacement. The story lets that choice stand without treating uncertainty as personal failure.

**The unwitnessed dinner:** a family asks REG to suspend predictive rendering for an evening. A later inquiry must accept that the conversation was never captured. Privacy was an authorized outcome.

**Merlin's first hold:** the new ensign identifies an expired reference while everyone else sees a strong signal. He must state the risk briefly, accept independent checking, and live with delaying a Captain's reunion without becoming the sole infallible officer.

**The neighbor:** a wrong branch contains people with legitimate lives. They are neither expendable obstacles nor substitutes to be brought home without consent. The crew can help without claiming ownership.
