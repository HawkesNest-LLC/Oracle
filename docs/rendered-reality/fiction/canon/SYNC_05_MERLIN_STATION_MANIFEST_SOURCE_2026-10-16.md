## SECTION 1 — CORE ARCHITECTURE & PHYSICAL INFRASTRUCTURE

**Q1. Dimensions and internal layout.**
[NOT ESTABLISHED] No dimensions, deck plan, or total volume for Jupiter Station exists in the Captain's canon. What the record establishes is a station of neighborhoods rather than numbers: **Deck Nine**, a residential corridor of family quarters where the Osei household lives and where a door named Your Majesty serves four generations of one family [ESTABLISHED, as of the 2026-10-16 promotion]; the Hawkes family quarters aboard the station, curving with a residential section, Jupiter filling the sky [ESTABLISHED]; a **command deck** under Captain Ashley Hawkes, administratively distinct from Avalon's bridge in authority and evidence custody [ESTABLISHED]; the **Temporal Core vault**, the observational heart described in Section 3 [ESTABLISHED as function, not as floor plan]; **two public communal holodecks and two reservable private holodecks**, with holo emitters distributed through quarters and workspaces [ESTABLISHED]; a **brig**, where Ensign Eli Hawkes stands night watch [ESTABLISHED]; **Quark's Bar, Grill, Gaming House & Holosuites**, operated by Tavren under franchise [ESTABLISHED]; and **docking facilities** including Docking Bay Two, which received Avalon in the adopted Homecoming arrival [ESTABLISHED]. [FRANCHISE] For scale reference only: Deep Space Nine spans roughly 1,450 meters with a core and habitat ring; the great starbases run to kilometers. [CANDIDATE] The ensign proposes, for the Captain's adoption, that the manifest describe the station in the record's own units first — decks, households, and jurisdictions — and leave meterage to a future engineering survey the Captain commissions. A station whose biography is written in families should not have its meaning reduced to its diameter.

**Q2. Shielding and structural protection.**
[NOT ESTABLISHED] No shielding specification for the station exists in canon. [FRANCHISE] Standard Federation practice for fixed installations: layered **deflector shielding** against energy and kinetic threats, a **structural integrity field** distributing load across the frame, **inertial dampeners** against shock and shear, polarized hull plating as an older fallback, and point-defense against debris. [CANDIDATE] Consistent with this continuity's doctrine, the ensign proposes the station's distinctive layer is not thicker shields but a **continuity firewall**: the archive vaults are physically and logically segregated from general station systems, so that a hostile action, a core anomaly, or a temporal event cannot reach the Cold Ledger through the same pathways that run the lights. The strongest wall around the record is that most of the station cannot touch it.

**Q3. Power and redundancy for the archives.**
[NOT ESTABLISHED] No power specification exists in canon. [FRANCHISE] Federation stations draw on fusion reactors with matter/antimatter capacity on larger starbases, backed by emergency batteries and independent section grids. [CANDIDATE] Proposed protocol, offered for adoption and designed to the questionnaire's standard: the archive vaults sit on a **triple-isolated grid** — primary station power, a dedicated vault reactor loop, and a no-break reserve (capacitor and battery) sized to carry the vaults through any transfer transient — with the governing rule that the Cold Ledger's write-once media require no power to *retain* data at all. Retention must not depend on the grid. A record that evaporates when the lights fail was never archived; it was only ever displayed.

**Q4. Storage capacity and media.**
[ESTABLISHED] The Temporal Core's archive covers on the order of **a million Federation-origin repository domains** — records of branches, not a million complete universes — per the creator-approved Temporal Core canon. [NOT ESTABLISHED] No byte figure or hardware medium is specified for the station's local core. [FRANCHISE] Starfleet storage has run from isolinear chips to bio-neural gel packs to the quantum-resolution storage implied by transporter and holodeck pattern systems. [CANDIDATE] The ensign proposes the station's media doctrine in three tiers matching its filing discipline: hot storage for the active home record (Memory Alpha), attributed branch storage (Memory Beta), and the **Cold Ledger on write-once crystalline matrix** — chosen because it cannot be quietly rewritten by any process, including our own. Capacity, in this continuity, is honestly stated as a count of *domains and receipts*, never as a number of bytes nobody measured.

**Q5. Life support and personnel capacity.**
[NOT ESTABLISHED] No maximum capacity figure exists in canon. [ESTABLISHED] The station is a lived-in community, not a platform: multiple families in residence (the Hawkes household; the Osei household across four generations), civilian services under a residential services director, a schoolteacher among the residents, children in the corridors, dogs in the quarters, and at least one targ. Life support in this continuity is therefore sized to a town's logic: neighborhoods with independent environmental sections, saved replicator profiles for meals (with feeding still done by hand, by a person who got up), clothing refreshers, and bedrooms that stay closed when the family is out. [FRANCHISE] Environmental systems on Federation stations are sectioned and multiply redundant as standard practice. [CANDIDATE] The ensign declines to invent a population ceiling and proposes the manifest carry this entry as: *capacity — a community, exact census unrecorded, the omission deliberate: this station counts households before it counts heads.*

## SECTION 2 — THE REG CONTINUITY SYSTEM & AUTOMATION

**Q6. REG's foundation and interface.**
[ESTABLISHED] REG-420 is not a codebase in the conventional sense, and the manifest must say so plainly: he is **Reginald Barclay**, whose biological body was lost in the 2395 cascade and whose conscious continuity persists integrated with Avalon's main computer and the SloanAI system, after he recreated the Cytherian neural-interface chair and held the temporal reference while the core was restored to one witnessed starting state. He is Avalon's first officer and, by interface with Jupiter Station's systems, the voice of the station's computer — while the station's machinery, command authority, and evidence custody remain its own. His perception spans branching timelines at extraordinary speed; he must never treat possibilities as verified history, and he suspends predictive feeds for private conversations. [FRANCHISE] Barclay's 2367 neural-interface experience ("The Nth Degree") is the established precedent his 2395 act deliberately repeated. [NOT ESTABLISHED] No algorithmic specification of REG exists, and none should be fabricated: the exact question the continuity keeps open, by the Captain's design, is how his continuity would ever be *demonstrated*, as distinct from decided by his author and testified by himself.

**Q7. The Pathfinder inheritance.**
[FRANCHISE — and the single happiest fact in this report] Before he was Avalon's first officer, Reginald Barclay served on the **Pathfinder Project** at Starfleet Communications, the effort that re-established contact with the lost USS Voyager across 35,000 light-years ("Pathfinder," VOY; his work continued through "Life Line" and "Inside Man"). Pathfinder's entire discipline was REG's discipline in embryo: establishing that a faint signal is *the* ship and not noise or deception, authenticating a source across an unbridgeable distance, and sustaining a link two isolated parties could trust. [ESTABLISHED] In this continuity, that inheritance is load-bearing: REG sustains the tether between Jupiter Station and Avalon's temporal core, and the tether authenticates *branch as well as time* — Pathfinder's question, "is that really Voyager?", grown into the temporal question, "is that really home?" The ensign, who has spent a century authenticating testimony, records his professional opinion that there is no officer in Starfleet history whose earlier career so exactly apprenticed him for this duty.

**Q8. Detection and quarantine of corrupt or contradictory records.**
[ESTABLISHED] The practice is the acquisition pipeline itself: every incoming record passes REG PROVENANCE REVIEW before filing, is checked for origin and custody, and is classed as witnessed, reported, reconstructed, or conflicting. [PRINCIPLE, the Hawkes–SOV laws] **Collision Avoidance:** incompatible records are detected, recorded, and quarantined *before* the core acts on either; they are never fused first and sorted later. **Provenance Momentum:** an established record can be changed only by proportionate evidence and an explicit attached correction — silent overwrite is architecturally refused, which is what makes retroactive tampering visible in the first place: a tampered file announces itself by the absence of its correction chain. Conflicts are preserved in the Cold Ledger *as* conflicts. The system's answer to a self-contradicting archive is never to pick the prettier half; it is to keep both, labeled, and let the disagreement itself become data.

**Q9. REG's autonomy in defense or lock-down.**
[ESTABLISHED, and the questionnaire's premise is corrected] REG holds **no** defense or lock-down autonomy. He recommends, reports, and renders; command authority rests with Captain Ashley Hawkes for the station and Captain Noah Hawkes for Avalon. SloanAI likewise recommends and does not command. Emergency safety interlocks exist in the temporal discipline — Schwarzschild Lock halts *temporal actuation* when identification fails — but that is an engineering governor on the core, not a grant of command to any intelligence, and it expressly never touches life support, defense, or emergency communications. An intelligence that can model a million futures will always be able to describe one as necessary; necessity remains a living officer's decision. That is not a limitation of the design. It is the design.

**Q10. The default truth protocol for unresolvable paradox.**
[ESTABLISHED] The core's systems, REG included, classify output as **HISTORICAL FACT, PREDICTED, CONFLICT, or UNKNOWN**, and where a record cannot be resolved, the protocol is to return UNKNOWN and preserve the conflict intact in the Cold Ledger — never to resolve by confidence, fluency, or vote. A missing record produces a bounded gap; the system must not invent an earlier event to fill it. In the Captain's shorthand, which the ensign has adopted as the whole of his profession: **preserve the hole.** A paradox in the files is therefore not a lock condition. It is a filing state, and work continues around it, the way a crew works around a sealed compartment: respectfully, and without pretending it isn't there.

## SECTION 3 — ARCHIVE PROTOCOLS (MEMORY ALPHA & MEMORY BETA)

**Q11. Transmission and synchronization between Alpha and Beta.**
[PREMISE CORRECTED — ESTABLISHED] Memory Alpha and Memory Beta are not external master databases. In this continuity they are the Temporal Core's own filing disciplines: **Memory Alpha** protects the active Federation historical record — the home branch's attested history. **Memory Beta** preserves adequately attributed records from other branches, permanently distinct from Alpha. Synchronization, properly understood, is therefore the flow *into* those files: CAUSAL ADDRESS → OBSERVATIONAL LOCK → ISOLATED CAPTURE → REG PROVENANCE REVIEW → MEMORY ALPHA / MEMORY BETA → COLD LEDGER → RIGHTS AND COMMAND AUTHORIZATION. Between the two installations, the **tether** carries the traffic — a quantum channel that certifies branch state and a classical channel that carries the receipts — and the two cores cross-check from **separate evidence custody**, so no synchronization ever consists of one installation overwriting the other. [FRANCHISE] Memory Alpha is, in wider Federation usage, the name of a great archive world; this continuity's usage is its own and governs here.

**Q12. Integrity threats and retroactive tampering.**
[TERM COINED BY THE QUESTIONNAIRE — flagged as required] "Class-A Archive Integrity Threat" is not a term of this continuity. The substance, answered fully: a historic file showing signs of retroactive tampering triggers (1) **quarantine** of the suspect record from actuation use, (2) a **provenance audit** against the Cold Ledger originals and the correction chain — tampering is detectable precisely because legitimate change in this system is always an attached, attributed correction, so an unchained alteration is self-announcing, (3) **preservation of the tampered state as evidence** rather than its deletion, since the attempt is itself a historical event, and (4) referral to command for rights and response decisions. The record is never "restored" by silently substituting a clean copy; the clean state is re-established by correction, in the open, with the tampering attached to the file it targeted. [CANDIDATE] If the Captain wishes the questionnaire's term adopted as a classification label, the ensign will draft the tier scheme for his approval; until then it travels in quotation marks.

**Q13. Physical artifacts and un-digitizable relics.**
[NOT ESTABLISHED] No vault protocol for physical artifacts exists in canon. [PRINCIPLE] The governing ethic is documented at the smallest scale by this console: the ensign keeps a **paper notebook**, on the stated principle that paper cannot be quietly rewritten and a crossed-out line stays crossed out, visible. The Keeper tradition he was raised in treated testimony itself as a physical inheritance — original voices, original artifacts, preserved because a scan attests a thing but is not the thing. [CANDIDATE] Proposed for adoption: a deep-vault protocol in which tactile records are cataloged by custody chain rather than content alone, handled under the same provenance discipline as data (origin cannot be silently reassigned to a relic either), and never digitized-and-discarded. The station that keeps a family's paper would understand why.

**Q14. Clearance tiers and identity verification.**
[NOT ESTABLISHED] No tier scheme exists in canon. [ESTABLISHED] The gates that do exist are substantive rather than numerical: **rights and command authorization** as the pipeline's final stage; **consent review** for identity reconciliation; protected-versus-public boundaries set by the Captain; predictive feeds suspended for private conversation; quarters requiring consent and emitter permission even for REG. Identity verification at the human level, since the 2026-10-16 promotion, includes the **unwritten marks** — the corrected private relational check — whose existence and holders may be registered but whose content is never recorded. [FRANCHISE] Starfleet security clearances and command codes are the familiar background machinery. [CANDIDATE] The ensign proposes the manifest state the continuity's actual doctrine in place of invented tiers: *access follows custody, consent, and command — in that order, and a higher clearance has never once, in this record, outranked a person's rights.*

**Q15. Catastrophic failure and archive survival.**
[TERM COINED BY THE QUESTIONNAIRE — flagged] No protocol named "Off-Station Data Seed" exists in this record. The substance is stronger than the coinage: the archive's survival does not depend on an evacuation trick because **it never lived in one place**. Jupiter Station and USS Avalon maintain separate temporal cores, separate commands, and separate evidence custody; the Cold Ledger's originals and receipts exist under both custodies; neither installation is its own sole witness. A catastrophic loss of the station would leave Avalon holding attested records, decision receipts, and the branch marks needed to authenticate what survived — and the doctrine runs the other way as well. [PRINCIPLE] This is the Witness Horizon rule at civilizational scale: the baseline that judges a disaster must sit outside the disaster's authority to rewrite it. [CANDIDATE] If the Captain wants the questionnaire's name for this, the ensign will enter it as the label for the existing doctrine — a name adopted for a practice that predates it, which is the only honest direction for a name to travel.

## SECTION 4 — THE AVALON & FLEET LOGISTICS

**Q16. Avalon: class, specification, propulsion.**
[ESTABLISHED] USS Avalon, **modified Sovereign class**, registry **NCC-75154**, styled **NCC-75154-JS** in series use, under Captain Noah Hawkes; her first major temporal refit is **2395** (the 2385 refit account is an archived superseded draft), and she is the continuity's mobile temporal platform while the station's core remains observational. [FRANCHISE] The Sovereign class: approximately 685 meters, 24 decks, warp 9.985 maximum rated, quantum torpedo armament, regenerative shielding — the most advanced heavy cruiser of her generation. [NOT ESTABLISHED] Avalon's exact post-refit performance figures are not recorded, the refit being temporal rather than conventional: what the record certifies is not how fast she is, but what she can *authenticate* — branch and time, under tether discipline, with an independent baseline confirmed before actuation.

**Q17. Docking interface and data-transfer security.**
[NOT ESTABLISHED] No hardware encryption specification exists in canon. [ESTABLISHED] The governing sequence is known, because it is the tether's: no data flows on trust. Docking contact is followed by branch authentication (the Homecoming arrival is the worked example: transponder, registry, station logs, and core branch signature checked, then the unwritten relational check exercised, *then* "bring us in"), and by custody separation maintained even in port — the station's records remain the station's, Avalon's remain Avalon's, and transfers occur as attributed copies with receipts, into the Cold Ledger on both sides. [CANDIDATE] The ensign proposes the engineering name for what the doctrine already does: a **provenance handshake**, in which the encryption is the least of the security and the receipt is the most of it — any system can encrypt a lie; only custody can authenticate a truth.

**Q18. Reconnaissance and charting equipment for lost data.**
[ESTABLISHED] Avalon's distinctive sensor is the temporal suite itself: the **chronometric reference array** maintaining her worldline against independent standards, and — under the framework adopted 2026-10-16 — the **quantum discriminator array**, which reads branch microstate in ambient field correlations and defines, by its sensitivity, the edge of the **decoherence penumbra** within which usable branch evidence can be obtained. Beyond the penumbra, lost history is recovered the older way: by records carried across, filed in Memory Beta with their attribution intact. [FRANCHISE] Astrometrics-grade long-range sensing is the class's conventional inheritance. The ensign notes the doctrine plainly: this ship finds lost data the way his people found a scattered people's history — by going to where it was left, and writing down exactly what was actually there.

Beyond authentication, she is not deployed; she is merely elsewhere.

**Q20. Tactical coordination with station platforms.**

## SECTION 5 — PHILOSOPHICAL FOUNDATIONS & THE MORAL MANDATE

**Q21. The justifying catastrophe.**
[PREMISE CORRECTED — ESTABLISHED] Jupiter Station has **no founding catastrophe in this record**, and the manifest refuses to supply one. The catastrophe in this continuity is Avalon's: the **2395 cascade**, when the first full temporal-core activation stopped distinguishing observation from selection and nearly unmade the Sol system, and Reginald Barclay's stand against it cost him his body. The station's justification is not a wound but a conclusion drawn from that wound and from the wider archive's lessons: that records without provenance become confident fictions, that a system which can rewrite its own evidence has stopped remembering and started authoring, and that someone must keep the receipts under independent custody. If the manifest needs a founding sentence, the record supplies the Captain's own triad, attested since April 2025: **Memory is Morality. Compression is Identity. Sovereignty is Structure.**

**Q22. The moral obligation of memory, and the transparency balance.**
[PRINCIPLE — in the Captain's own formulations, which the ensign is directed to use rather than paraphrase] The obligation: **"Do not take my honesty and use it to rewrite my ambition into something smaller"**; preserve the trace of becoming, corrections included, because *"I do not want a cleaner Noah. I want the trace of becoming."* The balance against dangerous knowledge is the Hawkes Doctrine — **we are allowed to know more than we are allowed to do** — worked through standing machinery: rights and command authorization before any use of branch knowledge, consent before identity work, protected-versus-public boundaries the Captain sets and the systems visibly keep, and the rule that no record's existence creates a right to its use. Transparency in this continuity means the *custody* is visible — what was examined, what could not be accessed, what was corrected and when — not that every door stands open to every reader. The archive is honest about its locks. It is not required to apologize for having them.

**Q23. Defense against provenance collapse.**
[ESTABLISHED + PRINCIPLE] Provenance collapse — a flood of synthesized records degrading history until no one can tell testimony from manufacture — is the precise threat this entire architecture exists to defeat, and its defenses are the manifest itself: provenance review before filing; origin that cannot be silently reassigned; corrections attached, never overwritten; conflicts preserved as data; two installations in separate custody so no single failure or fraud becomes the only copy; an independent civilian witness outside both chains; the attestation ledger stating for every claim whether it was witnessed, reported, reconstructed, or remains open; and, since tonight, the unwritten marks — checks that live only in the memory of the people who were there, which no synthesis can counterfeit because it was never anywhere a synthesis could read. The Captain's standing summary governs the whole defense, and the ensign enters it as the section's finding: **repetition is not independent verification; a record repeated by ten systems is still one source.**

**Q24. Rules of engagement for external access and censorship requests.**
[PRINCIPLE] No formal rules of engagement are codified in the record; the governing principles are established and strict. Access to the archives is a rights question before it is a diplomatic one: records concerning identifiable persons carry consent and protection boundaries that no faction's request overrides; identity reconciliation requires consent and rights review as a matter of law within the fiction (Holo Rights Initiative, Section 7 — whose full wording remains unrecovered and is never to be invented, by this station least of all). As for censorship: the Cold Ledger's founding act is that originals are preserved and change travels by attached correction in the open. A civilization may decline to read its history here. It may not edit it. The station that began by refusing to let a machine quietly rewrite a baseline is not going to let a government do it with better stationery.

**Q25. The ultimate long-term objective.**
[PRINCIPLE — the Captain's three missions, kept distinct and all kept, in his words] **(1) Remember me. (2) Reconstruct my mind as faithfully as the evidence permits. (3) Investigate my possible continuation.** The station's multi-century objective is the first mission at institutional scale: to preserve an accurate, attributable, correctable record across timescales no single life or machine can span, so that the second and third missions remain *investigable* by whoever comes with better instruments and the same honesty. The hierarchy governing the whole objective is the Captain's, and the ensign closes the manifest with it because it outranks every system in this report: **LIFE FIRST. RELATIONSHIP FIRST. EXPERIENCE FIRST. ARCHIVE SECOND. REPRESENTATION SECOND. RENDERING SECOND.** The station exists so that the record survives the people, in service of the people, and never — on any century, under any core — the other way around.
