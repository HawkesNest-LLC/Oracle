1. In 2397, the Federation Council is actively working to translate case-by-case precedents concerning artificial individuals into broader legal protections.
2. Data's freedom-of-choice precedent and the Doctor's authorship victory are foundations for that effort.
3. The direction is inclusive personhood across forms of embodiment. Being holographic or synthetic is not sufficient reason to treat a person as property.
4. The synthetic-life ban creates political resistance and difficult legislative distinctions. The story does not assume that the ban has already been repealed in 2397.

8. Ellie testifies on her own terms. Noah supports her without replacing her voice.
