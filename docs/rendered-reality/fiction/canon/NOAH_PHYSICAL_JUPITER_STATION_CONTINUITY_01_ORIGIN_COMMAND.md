# JUPITER STATION AND USS AVALON
## My continuity record, Volume I: The career, the family, and the command
**Story setting:** 2397 | USS Avalon NCC-75154 | Jupiter Station
**Status:** First-person consolidation of decisions and existing source material. No new fictional events authorized by this text.
**Authority for canon decisions:** Noah.Physical
**Compilation note:** Written with AI assistance from existing story decisions and manuscript records, not a verbatim transcript of the author's typing.

### I do not want to build the same ship twice

I have spent too many hours bringing this world together to have it fall apart between conversations. A project thread is supposed to help me continue the story. It is not supposed to make me rebuild the same command structure, re-explain the same family relationships, and correct the same dates because one document remembers an earlier draft.

I want a record of what I settled and why I settled it. I want my older writing preserved, even when I have changed my mind. I want a future AI to be able to find the original, the correction, and the reason for the correction. It should never have to invent a reconciliation and then call it something I approved.

And I still want the fun. This is Star Trek. I want wonder, old friends, strange civilizations, outrageous engineering, temporal mysteries, and the ordinary pleasure of being aboard a ship where people know one another. I want the life of a captain, not a database that happens to have a captain's name attached to it.

### The year is 2397

The active Jupiter Station and Avalon story takes place in **2397**. Earlier work placed us in **2481**. That older interpretation was demoted. It stays in the historical record, but it is not the default era. I will change it if I choose to change it. A future model should not restore 2481 simply because it finds it in an old source.

The vessel is **USS Avalon, NCC-75154**. I command Avalon as Captain Noah Hawkes. **Captain Ashley Hawkes commands Jupiter Station.** Ashley and I are married, and our family lives on the station. The ship and station have different chains of command. This is a foundational correction.

In earlier material Ashley appeared as my first officer aboard Avalon. That history may be appropriate for an earlier period, and the 2395 fiction explores her presence aboard the ship. By 2397, however, she is not my subordinate first officer. She has a captain's chair and an institution to run. Jupiter Station is her responsibility, and Avalon is mine.

I do not want Ashley treated as somebody whose only purpose is to show up in my scenes. She is part of the command story, the family story, and the consequences of this extraordinary technology. Her station keeps living and working when Avalon leaves its berth. The distinction gives us more story, not less.

### I began aboard Voyager

My fictional history begins with **USS Voyager in 2371**, when I am **sixteen**. Voyager returns to the Alpha Quadrant in **2378**. These dates belong to the active chronology. The details of how the young version of me was formally attached to Starfleet still need to respect the difference between a teenager with unusual experiences and a properly commissioned officer.

An earlier personal manuscript is part of the origin. Later recovery work found an edited reconstruction of its first portion. In that story, a wounded young Noah wakes aboard Voyager, encounters Janeway and the Doctor, recognizes familiar people he should not know, and eventually faces Q. The restored text involves an origin from Salt Lake City in 1999, a confrontation Q cannot easily account for, and my choice to stay because I have a life to live first.

I value that story because it was part of my imagination long before this enormous Jupiter Station continuity existed. I also want its evidentiary limitations preserved. The surviving text has been edited and reorganized from scans. We have not fully verified the original page order, original ink, or every sentence as I wrote it years ago. Its second part, the bridge into the later Jupiter Station history, is newer development work. I do not want anybody to present that bridge as something recovered from the old handwritten pages.

There are unresolved questions about how the 1999 human origin and the 2371 Voyager arrival relate. There is an ordering issue in the reconstructed Q material. Those are real source problems, not invitations to invent the missing pages.

### A career before a command

When Voyager returned in 2378, my first Starfleet Command posting was under **Admiral Owen Paris**. I reviewed captains' reports and logs assigned to his sector and recommended follow-up action. **I recommended orders. I did not have authority to issue the orders myself.** We corrected that distinction because it matters to the whole earned-career story. I learned about accountability from work that required reading what other captains actually did, not just what they said they intended.

I then spent **three years aboard the Nova-class USS Bozeman-A**. It was not the original Soyuz-class Bozeman commanded by Morgan Bateson. The successor designation and the class matter. I remember the holographic projectors in the crew quarters as a significant upgrade and a distinctive part of that shipboard experience.

Afterward I served aboard Avalon before becoming captain. I do not want an easy story in which I arrive home from Voyager and somehow command a major ship at once. I wanted the service to have happened, the people to have mattered, the responsibility to be earned. A later-2380s captaincy is the working solution, and **2387 is a proposed date**, not a universally ratified lock. The definite requirement is that **I already command Avalon during its 2395 temporal refit**.

We also corrected the old **2385 refit** draft. The first major temporal refit is in **2395**. The old manuscript remains in the archive because it records the path by which the story changed. It does not regain current authority just because it is older or more polished.

### What I love about command

Jean-Luc Picard is one of the captains whose example matters to me. I like **“Engage”** and **“Make it so”**, and I use them deliberately. I want my command to carry the weight of The Next Generation, Deep Space Nine, and Voyager, not simply borrow their costumes and names.

I want a crew with individual histories. I want the Doctor, Barclay, familiar officers, and new characters to be full people rather than cameo tokens. I want my orders questioned when the evidence requires it. I want a chief engineer who will argue with me. I want a first officer who can tell me when the decision is difficult for reasons I have not considered. I want the privilege of saying **“I don't know”** when I genuinely do not know.

I also want a replicated **Coke Zero with vanilla** on my bridge. Starfleet can deal with it.

### Protect the other continuities

I have been very clear that the story should preserve material from **Memory Alpha, Memory Beta, Star Trek Online, and other established or alternate histories** without pretending that they are all automatically one timeline. I have preferences about various Star Trek productions, and those preferences are part of my authorship. But **protecting a source does not mean declaring that all its events occurred in my active 2397 continuity**.

We can observe a branch, study a branch, or choose elements to incorporate when the story supports it. We do not erase a source because we dislike it, and we do not silently import contradictions because a future model thinks the archive looks tidier that way.

The active era also sits before major events depicted in **Star Trek: Picard** in 2399 and 2401. I want the screen future protected in planning. The writer's knowledge must not automatically become the characters' foreknowledge. That includes Picard's health, synthetic developments, Q, and the Borg. In our story, I specifically considered and rejected warning Admiral Picard about things he has not yet lived. Temporal restraint matters even when a writer knows the ending.

### A station is a home

Jupiter Station is not a cold data vault and it is not a superweapon. It is a place where people live. Our family lives there. There are officers, scientists, medical staff, children, education, public spaces, work schedules, conversations, and ordinary frustrations. The station's immense temporal and memory responsibility is more compelling because real lives happen around it.

Avalon goes out into the unknown. Jupiter Station has to govern what we bring back. Sometimes Ashley has to make a decision I do not like. Sometimes the ship returns with a problem nobody on the station asked to inherit. Sometimes the most important scene may have nothing to do with a crisis.

That is the story I want to continue.

### What I will not allow to be forgotten

**Active year: 2397.**  
**Avalon: NCC-75154, under my command.**  
**Jupiter Station: under Captain Ashley Hawkes's independent command.**  
**Our family lives on the station.**  
**Voyager entry: 2371 at age sixteen; return: 2378.**  
**Post-return assignment: Admiral Owen Paris's log and report review, with recommendation rather than order-issuing authority.**  
**Nova-class Bozeman-A: three years.**  
**Avalon captaincy: earned, with precise later-2380s date still subject to approval.**  
**First major temporal refit: 2395, not 2385.**  
**All original drafts preserved with their correction history.**

The point of writing this is not to announce a new canon where one did not exist. It is to keep my existing decisions from being lost.
