## Captain Noah Alexander Hawkes

**Citizenship:** Federation citizen in the authored setting.  
**Command:** USS Avalon, modified Sovereign class, base registry NCC-75154; series styling NCC-75154-JS.  
**Appointment:** September 3, 2394, 1000, under this new chronology.  
**Predecessor:** Captain Mara Ellison, adopted for this version from the earlier candidate.  
**Education:** Voyager Academy-equivalency curriculum; post-return institutional completion and graduate preparation at Starfleet Academy's University of Utah annex; doctorate in temporal mechanics defended December 12, 2384. The credential is fictional.  
**Specialties:** Operations, temporal incident investigation, comparative review of captains' logs, witness chronology, mission support, and command.  
**Formation:** Tuvok's Havoc Squad training, Voyager junior-crew life, Admiral Owen Paris's review staff, three years aboard Nova-class Bozeman-A, then ten years aboard Avalon before command.  
**Strengths:** Persistence, attentiveness to contradictory testimony, loyalty, practical responsibility, and willingness to stop an experiment.  
**Weaknesses developed in the story:** Overexplaining, impatience with bureaucracy, pursuing an answer after fatigue has impaired judgment, and mistaking a personally important question for everyone's immediate priority.  
**Bridge texture:** "Engage" and "Make it so" deliberately honor Picard. Replicator: Coke Zero with vanilla, cold.  
**Authority boundary:** Investigative assignment permits evidence gathering and recommendations within its remit. It does not grant an admiral's independent order-issuing power.

Noah arrives on Voyager in 2371 at sixteen as a civilian. Familiarity with Star Trek does not make him qualified. The commission follows coursework, supervised duties, examinations and assessment. Q's invitation is membership in the Continuum, attracted by an unknown variable that might bring fresh life to it. Noah declines. The exact 1999-to-2371 mechanism remains unresolved.

Havoc Squad is a new elaboration of Noah's requested premise: a cross-trained junior-personnel group under Tuvok. It practices casualty recovery, security, damage control, communication loss and field judgment. It is not a secret elite unit that replaces Voyager's established crew. The Lower Decks phrase here means junior Voyager personnel, not an automatic import of the later animated series cast.

Voyager adventures provide service experience. Delta Flyer involvement is mission support followed by supervised flight qualification after the craft exists in the chronology. Year of Hell branch experiences do not automatically become retained prime-branch memories. No erased-history promotion is silently inserted.

Janeway technology is learned through Voyager service, authorized study and later integration review. Familiarity with future-derived defensive systems does not grant unlimited future knowledge or unrestricted weapons access. The detailed workings remain source-bound.

## Captain Ashley Hawkes

**Citizenship:** Federation citizen in the authored setting.  
**Command:** Jupiter Station, independently of Avalon.  
**Appointment:** November 1, 2396, 0900, under this new chronology.  
**Education:** Academy entry 2374, graduation 2378; advanced operations and emergency-coordination study at the Utah annex in 2379.  
**Specialties:** Operations, logistics, emergency coordination, civilian services and fixed-installation command.  
**Career:** Earth-orbital support, fleet logistics, senior station operations in 2392, Jupiter Station executive officer in 2394, station captain in 2396.  
**Strengths developed in the story:** Realistic resource planning, recognizing hidden burdens, protecting civilian continuity, and making decisions without waiting for Noah's agreement.  
**Command principle:** A station must remain habitable while ships and research teams pursue their missions.  
**Family relationship:** Noah's spouse and equal partner. Her independent command is not a marital courtesy or Noah's appointment to give.

Ashley has a professional life before their meeting. She enters the Academy in 2374, graduates in 2378 and gains responsibility through operations. The Utah annex is where their paths intersect, not the beginning of her ambitions. Her staff, civilian responsibilities, and independent witnesses remain her responsibility when Avalon departs.

## Relationship and family

They meet at the Utah annex on February 17, 2379, at 1610. Noah is preparing for doctoral work while completing institutional requirements and serving Paris's staff. Ashley is an ensign pursuing advanced professional study. They marry June 21, 2381. Work, distance, leave, childcare and mutual decisions shape the years that follow. The new story gives those years scenes rather than treating marriage as a field added to a personnel database.

Their Deck Nine home is established in January 2397 in this chronology. The existing setting supplies curved residential quarters, Jupiter outside the glass, wood-grain cabinets with physical handles, feeding places and a holographic backyard. Their family includes the established Hawkes relationships; no child birth dates, maternal histories, or prior-family biographies are invented here. Ellie remains the holographic daughter and lieutenant junior grade in Science AI & Continuity within the active setting. Do not merge the real daughter, Drakin heroine, holodeck role and Starfleet manifestation into one unqualified biography.

**Real-world inspiration, kept separate:** Noah states that Ashley attended Davis High School and he attended Layton. Those are real-world school connections, not invented 24th-century enrollment records. They do not establish a shared school or a real-world first-meeting date.
