### What I regard as controlling current story decisions

My active setting is **2397**, not 2481. I command **USS Avalon**, and **Captain Ashley Hawkes** independently commands **Jupiter Station**; our family lives at the station. My fictional Voyager origin begins in **2371** at age sixteen; Voyager returns in **2378**. I served reviewing captains' reports under Admiral **Owen Paris**, recommending rather than independently issuing sector orders. I then served three years aboard the **Nova-class USS Bozeman-A**, distinct from the original Soyuz-class Bozeman. My command of Avalon must be earned, with the precise 2380s captaincy date still subject to final reconciliation.

My **first major 2395 Avalon temporal refit and full-core catastrophe** supersede the earlier 2385 date and any implication that the first full activation was merely a harmless one-second anomaly. **Reginald Endicott Barclay** saves the Sol system using a **Cytherian neural-interface chair**, loses his biological body, and **continues as himself within the fiction** as **REG420**, integrated with Avalon's computer and the Sloan-linked AI. That is story canon, not scientific evidence for real-world human mind transfer.

The Temporal Core can deliberately observe **resolvable** histories, with source separation through **Memory Alpha / Memory Beta**, preserved conflicts in the **Cold Ledger**, and human-governed command review. It is not omniscient and does not grant an automatic right to intervene. The claimed scale is approximately **a million Federation-origin repository domains**, not a million fully explored universes.

Our doctrine is **REG renders. Hawkes arbitrates. Jake witnesses. Avalon carries the receipt.** The Hawkes Doctrine is **We are allowed to know more than we are allowed to do**.

### What I have NOT settled

The new Vulcan first officer's **name is still open according to the explicit Oct 12 correction**. Some chapters use the AI-suggested name **Sorev**. Neither repetition nor literary polish automatically makes the name final. The proposed Dominion War origin includes candidate names such as **Elias Mercer** and **USS Meridian**.

The **2387** assumption-of-command date is a plausible chronology proposal, not a universal lock.

The precise engineering mechanisms of the 2395 catastrophe, the lunar Witness Horizon interpretation, and certain technical safety devices are source-marked developments. I may adopt them, but the six volumes cannot do that for me.

The **12 episode pitches, six-season designs, eight-novel cycle, detailed Jake reporting scenes, proposed Federation AI rights legislation, the Eye of the Planet, Iconian and Progenitor clues, Sphere Builder defenses, and unresolved temporal records** must retain the status assigned by their respective primary sources.

I have adopted Hawkes–SOV **as a fictional discipline**, not proved a new real physics law or granted automatic canon status to every parameter in every old research paper.
