## Part One recoverable story events

1. A wounded Noah recognizes people and technology aboard Voyager, then loses consciousness.
2. He wakes restrained within a large tube, hears Janeway welcome him aboard Voyager, recognizes her and loses consciousness again.
3. In Sickbay, the Doctor greets him; Noah thanks him and asks about B'Elanna, whose life he has reportedly saved.
4. Janeway, Chakotay, Torres and Tuvok arrive. Noah admits he comes from Salt Lake City, Utah, in the year 1999, and has watched/read their apparent universe before.
5. Q materializes, asks 'Who are you?', cannot account for Noah, calls him a threat and attacks.
6. The attack is reversed; Q apparently dies; the Doctor reports no life signs.
7. Noah restores Q, receives an extraordinary expansion of understanding, is embraced by Q and offered a return.
8. Noah refuses, saying he has a life to live first, and offers to help the Voyager crew.
9. A passage describing Q unconscious and mutual recognition appears AFTER the seeming ending in this edited reconstruction. Preserve as an **ordering problem**, not a duplicate historical event.

Short pivotal quoted expressions appear in the 2026-10-12 conversation and are preserved in the original connected source. Do not recast AI-generated later dialogue as handwritten original.

## Unresolved facts and contradictions

- Actual original page order and illegible terms require T0 photographs and faithful T1 manual transcription.
- Age 16/17 at arrival and 1999 terrestrial event versus 2371 fictional ship-year require deliberate treatment; do not silently normalize.
- First captain of Avalon and exact Noah captaincy year remain subject to newer human corrections and competing drafts. Reject the shortcut promotion that made Noah the ship's first captain in 2379.
- Preserve active story authority: Ashley commands Jupiter Station, Noah commands Avalon NCC-75154; family lives on station; Avalon refit 2395; REG continuity role; Jake external witness; Ellie/Tangly render and holodeck policy per latest author corrections.
- Two public social holodecks, two reservable private holodecks; distributed holo emitters throughout quarters and ship are author-specified worldbuilding in the current conversation. In-world Holo Rights Initiative Section 7 is a story law, NOT independently verified Star Trek franchise or actual legal charter.
