## Editorial separation of authorities
1. **Human testimony:** firsthand words, dated personal accounts, original memory, and uncertainty. Truth of a recollection and accuracy of every underlying event are different questions. Requires privacy and consent gates.
2. **Philosophical manuscript:** Rendered Reality: A Logical Exploration. Use its own argument, original order, and source provenance; do not turn belief into laboratory finding.
3. **CWP paper:** Cognitive World Projection and Rendered Reality. Treat as the technical/research account with citations, definitions, testable claims, and limitations. Do not infer peer review or publication acceptance from a draft.
4. **October corrections and governance:** creator's authority, source classes, research CANON-001, append-only corrections, no software restart, no unverified implementation claims.
5. **Creative work:** Drakin, Jupiter Station, Memory's Flame, and other fiction as authored narrative. They illustrate ideas and are primary evidence of creative work, not independent proof of biography or engineering.

## Proposed book movement
**Part I: The Human Before the Framework.** Establish the narrator's voice and motivation using permissioned firsthand sources only. Mark contested recollection as recollection, not forensic fact. No third-party story without consent.

**Part II: Rendered Reality, the Argument.** Let the philosophical manuscript supply the spine. Contrast remembered experience, interpretation, belief, and adopted doctrine. Show correction as an act of fidelity rather than loss.

**Part III: The Architecture and Its Tests.** Let CWP frame measurable continuity questions; put Legacy.GI, ORACLE witness/retrieval, SOV1 governance, and HYDRA concepts in their proper roles. Research CANON-001 adopts rate-distortion framing and six fidelity dimensions while explicitly refusing to claim proven identity preservation. Distinguish prototypes, documents, conjectures, and independent measurements.

**Part IV: Fiction as the Testing Ground.** Separate imaginative Star Trek and original fiction from empirical claims. Show what the ethical problems feel like to characters without laundering fiction into proof.

**Part V: What Remains Unanswered.** Rights and consent, uncertainty, scientific falsifiability, publication gates, failed retrievals, and the next writing batch. Close with documented questions rather than a false final synthesis.
