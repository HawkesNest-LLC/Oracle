## Governing Principles

**Life first. The archive stays downstream.**

A journal preserves the inside of a life. Genealogy preserves relationships around it. Provenance preserves trust. Correction preserves change. Uncertainty preserves honesty.

The point is not to turn people into research objects. The people are the point.

This cycle preserves first-person reflection separately from scientific claims. Personal memories remain personal testimony. Research constructs remain testable interpretations rather than retroactive facts.

The thought disappears. The traces do not.

The human keeps the keys. The system keeps the receipts.

The ship remembers. The captain decides.

And if the evidence stops, stop with it. Do not invent the rest.
