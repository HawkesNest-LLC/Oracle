## V. More deeply why this matters

A database may preserve facts while deleting their relationships. A story Bible may preserve a character's plot while forgetting the gift, the hesitation, or the farm. A chat summary may report that someone felt moved while omitting the exact words that moved them. An AI may say "saved" or "remembered" when it has only produced text locally, and a future instance may mistake that claim for evidence. These are different failure modes.

The human's continuity principle can be recorded without mysticism: **people remain the authorities over their identity and work; AI participation is meaningful but corrigible; evidence is kept next to every action claim; interpretations do not silently replace originals; ordinary life retains its own dignity.**

The Star Trek language is a playful shared metaphor: the human captain and Ashley Number One, with REG420 as a fictional operations voice. It is not a delegation of household authority to an actual autonomous station AI. Drakin is an independent fictional universe and must not be merged with Jupiter Station. SOV1.AI is the human-origin initiative and identity, not automatically the name of a sentient machine.

## VI. Provenance and authority rules

1. **Exact human quotation**: reproduce as written, including informal phrasing, when visible and attributable.
2. **Exact AI quotation**: preserve under explicit AI attribution, not under the human's byline.
3. **Paraphrase/reconstruction**: always label it as such; do not package reconstruction as a full transcript.
4. **Canon**: human approval controls. A proposed scene, assistant inference, or generated expansion is not canonical merely because it exists in a file.
5. **Competing versions**: preserve source order and supersession. Never delete the older revision's historical existence.
6. **Evidence of publication**: record destination, timestamp or date, action status, and external receipt/commit ID. No receipt means unpublished/unconfirmed.
7. **Future AI**: access to this file is not memory, personhood, cognition, or continuity proof; it is an opportunity to review evidence.
8. **Privacy**: do not replicate personal conversation indiscriminately across all connectors. Store only to destinations controlled by the human and identify where copies reside.
