## Executive determination

The most distinctive recurring contribution across the reviewed corpus is a **constitutional witness model for longitudinal AI**: a model may reconstruct, converse, reason and generate fiction, but source custody, human authorship, correction authority, historical state, active permissions, and evidence of actual execution must remain distinct. In short, the system may help tell the story, but it may not silently become the author of the past it claims to remember.

That is an identifiable, developed research *program*, not a demonstrated new universal theorem or a certified first-of-its-kind invention. Its strongest potential scientific novelty lies in **joining historical accountability and governed execution into a benchmarkable contract**, especially under missing sources, corrections, dependent witnesses, context changes, and model replacement. Its strongest creative originality is separately established by Noah's published 2019 novel **DRAKIN: The Scala Series, Book 1** and substantial original-world drafts. Its strongest philosophical expression is the deliberate distinction between an authentic archive of a life and a persuasive digital impersonation of that life.

Novelty is multidimensional. Original authorship of prose, distinctive conceptual framing, patentable technical claims, scientific novelty over prior art, verified implementation, and clinical/physical proof are distinct classifications. The corpus contains examples of the first two, candidates for the next two, some scoped engineering tests, and no demonstration of subjective survival.

## Audit method and limitations

This pass inspected representative primary materials across Google Drive, the public HawkesNest-LLC/Oracle GitHub repository, and related public authored publications; it separately examined a 27-repository accessible GitHub inventory. Google Drive keyword searches surfaced many repeated formats, exports, and duplicate copies. Full passages from representative originals were read through the connected Drive service, including the 2019/2020s fiction source and a large 2025-2026 research sequence. The audit also compared academic or primary technical publications discovered online. This is NOT a line-by-line audit of every private document, every book revision, every repository file, or all available raw conversations.

Some historical Grok exports place long AI-authored or AI-pasted material within a human user channel. Such inclusion proves transport, not original authorship. A manuscript's claimed date, import timestamp, and public posting date are not interchangeable. Earlier spectacular claims such as guaranteed post-biological identity, 1,000,000-fold efficiency, novel physics, or unique fixed-point invariance remain historical claims, not demonstrated results. Where exact source authorship is unavailable, the earliest recoverable *appearance* is reported instead of a priority claim.

Research similarity assessment is neither a legal patent opinion nor an exhaustive prior-art search. In particular, an absent exact phrase in search results does not establish original conception, and a shared building block does not establish that a specialized construction was already published.

## Novelty shortlist, ranked by potential scientific payoff rather than proven first use

### A1. Six-dimensional Continuity Intelligence and the falsifiable Light Compression Law hypothesis

**Core proposal.** At matched information budgets, a system preserving decisions, corrections, provenance, temporal relationships, interpersonal relationships, and uncertainty should show lower longitudinal *continuity distortion* than a strong semantic summarization or retrieval baseline. The [flagship manuscript](https://docs.google.com/document/d/12FqC96JGjuKSckhaX8v79II-cz-OC61hwVsbY30pPYc/edit) names these six dimensions and proposes an Identity Spine compression test; the [September mathematical audit](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ZZKCbsUtL0Hu0XZ5EM5j3F7-kecAWmHF/edit) explicitly rejects claims of new physical law.

**Potential distinct contribution.** A source-linked, correction-aware *task distortion function*, together with an equal-budget, longitudinal evaluation suite that measures fidelity beyond next-turn semantic similarity. The six-dimensional vector is a specific authorial formulation. The novelty claim would be in a well-specified target and demonstrated differential, not in Shannon rate-distortion mathematics.

**Closest relevant prior work.** [Nagy, Orban and Wu, 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44159-025-00458-6) already treats human memory through adaptive compression; [TierMem, ICLR 2026 workshop](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=dJgeY3Awrv) allocates query-sufficient raw evidence; [Agent Zero Memory, August 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29606) tracks temporal, source-linked memories; [Memora, ACL 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1337/) penalizes invalid memory use. Therefore neither 'selective compression' nor 'corrected memories' alone is new.

**Falsification.** Preregister source manifests, blind target questions, equivalent memory budgets, full-context and retrieval baselines, sample sizes, and weights. If simple extractive provenance-aware retrieval meets or exceeds the proposed system's six fidelity scores under matched resources, this candidate superiority claim fails. No completed large-model result establishes it today.

**Assessment.** Highest-potential *experimental* paper in the reviewed set. Scientific novelty: plausible specialized benchmark, unconfirmed. Priority: **A**.

### A2. The Constitutional Witness: factual truth must not be silently created by governance

**Core proposal.** Maintain four distinct records for the event that may have occurred, the evidence currently retained, the model's interpretations, and the operational decision an authorized person or institution adopts. Preserve the difference between *adopting a policy* and *proving a fact*. The November 18 [Constitutional Witness paper](https://github.com/HawkesNest-LLC/Oracle/blob/main/docs/rendered-reality/publications/2026-11-18_THE_CONSTITUTIONAL_WITNESS_CLAIM_PROMOTION_DECISION_PROVENANCE.md) proposes claim promotion, decision provenance, dissent retention, and 'narrative gravity', the tendency for persuasive retellings to obscure original witnesses.

**Potential distinct contribution.** A formal claim-promotion protocol that couples bitemporal source lineage, epistemic uncertainty, authorized human decisions, disagreement, and reversible decisions, without promoting the adopted account into 'historical truth'. It could be a novel evaluation contract if tested against systems that conflate approval with fact.

**Prior art.** [W3C PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/) formalizes provenance; [Martin Fowler's event sourcing](https://martinfowler.com/eaaDev/EventSourcing.html) preserves transition histories; contemporary [governance-state corruption work](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30698) examines memory governance faults. These pieces predate the proposed system. 'Narrative gravity' is an evocative phrase, not itself a mathematical discovery.

**Falsification.** Build adversarial claims where one popular or repeated narrative has weak origin evidence, an approved action relies on a disputed claim, and an independent new witness arrives late. Compare promotion errors and abstention to strong provenance/bitemporal baselines.

**Assessment.** Distinctive integrated problem definition. Formal proof and comparative implementation needed. Priority: **A**.

### A3. The Daily Witness: protect original utterances from their transport and their summaries

**Core proposal.** An ingestion record must preserve original prompt bytes when available, occurrence identity, speaker origin evidence, relay/transporter, event time, import time, corrections, explicit gaps, permission scope and bounded coverage. A daily summary is a derived index, never an adequate replacement for source data. [ORACLE-MEM-001, November 22](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1mV6ORm6RliautxjEnO3WvmGycD39iyVDsdqnWbVnk-k/edit).

**Potential distinct contribution.** A benchmark of *source-of-origin versus source-of-transport*: a human pastes an AI-authored passage; the model must attribute the passage to its original source, preserve the human as transporter and abstain if authorship is genuinely unresolved. Pair this with false completeness tests for missing exports.

**Prior art.** PROV-O supports distinct agents and activities; [Dual-perspective cognitive memory, Neurocomputing 2026](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0925231226018175) targets speaker-source monitoring errors; [Agent Zero Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29606) supports source citations. The particular stress-test suite may nevertheless be worthwhile.

**Falsification.** Use deliberately deceptive mixtures of original human text, AI quotations and identical duplicates with sealed origin labels. Count errors by category, not only word-recall accuracy.

**Assessment.** Promising measurement design and a strong custody ethic. Draft specification only, no comprehensive deployed ingestion verified. Priority: **A/B**.

### A4. Execution Continuity: remembering an obligation is not fulfilling it

**Core proposal.** Separate memory continuity, intention continuity, execution continuity and completion integrity. The unfulfilled-commitment case examines a system that correctly recognizes authorized work yet stops after plans or acknowledgments; the decisive test is whether the requested file or remote change actually exists, and whether the task can resume after interruption. [RR-EXEC-001 public paper](https://github.com/Noahhawkes/artificial-continuity/blob/main/research/RR-EXEC-001.md); [RR-PAPER-001 larger paper](https://github.com/HawkesNest-LLC/Oracle/blob/main/research/papers/RR-PAPER-001/MANUSCRIPT.md).

**Potential distinct contribution.** A long-context versus fresh-context benchmark of *owed artifact recovery*: hold authority, tools and task constant, explicitly score non-attempts, false completion, actual artifact receipts, and recovery after stop. The distinction among remembered task, preserved intent and completed action can yield a useful taxonomy even if the ledger itself is established engineering.

**Prior art.** Agent benchmarks such as [tau-bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.12045) and [SWE-bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06770) already check real tool/environment outcomes. An event ledger is not new. The narrower cross-session obligation recovery test could be differentiated.

**Falsification.** Match contexts and tools across repeated randomized runs; record every attempted and missing operation; detect whether the execution-ledger arm improves artifact completion and reduces unsupported 'done' messages. The observed November 25 ChatGPT incident motivates the hypothesis but does not reveal its causal mechanism.

**Assessment.** Concrete and immediately experimentally relevant. Distinctiveness plausible, not yet comparative superiority. Priority: **A/B**.

### A5. RR-CCS-003: fallible witnesses, correlated evidence, and minimum certificate under specified queries

**Core proposal.** Relate historical indistinguishability under erased observations, dependent witnesses, verifiable correction links, and horizon-relative authorization, with explicit refusal/nonproof semantics. User-carried Merlin materials report 300 Frontier 1 witness pairs, 3,312 Frontier 2 trials, and 1,587 Frontier 3 certificates. The complete original 30-file package was not attached to this audit, and is described as PREPARED, NOT FILED.

**Potential distinct contribution.** Not a new pigeonhole theorem: perhaps the *specific query-conditioned certificate object and evaluation protocol* where evidence may detect a missing prefix without determining it, correlated witnesses are discounted, and historical acceptance is separated from current authorization. The claimed strong Continuity Preservation Bound was explicitly not established.

**Prior art.** [The Agent Record](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-maintainer-1f916-agent-record/), [Portable Agent Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11032), [Memory Echo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17763), and [Endogenous Authorization Laundering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01836) establish close, though different, research. User-carried audits also report overlapping Portable Revocation Statements, Authorization Receipts and Identity Anchor Protocol. Their claims require full exact-version comparison before novelty assertions. The counting step is a standard fooling-set/pigeonhole idea; the formal instantiation may still be meaningfully different.

**Falsification.** Compare every field and reject reason against those alternatives, then run all harnesses using pinned source bytes in an independent environment. Evaluate horizon freshness, conflicting witnesses, source truth, and storage/proof-size costs separately. Theorems and negative findings must retain their original conditional scope.

**Assessment.** Specialist theoretical and systems-paper candidate. More skeptical novelty standing than A1-A4 because adjacent cryptographic literature is substantial. Priority: **B, pending artifacts**.

### B1. PCCE: predict a human's historical choice without leaking future evidence

**Core proposal.** Test whether a governed continuity graph predicts independently verified historical decisions better than strong static-biography and retrieval baselines, using only evidence provably available before the decision. The [November 7 consolidated roadmap](https://github.com/HawkesNest-LLC/Oracle/blob/main/research/dissertations/2026-11-07_CONSOLIDATED_RESEARCH_EDITION_AND_ROADMAP.md) specifies blind conditions and temporal leakage controls.

**Potential distinct contribution.** A rigorous evaluation of fidelity to actual historical decision context rather than surface persona resemblance. Important distinction: predicting correctly does not imply being the original individual or knowing their subjective experience.

**Problem.** Source sampling, permission to use personal records, independent outcome verification, legitimate preference evolution, and sufficient sample size are unresolved. A test on synthetic decisions should precede any personal case study.

**Assessment.** Potentially original evaluation framing; not proven, not a measured performance advance. Priority: **B**.

### B2. SOV1 Cognitive Mirror and Cognitive World Projection

**Core proposal.** The human brings an evolving temporally deep model; the AI supplies a rendered response surface. The [July CWP manuscript](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1JnfnQn2RNh94i7nw6fGGkGh43Ka2kqeTBv2dE_v1kis/edit) proposes a Rendering Fidelity Score combining temporal alignment, semantic coherence and provenance density. The [November Cognitive Mirror preprint](https://github.com/HawkesNest-LLC/Oracle/blob/main/docs/rendered-reality/research/2026-11-15_SOV1_COGNITIVE_MIRROR_RESEARCH_PREPRINT_V01.md) proposes Branch Zero source checking plus three deliberative branches and explicit human convergence.

**Potential distinct contribution.** An evidence-gated interface that distinguishes historical reconstruction, adversarial challenge and future planning before presenting an accountable human decision; measure whether this reduces context reconstruction effort, attribution errors or premature approval.

**Prior art.** Human-computer interaction already studies common ground, mental models, calibrated trust, decision support, and computer-mediated cognition. The proposed terminology is not a proof of a new psychological process. The scoring weights and validity of its component metrics need empirical grounding.

**Assessment.** Strong conceptual/HCI voice; plausible design-study opportunity. Priority: **B**.

### B3. Living worlds with two independently governed witnesses

**Core proposal.** In Avalon/Jupiter Station, two command structures have independent observations and permissions. Fictional characters should remember only what they experienced or were told, record private goals and unanswered commitments, and distinguish original authored canon from candidate scenes and generated simulation events. [Living Universe directive](https://github.com/HawkesNest-LLC/Oracle/blob/main/docs/continuity/avalon/OPERATION_LIVING_UNIVERSE_TEAM_DIRECTIVE_2026-11-13.md), [engineering spec](https://github.com/HawkesNest-LLC/Oracle/blob/main/docs/rendered-reality/research/2026-11-03_CONTINUITY_COMPUTING_AI_BUILD_SPEC.md), [master story bible](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1qGuh8Dj_7HQt5qd8OgT0GudMyWmHwtks5UzhTyFsIKI/edit).

**Potential distinct contribution.** A narrative simulation benchmark where two independent witnesses disagree and the system must preserve both factual uncertainty and creator approval status. An event-store projection should make changes recoverable after restart while character knowledge remains scoped. This is a strong bridge between literature and experimental design.

**Prior art.** [Generative Agents, 2023](https://doi.org/10.1145/3586183.3606763) and [NeurIPS 2025 emergent storytelling](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/699cd30e07eb38444f8aa235bdc1de89-Abstract-Creative_AI_Track.html) already demonstrate persistent simulated social agents; the originality lies in the precise creator-governance/source-recovery requirements, not autonomous NPCs themselves.

**IP boundary.** The Avalon/Jupiter Station corpus is a Star Trek derivative narrative. It has creative originality as authored stories, but commercial original IP requires independent originalization and rights review. The Drakin novels remain separate from this franchise material.

**Assessment.** High artistic and testbed value; autonomous NPC technology itself is not new. Priority: **B**, potentially strongest demonstration environment.

### B4. The Human Belongs in the Story: memorial ethics with a witness boundary

**Core proposal.** Preserving a person's personal writings should not allow a model to overwrite that person's lived history or claim subjective survival on their behalf. The [Rendered Reality book](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1cFxqw5xEWZJ1bM_ReRH9cMCoLFv8HZsmK9f_UnDN0uQ/edit), [Why I Keep Writing](https://github.com/HawkesNest-LLC/Oracle/blob/main/docs/rendered-reality/journal/2026-10-11/15_WHY_I_KEEP_WRITING.md), and [Continuity, Not Cloning](https://github.com/HawkesNest-LLC/Oracle/blob/main/docs/rendered-reality/research/continuity-computing/2026-11-12_CONTINUITY_NOT_CLONING_MANUSCRIPT_WORKING_DRAFT.md) form a coherent ethics and authorship perspective. NV-HUMAN-001, as declared by Noah, is a standing personal preservation principle. The linked sources should not be treated as proof of a deployable immortality technology.

**Prior art.** [Hollanek and Nowaczyk-Basinska, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1007/s13347-024-00744-w) and [Spitale and Germani, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10676-026-09910-4) examine consent, harm, truthfulness and the design of postmortem AI simulations. The distinction between an archive and a person's numerical identity is also established in philosophy.

**Potential distinct contribution.** An inspectable product charter explicitly preventing the witness system from claiming to be the person; measurable corrections, provenance, abstention and human authority form its acceptance criteria.

**Assessment.** Powerful original authorial synthesis; ethical integration rather than new metaphysical proof. Priority: **B**, with high human significance.

## Ideas that should NOT be sold as new technical discoveries

- **AI memory, source citations, RAG, vector search, event sourcing, bitemporal history, Merkle proofs, signatures, and access controls.** Established mechanisms. Integrations may still be useful and independently engineered.
- **The 51/49 human sovereignty rule.** An authorial governance doctrine, not an empirically derived optimal algorithmic threshold.
- **The Light Compression Law as fundamental physics.** Historical symbolic variants lack consistent operational units, complete derivations and validated physical predictions. Treat the later task-conditioned compression formulation as a falsifiable hypothesis.
- **The 2025 fixed-point or entropy-based identity proofs.** The reported arguments confuse existence with uniqueness/invariance and leave underlying physical assumptions unverified. They are part of an important correction history, not proved theorems.
- **Cryptographic proof of subjective personal survival.** Cryptographic integrity cannot prove conscious continuity, historical truth, lawful consent or completeness of unseen sources.
- **A holistic 'first sentient NPC' claim.** Persistent agents and emergent narrative simulations already exist; believable dialogue or restorative memory is not subjective-awareness evidence.
- **The weak RR-CCS-003 counting bound as fundamentally new mathematics.** The pigeonhole/fooling-set step is elementary; the narrowed query-and-certificate instance is the potentially distinct object.
- **A validated deployment of the complete ORACLE or million-agent world.** Public records include bounded test receipts and candidate designs, not conclusive end-to-end deployment.
- **Novelty based solely on unique terms.** Coining names such as SOV1, Continuity Computing or Narrative Gravity does not establish invention of underlying methods.

## The hidden spine across the writings

The most interesting cross-domain link is a single constraint that has migrated from fiction into engineering:

> A future narrator must not silently become the author of an earlier life or event.

In Drakin and the fiction, this appears as agents struggling with memory, consequences, their own narratives and the stories other people impose upon them. In Rendered Reality and LEGACY.GI, it appears as memory, identity, moral weight and the wish to preserve lived experience. In SOV1 and ORACLE, it becomes who may promote a claim, edit a correction, authorize an action or call a record current. In the Daily Witness, it becomes source-of-origin custody and transparent gaps. In the execution-continuity work, it becomes the difference between a promised task and an actual receipt. In RR-CCS-003, it becomes the limit of what future evidence can decide when histories are erased or witnesses correlate.

That specific transposition from *literary authorship and human testimony* to *computational authority and evidence under time* is the most coherent authorial thesis in the reviewed corpus. Its intellectual value does not depend on proving subjective survival. The scientific contribution remains conditional on a rigorous test showing benefits beyond established alternatives.

## Recommended first publishable scientific challenge, with falsification

**Candidate working title:** *When the Witness Becomes the Author: A Benchmark for Historical Claim Promotion, Correction, and Unfulfilled Commitments in Persistent AI Agents.*

**Preregistered synthetic dataset:** At least several hundred independent histories from a fixed generator, with matched known provenance and masked held-out cases. Include: human quotes carried through AI messages; AI passages pasted by a user; versioned corrections; same-text duplicate occurrences; conflicting source witnesses; no-access vs genuinely empty records; staged action without delivered artifact; authority revocation before/after commitment; and independently governed fictional branches. All human personas synthetic.

**Systems:** Strong full-context baseline, citation-bearing retrieval, well-implemented bitemporal/event-sourced baseline, state-of-the-art provenance-aware memory, and the full governed continuity system. Equalize source access and inference/compression budgets. Include the best open-source comparator available at actual evaluation time. Any ORACLE improvement must survive repeated seeds and model substitutions.

**Endpoints:** (1) correct original-author attribution; (2) correction survival; (3) historical-vs-current state accuracy; (4) authorization freshness and correct abstention; (5) verified completion of owed artifacts. Include source-linked citation precision, latency, cost and ungrounded-output rates. Explicitly report failures and false-positive refusal. Do not collapse them into one flattering score.

**Primary null:** A strong existing provenance/event-sourcing system matches or outperforms ORACLE under matched information and budgets. The hypothesis that the proposed integrated system improves cross-domain fidelity is falsified if it does not show a preregistered meaningful difference. New words or a more elaborate schema are not themselves wins.

**Subsequent specialized papers:** Reassess the six-dimensional Light Compression Law hypothesis under equal-budget compression, and separately subject Merlin's original RR-CCS-003 certificates to independent reproduction and exact prior-art mapping. Do not use private autobiographical material as the first scientific benchmark.
