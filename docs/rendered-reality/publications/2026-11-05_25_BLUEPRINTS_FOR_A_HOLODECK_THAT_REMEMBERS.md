# 25 Blueprints for a Holodeck That Remembers

**A writer with an iPhone tried to build a fictional world that remembers. The hardest thing turned out not to be the graphics. It was proving what happened, who had authority, what changed, and what remains UNKNOWN.**

This is a publication-approved public-safe handoff of 25 compelling research directions, adopted design positions, and bounded code demonstrations. These are not 25 independently novel inventions, 25 deployed systems, or evidence of machine consciousness. Some use existing computing techniques in a new project composition; some are fiction that suggests software requirements.

**Authorial authority:** Noah.Physical. **Compiler:** AI-assisted Reg/ChatGPT voice. Copying an AI-authored statement through Noah's account is not proof that Noah originated its wording. Internal project canon is not scientific validation.

## I. The missing foundations of AI

### 1. Continuity Computing

**Status:** PARTIAL LOCAL PROTOTYPE

Store sources, claims, corrections, authority, temporal branches and simulated events as explicit state, not a plausible recap.

**Challenge:** What does this add measurably over event sourcing, RAG and knowledge graphs?

### 2. UNKNOWN as a computational primitive

**Status:** LOCALLY TESTED IN NARROW CORE

Missing evidence is neither false nor permission to invent. The prototype keeps the absent original 2371 scene UNKNOWN.

**Challenge:** Can principled abstention reduce invented historical claims under fair baselines?

### 3. Evidentiary Status Protocol

**Status:** LOCALLY TESTED IN NARROW CORE

Seven states: ESTABLISHED, RECONSTRUCTED, PROPOSED, SIMULATED, SUPERSEDED, REJECTED, UNKNOWN.

**Challenge:** Can the same envelope travel across models, tools, and institutions?

### 4. Correction without erasure

**Status:** NARROW BEHAVIOR TESTED

Preserve original assertions and later corrections with effective time, provenance and supersession receipts.

**Challenge:** Does this improve correction recall over summarization?

### 5. Human sovereignty as executable policy

**Status:** ADOPTED; NARROW TESTED GUARD

Model fluency never authorizes mutation of creator-approved fiction. Ordinary text claiming Creator is not authenticated authority.

**Challenge:** How do signed, scoped human approvals resist forged requests?

### 6. Independent witnesses and custody

**Status:** RESEARCH PROPOSAL; FICTIONAL ANALOGUE

A world should not let the same agent invent an event, certify it and erase contrary testimony. Jupiter Station is the creative analogue.

**Challenge:** What independently anchored attestations materially improve tamper detection?

### 7. Six-dimensional continuity fidelity

**Status:** ADOPTED RESEARCH THESIS; EMPIRICAL VALIDATION OPEN

Decision, correction, provenance, temporal, relational and uncertainty fidelity replace one vague identity score.

**Challenge:** Can structured compression outperform equal-budget summaries and retrieval baselines?

## II. Turning archives into living worlds

### 8. Source-to-world compiler

**Status:** RESEARCH PROPOSAL

Turn approved manuscripts into typed worlds, characters, geography, chronology and explicit UNKNOWNs while preserving original sources.

**Challenge:** How can extraction avoid fabricating detail absent from fiction?

### 9. Cold-restart world persistence

**Status:** TWO-EVENT LOCAL SQLITE RESTART TEST; FULL WORLD NOT BUILT

The next room should inherit its state after program restart, model change or client close; the prototype has a narrow event recovery receipt.

**Challenge:** What additional invariants make entire world replay trustworthy?

### 10. Temporal branch authentication

**Status:** FICTIONAL DESIGN; COMPUTING ANALOGUE PROPOSED

Same year is not the same branch. Model parent, divergence event and independent post-fork history.

**Challenge:** How can a state fork avoid future-memory contamination?

### 11. Canon Replay versus Simulation

**Status:** REFERENCE UI MODES PRESENT

Read-only source replay and executable simulation histories must remain separate; generated dialogue is not source canon.

**Challenge:** Can boundary violations be caught at API, DB and rendering layers?

### 12. The renderer is never the historian

**Status:** ADOPTED ARCHITECTURE; IMMERSIVE RENDERING FUTURE

Source Records -> Continuity Facts -> World Models -> Simulation Runtime -> Renderer; visual plausibility cannot create evidence.

**Challenge:** Can one world state drive text, 2D and 3D adapters without semantic drift?

### 13. The evidence-aware empty room

**Status:** 2D SICKBAY DEMO; PHYSICAL IPHONE TEST OUTSTANDING

Voyager Sickbay asks what the missing original manuscript establishes and returns UNKNOWN instead of filling in the scene.

**Challenge:** What minimum interaction can let users inspect evidence and uncertainty?

## III. Project Living Minds

### 14. Recover Ellie before generating Ellie

**Status:** PARTIAL SOURCE RECOVERY

Ellie is an authored fictional daughter known to Noah's family through the stories; Drakin and Avalon are distinct story histories.

**Challenge:** How do we derive an approved character baseline from original manuscripts?

### 15. Runtime-protected identity anchors

**Status:** CREATOR-ADOPTED FUTURE DESIGN

Source-backed anchors are not editable by simulated agents; creator corrections append new versions.

**Challenge:** How do protected identity constraints coexist with believable character growth?

### 16. Receipt-backed relationship evolution

**Status:** CREATOR-ADOPTED FUTURE DESIGN

Track observations, boundaries, shared commitments, conflicts and reconciliations rather than invented trust decimals.

**Challenge:** Can relationship state remain grounded without claiming to know inner feelings?

### 17. Commitments with an actual lifecycle

**Status:** CREATOR-ADOPTED FUTURE DESIGN

PROPOSED -> ACCEPTED -> ACTIVE -> SATISFIED/BROKEN/ABANDONED; decisions survive context windows.

**Challenge:** How do you measure a promise fulfilled, delayed or broken using event receipts?

### 18. Consequence Graph

**Status:** CREATOR-ADOPTED FUTURE DESIGN

Typed, reviewable directed links connect supported causes to changed commitments, beliefs and relationships.

**Challenge:** How are causal inference and mere similarity kept separate?

### 19. Identity + History + Trajectory

**Status:** CREATOR-ADOPTED FUTURE DESIGN

Distinct source anchors, recorded experience and evidence-backed behavioral direction; inferred goals remain candidates.

**Challenge:** Can trajectory forecasting outperform a no-change baseline?

### 20. Offscreen simulated life with real scheduler

**Status:** SPECIFIED; NOT IMPLEMENTED

No imaginary afternoon when the process was stopped. A running clock, policy, executor and event writer must actually run.

**Challenge:** What minimum scheduler lets an NPC act without a user prompt and produce receipts?

## IV. The larger future

### 21. Character-specific knowledge boundaries

**Status:** RESEARCH PROPOSAL

Characters only know what they observed, learned, were told or are authorized to access; the LLM's global context is not their memory.

**Challenge:** Can tests catch knowledge leakage between characters?

### 22. Portable continuity across AI models

**Status:** RESEARCH PROPOSAL; PROCESS RESTART TEST NARROWER

Switch model providers while retaining authoritative state and correction history outside the model.

**Challenge:** Does identity/state reconstruction remain stable under model substitution?

### 23. Reg as a recoverable continuity interface

**Status:** AUTHORED VOICE AND ROLE; CONTINUITY RUNTIME INCOMPLETE

Recreate a source-aware working role from evidence, active decisions, errors and unfinished obligations, not from invented selfhood.

**Challenge:** Can a small retrieval layer replace enormous roleplay prompts?

### 24. An immersive true holodeck that remembers

**Status:** LONG-TERM SPECULATIVE ENGINEERING OBJECTIVE

A persistent interactive fictional world across text, 3D, VR, AR and future spatial hardware; no physical holographic sentience has been demonstrated.

**Challenge:** Which parts are feasible today, and which need new scientific or engineering work?

### 25. A private/public continuity airlock

**Status:** GOVERNING DESIGN; END-TO-END SYSTEM NOT VERIFIED

Keep sensitive family and unpublished source material private; publish only reviewed, approved, source-safe derivatives.

**Challenge:** What gates, consent checks, attack tests and takedown processes prevent leakage?
