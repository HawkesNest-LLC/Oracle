What this means

The model compares an idealized inertial traveler clock with a stationary clock in the selected Earth reference frame. A real round trip requires acceleration and turnaround modeling. This demonstration does not simulate gravity, wormholes, closed timelike curves, or travel to the past.

Equation: traveler proper time = Earth-frame time × √(1 − v²/c²). The slider stops below light speed. No fictional canon or real-world history is changed.
